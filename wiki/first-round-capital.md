# First Round Capital

Early-stage venture firm. Portfolio includes Uber, Notion, Square. Within the wiki, First Round's significance is the **PMF Method** program at pmf.firstround.com, led by partner [[todd-jackson|Todd Jackson]].

## PMF Method

A structured program distilled from studying hundreds of First Round portfolio companies. The key deliverables:

- **[[four-levels-of-pmf|Four Levels of PMF]]** with benchmarks at each level.
- **Three dimensions**: satisfaction, demand, efficiency.
- **[[four-ps-framework|Four Ps]]**: persona, problem, promise, product.
- **[[dollar-driven-discovery|Dollar-driven customer discovery]]**.
- Case studies: [[vanta|Vanta]], Lattice, Plaid, Ironclad, Looker, Verkada, Persona.
