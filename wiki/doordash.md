# DoorDash

Food/goods delivery marketplace. Founded 2013 by Tony Xu, Andy Fang, Stanley Tang. Went public 2020. Distinctive leadership culture featured across two B6 sources.

## Canonical culture traits

- **WeDash** — All employees, including execs, do deliveries 4x/year. [[source-keith-yandell|Keith Yandell]] does monthly. Acts as a hiring filter — "Not every software engineer wants to hop in their Tesla and deliver McDonald's."
- **"No politics / no asshole" culture** — Interview for it; willing to blackball top candidates on culture grounds. Scores #1 in DoorDash's exec survey year after year.
- **Generalists over specialists** — Tony Xu's thesis (citing Epstein's *Range*): specialists do things as always done, capping upside. Bring a non-expert with first-principles thinking; hire best-in-class SMEs beneath them.
- **Founder humility at scale** — Tony still responds to customer escalations in real time. Story: [[source-keith-yandell|Keith's]] 8-year-old daughter called Tony about a bad delivery route; Tony emailed the product org with a fix before they got home.
- **Pre-Series-D near-death experience as founding myth** — Scarcity pushed out mercenaries, forced unit-economics discipline. "Tough times make companies."

## Operating practices

- **T3/B3 feedback** — Three Tops, three Bottoms in reviews. Adopted from Travis Kalanick at Uber.
- **"How to work with [manager]" docs** — Keith's has three sections: expectations, own growth areas/blind spots, commitments (including "I will help you find your next job").
- **Steel-man + tiebreaker for decisions** — Ask each side to argue the opposing case; name a tiebreaker; set a time horizon.
