# Coda

All-in-one doc platform founded by Shishir Mehrotra (ex-YouTube). Coda combines documents, spreadsheets, and applications into a single surface. Mehrotra uses Coda as the testing ground for his frameworks on team rituals, eigenquestions, and growth loops.

## B4 appearances

- [[source-shishir-mehrotra|Shishir Mehrotra]] — Coda CEO. Introduces the eigenquestion framework (the single question whose answer determines all others), Black/Blue Loops for growth, rituals of great teams (Dory for anonymous Q&A, Pulse for weekly morale), and the PSHE talent evaluation model.
