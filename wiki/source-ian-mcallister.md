# What it takes to become a top 1% PM | Ian McAllister (Uber, Amazon, Airbnb)

**Guest**: Ian McAllister

## Executive summary

Ian McAllister — 12 years at [[amazon|Amazon]], then Airbnb, now Senior Director of Product for Vehicles at Uber — unpacks his legendary Quora post on what separates a top 1% PM from a top 10% PM. He also gives the most nuanced account in B4 of Amazon's working-backwards process: the PR/FAQ is a *mechanism* to enforce problem-first thinking, not an artifact to worship, and the spirit of it can live in any document format. Bezos's three investment tests and the Weekly Business Review as a cascading-accountability mechanism complete the operating picture.

## Key ideas

1. **Top 3 skills for new PMs — Communicate, Prioritize, Execute**: Prioritization is the biggest leverage point (5x impact differences at the same seniority). Execution is the drumbeat. Communication never stops mattering.
2. **Top 3 skills for senior PMs — Communicate, Think Big, Earn Trust**: Trust is the currency of a product leader. Think Big means asking "could this be bigger?" before scoping.
3. **Working backwards is about the problem, not the press release**: The PR/FAQ is a mechanism enforcing problem-first thinking. The most common failure is teams that retrofit problems onto existing solutions.
4. **Bezos's three investment tests**: (1) Is it a big idea? (2) Is it something we should be doing? (3) Is there a legitimate plan to succeed? All three must pass.
5. **The Operator's Manual**: Answer first, then explain. Avoid weasel words. Own your mistakes. Grade yourself after every communication. Self-reflection is the simplest continuous-improvement tool.
