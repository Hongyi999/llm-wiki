# Building better roadmaps | Janna Bastow

**Guest**: Janna Bastow
**Published**: 2022-10-16
**Duration**: 53:40
**YouTube**: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W3cvqPCGcck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W3cvqPCGcck)

## Executive summary

Janna Bastow — CEO of ProdPad, co-founder of Mind the Product (300k-member PM community), and popularizer of the Now/Next/Later roadmap — makes the case that traditional Gantt-chart roadmaps are organizational poison. They promise dates the team can't keep, collapse strategy into deliverables, and train stakeholders to demand features instead of outcomes. Her alternative: a roadmap that's a living strategy prototype, not a commitment register. The episode is the definitive case against timeline roadmaps and the clearest explanation of how to run the Now/Next/Later format without losing accountability.

## Key ideas

1. **Timeline roadmaps are lies we all agree to tell**: The moment dates go on a roadmap, it stops being a plan and starts being a promise. Promises made under uncertainty become broken promises. The fix is to communicate confidence levels, not dates.
2. **Now / Next / Later as strategy prototype**: Three buckets with decreasing specificity. "Now" is what the team is actively building (weeks). "Next" is the staged problems (months). "Later" is the strategic direction (quarters+). Each bucket is a theme/outcome, not a feature.
3. **Roadmap as conversation starter, not contract**: The roadmap's job is to align — with execs, sales, customers — on the *why* of the next work, not to lock the team in. When sales wants to sell a feature, the roadmap should help them sell the *outcome* the feature creates.
4. **Soft launch over hard launch**: Release to a sliver of users, learn, iterate, expand. Hard launches with PR blitzes are a capital-intensive way to discover problems. Soft launches are the default for uncertain bets.
5. **Outcome over output**: Every roadmap item should name the user problem and the success metric. If you can't name either, it's not ready to be on the roadmap — it's a solution in search of a problem.
