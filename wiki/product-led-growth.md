# Product-Led Growth (PLG)

A go-to-market motion in which the product itself drives acquisition, activation, and expansion — free product, self-serve signup, viral loops — often evolving into product-led sales (PLS) for enterprise expansion.

## Core definitional elements

- **Free tier or free trial** — Users experience value before paying.
- **Self-serve signup** — No sales touch required for initial value.
- **Activation-to-upgrade funnel** — Measure users reaching aha moment; convert via in-product cues.
- **Expansion via usage** — Seats, data volume, or feature access scale naturally.

## The key reframe: PLG = DLG

[[source-hila-qu|Hila Qu]]: "PLG is fundamentally data-led growth (DLG)." If you give away product without instrumentation/data foundation, "you are giving away free product for nothing."

## The PLG funnel (Airtable naming)

[[source-lauryn-isford|Lauryn Isford's]] **Join → Evaluate → Upgrade → Expand**. "Evaluate" (not "activate") centers the user's perspective.

## Aha moment / activation metric

- [[source-hila-qu|GitLab]]: 2 users × 2 features in 14 days.
- [[source-merci-grace|Slack]]: 3 humans, 50 messages.
- [[source-sri-batchu|Ramp]]: 4 events in first 30 days.
- [[source-naomi-gleit|Facebook]]: 7 friends in 10 days.

**[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford's rule]]**: 5-15% hit-rate activation is better than 40% because it correlates more strongly with retention.

## When PLG works

- Short time-to-value
- Low customization
- End-users are SMB / individuals / teams (not CISO/procurement-led)

## PLG → PLS evolution

[[source-ben-williams|Ben Williams / Snyk]]: Community-led growth + product-led acquisition gets you scale, but unlocking enterprise requires PLS (governance features + sales/marketing). Don't wait for monetization; enterprise buyers are often different personas than adopters.

## Commitment red flags

[[source-hila-qu|Hila]]: Companies fail at PLG when (a) they equate it with launching a free tier, (b) they don't dedicate a team, (c) they do it because it's trendy without product-fit assessment.
