# Stripe

Payments infrastructure company founded by Patrick and John Collison. Referenced across three B4 sources as a case study in scaling, operating systems for companies, and high-caliber PM culture. Claire Hughes Johnson (ex-COO) provides the deepest inside perspective.

## B4 appearances

- [[source-claire-hughes-johnson|Claire Hughes Johnson]] — Ex-COO of Stripe (and before that VP at Google). Describes building Stripe's company operating system: founding documents, self-awareness as leadership prerequisite, and how to scale from 200 to 7,000 employees without losing culture.
- [[source-shreyas-doshi|Shreyas Doshi]] — Ex-Stripe PM leader. Introduces the LNO framework (Leverage/Neutral/Overhead tasks) and opportunity-cost thinking developed during his time at Stripe.
- [[source-shreyas-doshi-live|Shreyas Doshi]] — References Stripe culture in the context of high-agency leadership and Patrick Collison's approach to product excellence.
