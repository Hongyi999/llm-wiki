# Word-of-Mouth Engineering

The practice of systematically engineering word-of-mouth acquisition (not leaving it to happenstance). Canonical treatment from [[source-nilan-peiris|Nilan Peiris]] (Wise CPO) and [[source-oji-udezue|Oji Udezue]] (Typeform CPO).

## The core claim

Word of mouth is downstream of **perceivable product quality**, not referral mechanics. Synthetic virality (refer-a-friend-get-$5) fails if the product isn't radically better; authentic word of mouth compounds if the product is.

## Wise's operationalization

- **70% word-of-mouth acquisition** on 16M+ customers.
- **Invites double per NPS bucket** — Overlaying referral data on NPS responses showed invite rates ~2x at each jump (6 → 7-8 → 9-10).
- **NPS as growth leverage metric**, not a happiness metric.

## The 10x rule (virality threshold)

[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]: "If you entered with 5.9% pricing vs 6%, customers would use us but not talk about us. We only got advocacy when we were 8 to 10 times cheaper."

[[source-oji-udezue|Udezue]]: "If you do something 20% better, people don't notice. For a product to make a difference, it has to be at least two or three more X."

## Sharp problems ([[source-oji-udezue|Udezue]])

Word of mouth flows naturally from solving **sharp problems** — problems that steal time/energy/money/focus. Diagnostic: draw pre- and post-customer workflows; measure compression. Qualitative tell: pupils dilating + spontaneous "when can I pay?"

## Anti-patterns

- **Synthetic virality** — Viral tags, referral hacks, share-to-unlock.
- **Split-testing your way to love** ([[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]) — A/B tests can't produce 10x improvement; they only optimize incremental.
- **Incremental improvements** — 10-20% better is invisible.

## What actually works

- **Work backwards from the theoretical ideal** — What's the minimum possible cost / time? Then fight for years to reach it.
- **Mission authenticity** — A rebrand email with no CTA produced more Wise signups than any campaign because immigrants recognized Wise genuinely cared.
- **Three-pillar focus** — Wise: price, speed, ease of use. Derived from NPS verbatims, not strategy offsites.
