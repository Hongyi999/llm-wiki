# Comparison — B4 Product Craft & Strategy Approaches (Older)

B4 covers 29 Lenny's Podcast transcripts on product strategy and PM craft (older episodes, 2022-2023). Where B3 featured canonical frameworks (7 Powers, Playing to Win, Shape Up), B4 provides the practitioner layer — PMs and leaders sharing hard-won operating wisdom from inside companies. The 29 guests group into 7 schools of thought.

## 7 schools of thought

### 1. PM frameworks & mental models (4 guests)

The most framework-dense cluster in any batch so far.

|---|---|---|

## Key disagreements

| Question | Position A | Position B |
|---|---|---|
| **Frameworks: liberating or limiting?** | Shreyas, Ravi, Shishir: frameworks create shared vocabulary and accelerate decisions | Cutler: "best practice" advice is context-dependent; no framework universally applies |
| **Process: essential or diseased?** | Hughes Johnson: a company operating system is essential for scaling | Cagan: "the disease of process" kills empowered teams; Nikita Bier: process is death for consumer social |
| **Should PMs specialize or generalize?** | Mehta: 12 competencies × 4 levels → specialize by stage | Crowley: the best PMs just simplify, follow up, and carry the water — domain-agnostic |
| **Customer research cadence?** | Torres: weekly touchpoints, no exceptions | McAllister: Working Backwards documents replace most ongoing research with upfront rigor |
| **Growth: missionary or latent demand?** | Bier: only build into latent demand; missionary growth is a death trap | Egner, Pearl: you can create demand through champion-first adoption and PLG |
