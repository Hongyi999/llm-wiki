# Product-Led Sales (PLS)

A hybrid GTM motion where **product owns pipeline creation and monetization targets**, not just feature-building, while sales closes enterprise-grade deals sourced from product-qualified accounts. Distinct from both pure [[product-led-growth|PLG]] (self-serve only) and traditional sales-led growth (marketing → pipeline → sales).

## Structural change

In traditional sales, marketing creates pipeline and sales closes. In PLS, **product acquires, activates, and creates pipeline for sales**. Product must take on accountability over pipeline targets and monetization KPIs.

## Core mechanics

1. **Monetization awareness** (75% of conversion battle) — Feature walls, usage limits, visual cues that make free users aware of paid value.
2. **PQA (Product Qualified Account)** — Product-defined signal that an account is ready for sales engagement. Product owns and iterates the definition.
3. **User profiling at signup** — 3–4 questions (company size, department, seniority, use case) to enable proper sales routing.
4. **12-month enterprise escalation** — Individual usage takes 12+ months to become an enterprise contract.

## Benchmarks

- 5–10% freemium-to-paid conversion rate (Elena Verna)
- 12+ months from individual user to enterprise contract (Netlify, Miro, Amplitude)

## Common pitfalls

- Treating every free user as a sales opportunity (flooding sales with low-intent leads).
- Sales disrupting the user journey instead of adding value.
- Leaving marketing out (need ABM to find the buyer within the account).
- Ignoring data infrastructure too long.
