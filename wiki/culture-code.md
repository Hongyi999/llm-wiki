# Culture Code (HubSpot)

The **Culture Code** is HubSpot's living 128-page document authored by co-founder/CTO Dharmesh Shah. It is the canonical example of treating culture as a product spec — versioned, debated, and continuously updated — rather than a one-time memo or poster.

## Key properties

- **Versioned like software** — Releases, change logs, stakeholders.
- **Authored by "the worst possible person for the job"** — Dharmesh explicitly chose to write it because he didn't fit the stereotypical culture role; that gave him the outsider's eye to spec what culture *should* produce rather than what it already was.
- **Public** — HubSpot publishes the Culture Code openly; candidates and customers read it.
- **Operational** — Transparency defaults ("all information equally shared" except illegal-to-share or other people's salaries) are concrete, not aspirational.

## Why culture-as-product works

Traditional culture artifacts (values posters, mission statements) suffer from **entropy** (Dharmesh's frame: second law of thermodynamics). Without maintenance, they decay into decoration. Versioning forces active upkeep, which forces conversations about whether the stated values match reality.
