# Scale AI

Data-labeling company founded by Alexandr Wang in 2016 that became the first billion-dollar AI data vendor. In B1 Scale is the reference point for "the old data-labeling market" against which the new expert-labeler generation ([[mercor|Mercor]], [[source-garrett-lord|Handshake]]) is positioned.

## B1 appearances

- [[source-jason-droege]] — **Jason Droege ran GTM at Scale AI** before joining Handshake; the episode recounts how Scale evolved from commodity bounding-box labeling to expert-judgment tasks (80%+ bachelor's degree labelers) as frontier labs outgrew simple data.
- [[source-brendan-foody]] — [[mercor|Mercor]]'s founder positions his company as "post-Scale": Scale was built for the image-classification era, while Mercor supplies the PhD-level evaluators needed for [[ai-evals|post-training evals]]. Mercor grew from $1M → $500M ARR in 17 months.
- [[source-garrett-lord]] — **Handshake** pivoted from student careers to competing directly with Scale for the [[post-training-data-moat|frontier-lab data contract]], leveraging its 18M-student network as an expert-labeler moat.

## B1 framing

Scale AI is discussed across three sources as the **old paradigm** in the data-labeling market: legacy infrastructure built for supervised learning and computer vision, now getting disrupted by companies that supply **expert human judgment for post-training**. The consistent thesis: whoever best matches *frontier-lab demand for PhD-level labelers to their supply of expert humans* wins the next $10B in labeling spend.
