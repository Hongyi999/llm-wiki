# JJELLYFISH

Firm co-founded by [[jen-abel|Jen Abel]] that embeds alongside seed-stage founders to teach and drive [[founder-led-sales|founder-led sales]], typically through the first $1M of ARR.

## Operating model

- Embed with the founding team; do not outsource the sales function.
- Founder remains **"the tip of the spear"** — engaging directly with the market, learning first-hand.
- Focus: enterprise and upper mid-market (500+ employees) where the sales cycle is a craft rather than a volume game.

## Philosophy

- Sales in the earliest stage is **research, not revenue**.
- Don't hire professional salespeople until Series A — seed is about experimentation.
- The founder cannot be replaced by a sales hire until the motion is repeatable by a stranger.
