# Todd Jackson

Partner at [[first-round-capital|First Round Capital]] and author of the First Round PMF Method (pmf.firstround.com). Previously a PM at Facebook, Twitter, and Gmail. Todd's framework is the most structured public treatment of [[product-market-fit|PMF]] as a multi-level, multi-dimensional concept.

## Key contributions

- **[[four-levels-of-pmf|Four Levels of PMF]]**: Nascent → Developing → Strong → Extreme. Makes PMF a continuum, not a binary.
- **Three dimensions of PMF**: satisfaction, demand, efficiency. A product can be strong on one and fake PMF on the others.
- **[[four-ps-framework|Four Ps]]**: Persona, Problem, Promise, Product — the required clarity for a PMF claim.
- **[[dollar-driven-discovery|Dollar-driven customer discovery]]**: trust dollars, not words.
- Popularized Brett Berson's **"$100 vending machine"** metaphor.
- Popularized the **marginal customer** lens — look at your last 10 customers, not your first 10.

## Key heuristics

- **60–70% of startups get stuck at L1 or L2** because they conflate one strong signal with PMF.
- **4–6 year timeline** to reach Extreme PMF even for successful companies — patience is the differentiator.
- **Don't pivot on 10% signal; wait for 200% signal** (via Jack Altman at Lattice).
- **Friend zone test** (via Rick Song at Persona): "cool, keep me posted" = not PMF.
- **Wow statements**: listen for spontaneous superlatives.
