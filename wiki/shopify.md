# Shopify

**Shopify** (~$130B market cap, powers 10%+ of US e-commerce) is a Canadian e-commerce platform founded by Tobi Lütke in 2004 as a snowboard store before pivoting to the platform itself. Its operating model is an unusually explicit implementation of first-principles thinking, anti-OKR culture, and a "hard path" intensity that many scaling companies import.

## Operating principles

- **[[first-principles-re-derivation|First-principles re-derivation (Tobi tornado)]]** — When facts change, compress time: kill the project, re-run the decision tree from atomic building blocks. Sunk cost is the enemy.
- **Anti-OKR / Goodhart-aware** — Data-informed, not metrics-driven; taste, delight, pride of craft are ~80% of value space.
- **Infinite games + positional play** — 100-year vision; iterated-prisoner's-dilemma partnerships (e.g., Stripe) that cooperate even at short-term margin cost.
- **Exothermic leadership** — Leaders inject energy; great products reflect how much the team gives a shit.
- **Human potential throughline** — Every person performs far below their ceiling; the leader's job is to remind and hold them accountable.

## Signature practices

- **Trust battery** — Trust starts at 50% and charges/drains based on delivery; used for credibility across the org.
- **Meetingageddon** — Periodic zero-based purge of all recurring meetings; rebuild from scratch.
- **GSD ("get shit done") prize** — Standing award that rewards shipping over coordinating.
- **Pair/mob programming as default** — Solo coding treated as a bug; pairing is how onboarding, mentorship, and rotation happen.
- **Job trials over interviews** — Paid work-sample hiring.
- **6-week performance cycles** — Annual reviews too slow to change behavior.
- **Delete Code Club** — Celebrate removal, not lines written.
