# Source: How to scrappily hire for, measure, and unlock growth | Crystal Widjaja (Gojek, Kumu)

**Guest**: Crystal Widjaja (built and led growth at Gojek through its hyper-scaling years; now CPO at Kumu)
**Published**: 2022-07-31
**Duration**: ~1h 3m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYaiyi2ZX6Q

## Executive summary

Widjaja argues the best growth data comes from shipping real experiences fast, not pondering dashboards. Scrappy Wizard-of-Oz experiments, ruthlessly specific instrumentation, and grounding growth strategy in the "physics" of your existing model beat framework-worship every time.

## Key ideas

1. **Do-things-that-don't-scale at extreme scale** — Gojek rented football stadiums to onboard 60,000 drivers in weeks, added drivers to WhatsApp groups to hand-sell GoPay subscriptions, ran Typeform "personality quiz" features before building real ones. "Every idea is so cheap at that scale."
2. **Run experiments at n=30** — "What's better than having 30 data points? Certainly having 100. But what's better than having zero is definitely 30."
3. **Growth physics and the lever you're under-using** — Start from current market/product/model/channels, find the under-leveraged lever. At Gojek, drivers were used as human salespeople for GoPay top-ups; that one move drove 60% of GoPay acquisition.
4. **Retention benchmarks as decacorn bar** — Free products should hit ~60% W1 retention and flatten there; paid products 20-30%. Early-stage friends-and-family closer to 80% or "the product probably isn't going to solve the job for anyone else."
5. **Most analytics fails at instrumentation** — Teams track events with zero properties, producing observations not insights. Fix: track rich contextual properties (drivers on screen, surge, voucher) so you can answer "why" not just "what."

## Concepts that deserve their own wiki page

- **Growth physics / constraints-first modeling** — Four-part (market, product, model, channels) grounding before picking experiments.
- **Wizard-of-Oz growth experiments** — Validating features via WhatsApp, Typeform, screenshots before engineering.
- **Instrumentation taxonomy for insights** — Event + rich properties, not event-only.
- **Retention benchmarks** — 60% free / 20-30% paid, flat after week 1.
- **Super app dynamics in emerging markets** — Conglomerate trust + phone-storage constraints.

## Notable quote

> "Please don't track your KPIs. Please track your user journeys and experiences."

## B6 angle (hiring, management, leadership Part 2)

Widjaja is a rare Southeast Asia growth voice and a sharp counterpoint to the Silicon Valley playbook: her hiring advice ("I'd hire someone willing to take an intro stats course" over flashy experience) and case-study interview process (5 days, 4 expected hours, watch how they Google) are distinctively concrete compared to B5's American coaching-heavy frames. Her growth-team hiring thesis — hire for statistical rigor and opportunity-cost thinking, not charisma — pairs interestingly with [[source-ben-horowitz|Horowitz]]/[[source-camille-fournier|Fournier]] on functional leadership and fills a gap none of them covers: staffing a growth team in a capital-constrained environment.
