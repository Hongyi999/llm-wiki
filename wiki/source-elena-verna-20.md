# Source: The ultimate guide to product-led sales | Elena Verna

**Guest**: Elena Verna (ex-SVP Growth SurveyMonkey; interim CMO Miro; interim Head of Growth Amplitude; Reforge instructor)
**Published**: 2023-04-23
**Duration**: ~1h 16m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bxghtN-OlJQ

## Executive summary

Defines product-led sales (PLS) as a distinct paradigm from both pure PLG and traditional sales-led growth. Core structural change: product must own accountability for pipeline creation and monetization targets, not just feature-building. Most freemium conversion failures stem from monetization awareness (users don't know what they're buying), not pricing or product gaps. Practical benchmarks: 5–10% freemium conversion rate, 12+ months from individual user to enterprise contract.

## Key ideas

1. **PLS requires a structural collaboration change** — In traditional sales, marketing creates pipeline and sales closes. In PLS, product acquires, activates, and creates pipeline for sales. Product must own monetization accountability.
2. **Monetization awareness is 75% of the battle** — Most freemium users don't know what they're buying. Feature walls, usage limits, and consistent visual cues (like Amazon's gold color for premium) drive conversion far more than pricing changes.
3. **Free-to-paid conversion levers in order** — (1) Monetization awareness (feature/usage walls), (2) conversion rate optimization (pricing/checkout friction), (3) changing what you sell (product/pricing model). Fix #1 before restructuring your model.
4. **PQA is product's KPI, not sales' definition** — Product Qualified Account definition must be owned and iterated by product, not imposed by sales. Product should own a PQA conversion rate target.
5. **Profile users at signup** — Ask 3–4 profiling questions (company size, department, seniority, use case). People who drop off were low-intent anyway. You need to know who you're talking to so sales reaches the right segment.
6. **12 months to enterprise contract** — At Netlify, Miro, Amplitude, individual usage takes 12+ months to escalate into an enterprise deal. Don't expect fast top-down closes from free users.
