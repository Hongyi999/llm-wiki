# Block

Payments and financial services company (formerly Square), parent of Cash App. Jack Dorsey is CEO; [[source-dhanji-r-prasanna|Dhanji R. Prasanna]] is CTO. In B1 Block is the case study for **how a large, mature enterprise rapidly becomes [[ai-native-organization|AI-native]]**.

## B1 role

[[source-dhanji-r-prasanna]] is the definitive episode. Dhanji led Block's AI transformation through a combination of:

- **An internal manifesto** arguing Block must centralize its technology approach and think like a tech company again.
- **Org restructure from divisional GMs → functional org** (all engineers report to engineering lead). [[ai-native-organization|Conway's Law]] in action.
- **Goose** — Block's internal, open-source AI agent that saves engineers **8–10 hours per week** and is already producing pull requests based on Slack discussions.
- **Reinstating hack weeks** and top-IC gatherings as culture signals.

## Key themes

- **Non-technical employees see the largest AI productivity gains**, not engineers.
- **Code quality has nothing to do with product success.**
- **Centralized AI strategy is table-stakes** — grassroots adoption alone doesn't move a 3,500-engineer org.
- **Org structure determines AI-readiness** — divisional GMs blocked unified AI rollout until the restructure.
