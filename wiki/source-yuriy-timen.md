# Source: How to grow a subscription business | Yuriy Timen

**Guest**: Yuriy Timen (ex-Grammarly 8.5-year Head of Growth & Marketing; advisor to Canva, Airtable, Hims, Flo, Whimsical, Monarch)
**Published**: 2022-09-01
**Duration**: ~1h 8m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-iN7sweFBM

## Executive summary

Most tactical subscription-growth conversation on the feed: three archetypes of subscription growth engines (paid, virality/referral, SEO), benchmark conversion rates for prosumer SaaS, and an argument that current macro turbulence is making later-stage growth strategies trickle into Series A playbooks. Core thesis: focus on one engine with guardrails and rapid iteration.

## Key ideas

1. **Three subscription growth archetypes** — (a) Paid, when LTV is strong and free-to-paid >5% (Grammarly, Canva prosumer LTVs in the hundreds vs. typical consumer-sub $50-60); (b) Virality/referrals, when inherent product network effects exist (Laika dog food); (c) SEO, especially with long-tail programmatic angle (Canva templates).
2. **Prosumer SaaS conversion benchmarks** — Visitor → free signup: 20-35% healthy, asymptotes at ~25-30%; free → paid: below 5% won't sustain, 7%+ is the target.
3. **Focus with guardrails, not diversification theater** — Early-stage companies over-diversify too soon; later-stage become dangerously single-channel dependent. Coach early founders to resist diversification until main engine scales.
4. **Attribution/measurement comeback** — iOS 14 + budget tightening means click-based attribution is dying; Media Mix Modeling (Recast) + incrementality tools (Measured, Incremental) are resurging. Click attribution was never causal anyway.
5. **SEO, paid, and attribution mandates shift earlier** — In 2020-2021 "grow at any cost" regime, SEO was "for grown-ups"; now with 18-24 month runway mandates, Series A companies are investing in SEO and attribution that used to be Series B+ concerns.
6. **Giving channels a real shot** — "YouTube, we tried it, doesn't work" is almost always a case of not having been designed to work. Channel-test guardrails: minimum impressions, 2-3 creative angles, CTR benchmarks.
7. **TikTok under-saturated for non-obvious demos** — Even 40+ married, $200K+ HHI audiences are on TikTok in numbers advertisers don't expect.
