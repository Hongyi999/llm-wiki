# Now / Next / Later roadmap

**Now / Next / Later** is the alternative to timeline/Gantt roadmaps, popularized by [[source-janna-bastow|Janna Bastow]] (ProdPad, Mind the Product). It organizes a product roadmap into three buckets of decreasing specificity instead of by calendar date.

## The three buckets

### Now
- **Horizon**: What the team is actively building (weeks).
- **Specificity**: High. Specific outcomes, identified opportunities, sized bets.
- **Commitment**: Near-term delivery, but still outcome-framed, not feature-locked.

### Next
- **Horizon**: Staged problems queued up after Now (months).
- **Specificity**: Medium. Named opportunities and rough solution directions.
- **Commitment**: Direction, not dates. Subject to learnings from Now.

### Later
- **Horizon**: Strategic direction (quarters+).
- **Specificity**: Low. Themes, bets, explorations.
- **Commitment**: Aspirational. Intentionally vague.

## Why timeline roadmaps are poison

Janna's core argument: the moment dates go on a roadmap, it stops being a plan and starts being a promise. Promises made under uncertainty become broken promises. The team loses trust; stakeholders lose patience.

Now/Next/Later sidesteps the problem by communicating **confidence intervals** instead of dates. Everyone understands that "Later" is high-uncertainty and "Now" is high-confidence — without pretending to know what a calendar says.

## Outcome-framed, not feature-framed

Each item on the roadmap should name:
- The user problem / opportunity (not the feature name).
- The success metric (outcome, not output).

If you can't name either, the item isn't ready for the roadmap — it's a solution in search of a problem.

## Roadmap as conversation starter

Janna reframes the roadmap as a **conversation tool**, not a contract. Its job is to align execs, sales, customers, and the team on the *why* of the next work. When sales wants to sell a feature, the roadmap helps them sell the *outcome* the feature creates.

## Fitting into the operating system

Now/Next/Later sits alongside two other modern PM artifacts:

- [[opportunity-solution-tree]] — The discovery artifact. Opportunities and validated solutions flow from the OST into Now/Next/Later.
- [[product-strategy-stack|Product Strategy Stack]] — The strategy layer above the roadmap. Themes in Later should trace to pillars in the strategy.

## Common failure modes

- **Feature lists inside the buckets** — Defeats the purpose. Items must be outcome-framed.
- **Dates sneaking back in** — Pressure to commit to Q3 launches converts the format back into Gantt.
- **Too much in Now** — A crowded Now is a signal the team is multitasking, not prioritizing.
- **Empty Later** — An empty Later means no strategy. Later should always have named themes.
