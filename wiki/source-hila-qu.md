# Source: The ultimate guide to adding a PLG motion | Hila Qu (Reforge, GitLab)

**Guest**: Hila Qu (Reforge EIR; ex-GitLab; ex-Acorns)
**Published**: 2023-04-02
**Duration**: ~1h 33m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7l1fIxk7SnA

## Executive summary

Comprehensive playbook for adding a product-led growth motion to a traditionally sales-led B2B business. Core thesis: PLG is fundamentally "DLG" (data-led growth) — giving away a free product is wasted effort unless you have the data foundation to understand usage behavior, identify aha moments, and iterate.

## Key ideas

1. **"PLG is fundamentally DLG"** — Free product gets you two things: broader reach AND usage behavior data. If you lack the data foundation, "you are giving away free product for nothing." Data instrumentation must precede tool selection.
2. **The full-funnel PLG audit** — Starting diagnostic: pretend to be a customer, walk from landing page to signup to aha moment to self-checkout, then layer quantitative funnel data on top. Low-hanging fruit (broken checkout forms, missing localization, no onboarding) surfaces fast.
3. **GitLab's aha moment = 2 users × 2 features in 14 days** — Derived via correlation analysis across 10 high-value actions against both conversion AND retention.
4. **Four PLG starting points, ranked** — Acquisition, activation, conversion, retention/expansion. Activation is usually best for most B2B SaaS; retention is "messy middle" and should not be first.
5. **Commitment red flags** — Companies fail when (a) they equate PLG with launching a free tier, (b) don't dedicate a team, or (c) do it because it's trendy without product-fit assessment (short time-to-value, low customization, SMB end-users).
6. **Org evolution: PLG team → PLG org** — Start with head of growth (growth PM) + analyst + engineer + designer. Eventually requires head of growth product + head of growth marketing + head of product-led sales.

## B7 angle

Most comprehensive, practitioner-level PLG playbook in the B7 set. Converges with [[source-elena-verna|Elena]] on "every company will need both PLG and sales" but adds operational detail (audit, data dictionary, metric-mapping). GitLab aha-moment story (2 users × 2 features × 14 days, derived via correlation) is concrete counterpoint to [[source-sean-ellis-growth-hacker|Sean Ellis's]] 40% "very disappointed" — two different empirical methods for the same activation question.
