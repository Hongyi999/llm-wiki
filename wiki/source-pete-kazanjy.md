# Source: Founder-led sales | Pete Kazanjy (Founding Sales, Atrium)

**Guest**: Pete Kazanjy (author of *Founding Sales*; founder of Modern Sales Pros, 30K+ member community; CEO of Atrium)
**Published**: 2022-12-15
**Duration**: ~1h 02m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cZd5234Eem0

## Executive summary

Founders must personally sell their first 20–30 customers to keep the feedback loop tight and learn positioning. Outsourcing sales too early breaks the PMF signal. Modern sales is a learnable, measurable discipline — not an innate talent. Specific threshold for hiring your first salesperson: 15–25% win rate on first meetings after 50–100 at-bats. Hire pioneer sellers (battle-tested ICs from successful sales orgs), not VPs expecting polished playbooks.

## Key ideas

1. **Founder-led sales is non-negotiable** — Founders must sell the first 20–30 customers. Outsourcing early causes them to lose critical product-market fit learnings. The feedback loop between "what customers object to" and "what you build" is the entire point.
2. **The "while loop" concept** — Treat your sales motion like software: run it locally (founder-led), debug it, then replicate to additional sellers (cloud deployment). Only scale after proving consistent, repeatable results.
3. **15–25% win rate threshold** — Hire your first salesperson after achieving 15–25% conversion on first meetings across 50–100 at-bats. This proves repeatability and gives you defensible data to train others.
4. **Hire pioneer sellers, not VPs** — First sales hires should be battle-tested ICs who thrive in ambiguity, not seasoned leaders expecting mature infrastructure. The wrong VP hire at this stage is a 6-month setback.
5. **Leading indicators over lagging** — Monitor second-date rates, proposal progression, and activity levels (not just closed deals). Identify underperformance within 1–2 months rather than waiting until quarter-end.
6. **Sales positioning as product roadmap** — Each objection or failed conversation is a data point. Update pitch deck, discovery questions, and demo script iteratively. Your sales motion evolves the same way your product does.
