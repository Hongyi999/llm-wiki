# Source: Building a long and meaningful career | Nikhyl Singhal (Meta, Google)

**Guest**: Nikhyl Singhal (VP Product at Meta; ex-Google, ex-Credit Karma; author of *The Skip*)
**Published**: 2023-06-11
**Duration**: ~1h 25m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_WQuUIYnJg

## Executive summary

PMs sabotage their careers with short-term thinking. "Think two jobs ahead," avoid "ex-growth" companies whose valuations exceed their PMF, treat career like a product with versions, and master one of several product ambiguities (domain, market, org, craft, team) rather than collecting logos.

## Key ideas

1. **Think two jobs ahead (the Skip)** — Don't optimize the next move; reason backwards from the career you want, treating career like a product roadmap.
2. **Avoid ex-growth companies** — Well-capitalized unicorns still searching for PMF at unicorn valuations are career traps; employees have no recourse (equity is the risk) while founders can recap.
3. **Four reasons you're not getting promoted** — (1) no advocate sees your magic, (2) the next role doesn't exist, (3) you're impatient, (4) you have a real development area you refuse to hear. About half of stuck cases are #4.
4. **Pick an ambiguity lane early** — Become world-class at one of: craft, market ambiguity, organizational ambiguity, domain, team management, or growth. Early-career PMs need a story to tell, not just logos.
5. **Management as "sidecar," not divide-and-conquer or training-wheels** — Share the steering wheel; you must be "invited in" as a manager — organizational authority ≠ earned authority.
