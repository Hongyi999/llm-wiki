# Source: Succeeding as an introvert, building zero-to-one, and PM'ing your career like a product | Deb Liu

**Guest**: Deb Liu (CEO of Ancestry; former VP Product at Facebook 11 years — Marketplace/Facebook Pay/first mobile DR ad product; board member Intuit)
**Published**: 2024-08-22
**Duration**: ~1h 11m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MajB5CQUKDA

## Executive summary

Career-design wisdom for product leaders. Core theses: treat your career like a product (write its spec, set metrics, work backwards); resilience and reframing failure beat a charmed path; introverts must do their own "product marketing of themselves"; zero-to-one inside big companies is a portfolio game with a ~50% hit rate and requires dodging the limelight.

## Key ideas

1. **PM your career like a product** — "Some of the best PMs I have ever worked with are terrible PMs for their career." If you'd never ship without a spec, metrics, or milestones, why run your career that way? Set a 5-year destination.
2. **Growth is a game of inches** — Product-led growth at Ancestry, Marketplace, FB ads works via 100-hypothesis lists, 1% weekly wins, accepting 80% of bets fail. Prefer 20-ship/20%-success over 4-ship/50%-success: same output, more learning.
3. **Zero-to-one inside a big company needs the shadows** — Resources scarce, attention high. "You can love something to death" — over-scrutiny kills innovation. Deb ran payments as a second job while incubating the first direct-response mobile ad product (eventually majority of FB ad revenue).
4. **Introvert's tax + "It's not self-promotion, it's education"** — The workplace favors speakers. Reframing self-promotion as "educating your manager about the team's work" unlocks action. [[source-boz|Boz]] forced Deb into a monthly writing habit as a management contract — external accountability as the unlock.
5. **Resilience as differentiator** — "Life is 10% what happens and 90% how you react." Mark Zuckerberg once told Deb "not only will I not give you that job, you'll never have that job at this company" — she turned the job she had into what she wanted. 30/60/90 plan: 30 days listening (60+ interviews at Ancestry), 30 days aligning, 30 days executing.

## B7 angle

Growth as wrapping-optimization around a working core product — closer to [[source-naomi-gleit|Gleit's]] micro-barrier removal than [[source-elena-verna|Verna's]] earned-channel thesis. Unique contribution: the zero-to-one case study — FB's first mobile DR ad product built in the shadows, iterated through 5-6 versions on the verge of death, eventually became majority of FB ad revenue. A counter-example to "you can't bolt growth onto a slowing business."
