# Source: He saved OpenAI, invented the "Like" button, and built Google Maps: Bret Taylor (Sierra)

**Guest**: Bret Taylor

**Duration**: 1:28:58  
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qImgGtnNbx0  

## Executive summary

Bret Taylor argues the entire AI market will shift toward agents and outcomes-based pricing because it's the only way that truly aligns vendor incentives with customer outcomes. He explains how Sierra charges based on call resolutions instead of seats, and details how the future of software requires different thinking: from systems that merely improve productivity to systems that autonomously accomplish measurable business results. This is a paradigm shift as significant as the cloud.

## Key ideas

1. The whole market is going to go towards agents and outcomes-based pricing because it's just so obviously the correct way to build and sell software—aligning vendor incentives with customer success.
2. Outcomes-based pricing works when you can attribute the impact autonomously: Sierra charges per resolved customer service call, not per seat, aligning incentives perfectly.
3. AI agents represent a step change in software business models, comparable to the cloud transition—from pay-for-access to pay-for-work-delivered.
4. Attribution and autonomy are the prerequisites for outcome-based pricing in AI; without both, you'll default to usage-based pricing.
5. Study computer science and systems thinking, not just coding syntax—AI will handle the tedious code generation, but you'll need to understand complex systems to operate code-generating machines effectively.

## Concepts & frameworks

- [[outcomes-based-pricing|Outcomes-Based Pricing]] — Charging for autonomous work delivered (e.g., call resolution) rather than access or usage; requires both attribution and autonomy.
- **Call Deflection / Containment** — When an AI agent resolves a customer problem without human intervention, saving the cost of a call center rep (~$15 per call).
- [[ai-agents|Agents as the New App]] — The product form factor will shift from applications to autonomous agents that accomplish specific business outcomes.
- **Systems Thinking Over Syntax** — As AI handles code generation, the limiting skill becomes designing systems that work with unpredictable inputs and produce robust outcomes.
- **Single Issue Voter Bias** — Founders naturally default to solutions matching their strengths; a founder engineer thinks engineering fixes everything, when the real problem may be distribution or product-market fit.
