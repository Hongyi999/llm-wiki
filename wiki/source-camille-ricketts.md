# Source: How Notion leveraged community to build a $10B business ft. Camille Ricketts

**Guest**: Camille Ricketts (First marketing hire & Head of Marketing, Notion)
**Published**: 2022-12-11
**Duration**: ~1h 8m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bY5KC9Gguz8

## Executive summary

Camille Ricketts, employee #11 at Notion, explains how community-led growth powered Notion from a small startup to a $10B business. The strategy centered on identifying passionate users, supporting them as ambassadors and consultants (some building million-dollar businesses around Notion), and fueling organic discovery rather than forcing transactional relationships. She also shares frameworks for when community-led growth is (and isn't) right for a company.

## Key ideas

1. **Community-led growth = discovery, not just awareness** -- True community-led growth goes beyond brand awareness to create *intent to learn more*. Notion's north star metric was net new visitors to the website month-over-month -- people motivated enough by word-of-mouth to actually come explore the product.

2. **The atomic unit of sharing** -- Community works best when your product creates something users want to share because it says something about them. Notion templates, Figma designs, and Canva creations are aspirational to show off. If your product has this quality, community is a no-brainer investment.

3. **Ambassador program design** -- Start with ~20 of your most vocal organic advocates (not paid, not transactional). Give them early feature access, direct conversations with founders, and event subsidies. Grow slowly (~20 new members/month) to preserve the intimate, familial quality. Notion's ambassadors started businesses, built template marketplaces, and ran 250,000-member Facebook groups.

4. **Community 2x2 matrix** -- Pre-PMF + Enterprise: customer advisory boards. Post-PMF + Enterprise: champions and consultants communities. Post-PMF + Consumer: ambassadors and influencers. Pre-PMF + Consumer: small feedback groups. Different contexts require different community forms.

5. **Don't measure community ROI too early** -- Notion didn't concretely measure community until years after launch. Premature ROI pressure kills the organic fervor that makes community valuable. Ivan Zhao's conviction that community mattered was essential -- founder buy-in is a prerequisite.

6. **Content market fit** -- Just as you seek product-market fit, you need content-market fit. Ask: Who is my audience? What do they need to get promoted, avoid failure, or reduce anxiety? Then create content that addresses those needs.

7. **Influencer marketing as a measurable channel** -- Notion's influencer program (led by Lexi Barnhorn) was one of their most measurable awareness channels, with tight attribution from sponsored creator content to website visits.

## Concepts that deserve their own wiki page

- **[[Community-led growth]]** -- The strategy of leveraging passionate user communities to drive product discovery, enterprise adoption, and organic ubiquity.
- **[[Atomic unit of sharing]]** -- The concept that community thrives when a product creates shareable artifacts that let users express identity or competence.
- **[[Ambassador program]]** -- A structured program that supports a product's most vocal organic advocates with early access, events, and promotion of their work.

## Notable quote

> "The way that you think about product market fit, you have to think about content market fit. What is it that they need to get promoted? What is it that they need to avoid failure? What causes them anxiety? Can you create content that addresses this?"
