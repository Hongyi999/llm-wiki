# The ultimate guide to PR | Emilie Gerber (founder of Six Eastern)

**Guest**: Emilie Gerber
**Published**: 2024-03-21
**Duration**: 1:33:55
**YouTube**: [Lenny's Podcast — Emilie Gerber](https://www.lennyspodcast.com/)

## Executive summary

Emilie Gerber — founder/CEO of Six Eastern (PR agency for 100+ tech companies), previously PR lead at Uber (BD/B2B) and Box (product comms) — argues that press is rarely a direct growth lever for B2B but is a powerful second-order asset for hiring, sales, and credibility: the logo on your site, the article recruiters link to, the validation that unlocks a deal. She breaks down with unusual tactical specificity which publications to target for which stories, how to pitch reporters cold in three sentences, and why warm intros are overrated.

## Key ideas

1. **Press is recruiting and sales collateral, not growth**: For B2B, the main ROI of press is downstream — logos on the site, links in outbound and AE emails, third-party validation that helps recruiters close candidates and buyers overcome startup-risk skepticism.
2. **Pattern-match, don't category-create**: Pitches positioning the company as "taking on [incumbent]" land far better than "we're creating a new category." Giving reporters an immediate frame of reference (Ramp vs. Bill.com) unlocked coverage product news couldn't.
3. **Cold outreach beats the Rolodex**: Relationships only get you an opened email; they don't get coverage. Three-sentence pitches, an exclusive offer, a clear subject line, a controversial or contrarian angle outperform long essays.
4. **Match spokesperson to audience**: If you sell to CFOs, pitch your CFO — not your CEO — to CFO podcasts. A hiring argument in disguise: functional leaders in the roles you sell into make comms and credibility dramatically easier.
5. **Publication-to-story fit is tactical**: TechCrunch for funding, Axios for deals, Business Insider for pitch decks and lists, Fast Company for future-of-work/op-eds, Forbes for awards, VentureBeat for AI. NYT/WSJ features require revenue and valuation disclosure.

## B5 angle

For hiring and leadership, press is a retention and recruiting weapon more than a demand-gen channel — leaders should invest in founder-journey and culture stories that make candidates want to join, and should hire persona-matched executives (CFO, CMO) who can carry comms in the verticals they sell into.
