# Comparison — B4 Product Strategy & PM Craft Approaches (Part 2)

B4 covers 29 Lenny's Podcast transcripts from the older strategy/craft catalog (2022–2023). Where B3 leaned on canonical strategy theorists and operating-model reformers, B4 is denser on **career mechanics, coaching, roadmap/discovery artifacts, and operating-rhythm rituals**. The 29 guests group into seven schools of thought.

## 7 schools of thought

### 1. Career mechanics & altitude (6 guests)

The strongest single theme in B4: how PMs grow through role transitions that require skill resets, not skill extensions.

| Guest | Frame | Core insight |
|---|---|---|
| [[source-fareed-mosavat\|Fareed Mosavat]] | [[product-leader-canyon\|Product Leader Canyon]] | Skills that got you to senior PM aren't the job at VP/CPO; sponsorship ≠ mentorship |
| [[source-ian-mcallister\|Ian McAllister]] | Top 1% PM | Communicate/Prioritize/Execute (new) → Communicate/Think Big/Earn Trust (senior) |
| [[source-shreyas-doshi\|Shreyas Doshi]] | 5 big ideas | [[lno-framework\|LNO framework]]; operate one level above your title |
| [[source-shreyas-doshi-live\|Shreyas Doshi — live]] | Reflective questions | Questions he wishes he'd asked himself sooner; taste as meta-skill |
| [[source-jason-shah\|Jason Shah]] | Ladder vs. map careers | Taste is trainable; writing is compressed thinking |
| [[source-lauren-ipsen\|Lauren Ipsen]] | Executive recruiter | Three product-leader archetypes; back-channel references reveal truth |

## Key disagreements

| Question | Position A | Position B |
|---|---|---|
| **What matters most in the PM career ladder?** | [[source-ian-mcallister\|McAllister]]: master Communicate/Think Big/Earn Trust | [[source-fareed-mosavat\|Mosavat]]: skill reset, not extension; find sponsors |
| **Roadmap format?** | [[source-janna-bastow\|Bastow]]: Now/Next/Later, never Gantt | [[source-nickey-skarstad\|Skarstad]]: vision → goals → execution cascade, OKR-compatible |
| **Should PMs originate ideas?** | [[source-yuhki-yamashata\|Yamashita]]: PMs own the "why," not the "what" | [[source-adriel-frederick\|Frederick]]: PMs must hold the user reality; humanize the process |
| **Working Backwards — universal or Amazon-only?** | [[source-ian-mcallister\|McAllister]]: mechanism; portable in spirit | [[source-adriel-frederick\|Frederick]]: mature-Amazon governance kills 0→1 inside a mature org |
| **Hire for frameworks or for traits?** | [[source-petra-wille\|Wille]]: hire for curiosity and empathy; coach frameworks | [[source-lauren-ipsen\|Ipsen]]: hire for the specific mandate; FAANG title ≠ startup fit |
| **Strong opinions, weakly held?** | Common wisdom: state opinions strongly, update on evidence | [[source-shreyas-doshi\|Doshi]]: in practice becomes "strong opinions loudly shared, rarely updated" |
