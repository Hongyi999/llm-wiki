# Source: The future of AI in software development | Inbal Shani (CPO of GitHub)

**Guest**: Inbal Shani
**Published**: 2023-12-01
**Duration**: 50:04

## Executive summary

Inbal Shani, CPO of GitHub, makes a forceful case that AI will augment rather than replace software developers, arguing that 'Copilot is a copilot, not a pilot.' She reveals that 92% of developers already use AI tools and that Copilot users write code 55% faster, but emphasizes that the real value is giving developers time back for creative thinking and system-level architecture rather than just raw code output. Shani identifies AI-driven testing as the most underhyped opportunity in software development and predicts a hybrid future of specialized and generalized AI models. She also shares how GitHub's innovation engine works, from the GitHub Next research team that spawned Copilot to the philosophy of creating bandwidth for experimentation while maintaining tight connections to production.

## Key ideas

1. **Copilot is a copilot, not a pilot**: AI will not replace developers but will shift their focus from writing code (less than 25% of their current time) toward systems thinking, architecture, and creative problem-solving. Junior developers especially benefit, as AI handles basic coding while they learn to understand systems from day one.
2. **AI-driven testing is underhyped**: While everyone focuses on AI code generation, the massive opportunity lies in using AI to automatically generate comprehensive test suites -- unit, integration, load, security, and penetration testing -- which becomes critical as AI produces more code at higher velocity.
3. **Work backwards from the customer problem**: The most common mistake companies make with AI is asking 'what should we do with AI?' instead of 'what problem are we solving and can AI help?' GitHub's own Copilot emerged from working backwards from developer productivity pain points, not from an AI-first mandate.
4. **The future is hybrid AI models**: Generalized LLMs won't solve everything. Specialized domains like autonomous vehicles and aerospace will still require purpose-built models, leading to a world of multiple LLMs with different strengths coexisting alongside niche AI systems.
5. **Innovation requires production proximity**: GitHub Next (their research team) succeeds where other innovation labs fail because it maintains tight synergy with product and engineering, ensuring ideas flow toward production rather than remaining academic exercises.

- [[ai-native-organization|AI Change Management]] — The organizational process companies must invest in when adopting AI tools; simply providing the tool is insufficient.
- [[ai-coding-tools|Copilot Workspace]] — GitHub's next-generation vision for Copilot, expanding beyond code completion to a full AI-assisted development environment.
