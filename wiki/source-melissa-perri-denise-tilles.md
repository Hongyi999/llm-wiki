# The ultimate guide to product operations | Melissa Perri and Denise Tilles

**Guest**: Melissa Perri + Denise Tilles  
**Published**: 2023-11-16  
**Duration**: 1:19:32

## Executive summary

Melissa Perri (CEO of Produx Labs, author of Escaping the Build Trap) and Denise Tilles present the definitive overview of the emerging product operations role. Product ops has gone from nearly non-existent to present in roughly half of scaling tech companies in just five years, with pioneers like Blake Samic spreading the function from Uber to Stripe to OpenAI. The role is structured around three pillars: business data and insights (quantitative), customer and market insights (qualitative), and process and practices (governance). Critically, product ops does not take away decision-making from PMs — it frees them from operational overhead (wrangling data, setting up interviews, standardizing templates) so they can focus on strategic work. The episode includes a detailed case study of rolling out product ops at Athenahealth (365 product teams, 5,000 developers) and practical advice on hiring, reporting structure (always to the CPO), and starting with a single person focused on one pillar.

## Key ideas

1. **Three Pillars of Product Operations**: Business data and insights (quantitative dashboards, product-lens metrics), customer and market insights (research ops, participant databases, qualitative feedback loops from sales/support), and process and practices (roadmap templates, portfolio views, go-to-market coordination).

2. **Product Ops Frees PMs for Strategic Work**: Product ops does not take away decision rights — it removes operational overhead so PMs spend the majority of their time on strategy, discovery, and outcomes instead of learning MongoDB, building one-off reports, or figuring out roadmap templates.

3. **Start with One Person, One Pillar**: Begin by identifying the highest-leverage pillar for your company (high-growth companies usually start with data insights; enterprises undergoing transformation usually start with process/governance), hire one person, demonstrate quick wins, then expand.

4. **Athenahealth Case Study — Product Ops at Scale**: At 365 product teams, product ops solved executive visibility (portfolio roadmaps above Jira), data instrumentation (Amplitude rollout), and process standardization. The CPO who inherited the function says he will never work somewhere without product ops again.

5. **Product Ops Reports to the Head of Product**: The role should always report to the CPO or VP of Product, functioning as a right-hand person who operationalizes strategy, builds repeatable dashboards, and creates shared infrastructure so product leaders stop firefighting and start strategizing.
