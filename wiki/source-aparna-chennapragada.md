# Source: Microsoft CPO: If you aren't prototyping with AI you're doing it wrong | Aparna Chennapragada

**Guest**: Aparna Chennapragada
**Published**: 2025-05-18
**Duration**: 1:01:13
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HbbfXAWcuUo

## Executive summary

Aparna Chennapragada, CPO of Experiences and Devices at Microsoft, articulates a practical product-building philosophy for the AI era rooted in her experience leading Google Lens, Google Now, Robinhood, and now Microsoft's AI productivity tools. Her central argument is that 'prompt sets are the new PRDs'—if you're not prototyping with AI to communicate and validate ideas, you're doing product development wrong. She introduces the concept of NLX (Natural Language Experience) as the new UX, arguing that conversational interfaces require just as much intentional design as graphical ones, with new UI primitives like editable plans, progress indicators, and proactive follow-ups. She outlines a three-factor framework for zero-to-one products (tech inflection + consumer behavior shift + business model shift, need at least 2 of 3) and distinguishes between 'solve mode' and 'scale mode,' warning against premature metrics and premature fixation on a single hill.

## Key ideas

1. **NLX is the new UX—natural language interfaces require intentional design**: Conversations have grammars, structures, and invisible UI elements. New design primitives are emerging: prompts as UI elements (like dropdowns), editable agent plans, progress/thinking indicators, and proactive follow-up suggestions. The balance between verbose and terse thinking-aloud is a critical design decision.
2. **Prompt sets are the new PRDs—demos before memos**: In the AI era, the fastest path from idea to validation is building a prototype, not writing a document. This doesn't mean everyone becomes an engineer; it means the loop from concept to tangible experience compresses dramatically, raising both the floor and ceiling of product quality.
3. **The Van Damme splits of enterprise AI**: Companies must simultaneously ride two diverging trucks—the most compressed tech cycle ever (weeks/months) and the slow reality of human habit change and enterprise change management. The Frontier Program at Microsoft addresses this by creating a fake company where early adopters can experiment with cutting-edge AI tools.
4. **Agents are defined by three dimensions**: Autonomy (delegating higher-order tasks on a spectrum), complexity (multi-step goals rather than one-shot requests), and natural interaction (voice, pointing, conversational rather than just text chat). These three product principles should shape agent design.
5. **Solve before scale—and beware premature metrics**: When building zero-to-one products, be comfortable with wide lurches and apparent chaos. Premature fixation on metrics (CTR, retention) with small samples is 'false precision.' Look for qualitative signal and the 'set timer and play music' use cases that nail a narrow job extremely well before broadening.

## Concepts & frameworks

- **NLX (Natural Language Experience)** — The principle that natural language interfaces require just as much intentional design as graphical UIs, with new primitives like editable plans, progress indicators, and proactive follow-ups.
- **Solve before scale** — A product philosophy that distinguishes between the chaotic 'solve mode' of zero-to-one discovery and the disciplined 'scale mode' of post-PMF growth, warning against premature optimization.
- **Frontier Program** — Microsoft's internal initiative creating a simulated company where teams use cutting-edge AI tools to live one year in the future and discover new ways of working.
- **Intelligence overhang** — The gap between what AI models can currently do and what organizations are actually using them for—a source of competitive arbitrage for product builders who update their priors.
- [[ai-native-organization|Reflexive AI usage]] — Tobi Lütke's concept of habitually asking 'how can I use AI to do what I'm about to do?'—which Chennapragada operationalized with a Chrome extension on every new tab.
- **Three inflection points** — Framework for zero-to-one products: look for tech step function + consumer behavior shift + business model shift; need at least 2 of 3 for a breakthrough product.
- [[ai-coding-tools|Software operator (SO)]] — Chennapragada's coined term for the order-of-magnitude more people who will direct computers at higher levels of abstraction—coding isn't dead, it's democratizing.
