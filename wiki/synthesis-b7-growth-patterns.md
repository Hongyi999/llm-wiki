# Synthesis: B7 Growth Patterns

Across 30 B7 transcripts on growth, acquisition, retention, and activation, **ten convergent patterns** emerge. The disagreements — which are substantial — are covered in [[comparison-b7-growth-approaches]].

## 1. Growth teams can't manufacture PMF

Strongest consensus in the batch:

- **[[source-elena-verna|Verna]]**: Hire head of growth too early = destined to fail. Can't reverse a declining business with a growth hire.
- **[[source-gustaf-alstromer|Alströmer]]** (YC): "Make things people want" is still the whole game.
- **[[source-deb-liu|Deb Liu]]**: Zero-to-one lives in the shadows, not in growth infrastructure.
- **[[source-naomi-gleit|Gleit]]**: FB's growth team may have been 90% riding PMF; the activation metric's value was rally-alignment.
- **[[source-karri-saarinen|Saarinen]]**: [[linear|Linear]] has no growth team; product craft does the work.

**Shared insight**: Growth is a multiplier on PMF, not a substitute for it. Extends B1/B2 "PMF is a treadmill" refrain.

## 2. Activation metric matters — and should be derived, not picked

- **[[source-sean-ellis-growth-hacker|Sean Ellis]]**: 40% "very disappointed."
- **[[source-hila-qu|Hila Qu]]** (GitLab): 2 users × 2 features × 14 days, derived via correlation.
- **[[source-naomi-gleit|Gleit]]** (Facebook): 7 friends × 10 days.
- **[[source-merci-grace|Grace]]** (Slack): 3 humans × 50 messages.
- **[[source-sri-batchu|Batchu]]** (Ramp): 4 events × 30 days.
- **[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford]]**: Lower-rate activation is better (5-15% > 40%).

**Shared insight**: Pick one magic-number engagement event that correlates with retention. Rally the team around it.

## 3. The earned-channel thesis

- **[[source-elena-verna|Verna]]**: Own virality/UGC/sharing; paid/organic search makes Google/Meta rich.
- **[[source-drew-houston|Houston]]**: [[dropbox|Dropbox]] sharing loop still drives 50%+ of acquisition 17 years later.
- **[[source-ben-williams|Ben Williams]]**: Snyk GitHub fix-PR loop is classic company-distributed content loop.
- **[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]**: 70% word-of-mouth via 10x product.
- **[[source-luc-levesque|Levesque]]**: SEO single-keyword wins can build entire companies.

**Shared insight**: [[growth-loops|Growth loops]] compound; funnels deplete. Earned channels > rented channels over the long horizon.

## 4. Retention compounds harder than acquisition

- **[[source-dan-hockenmaier|Hockenmaier]]**: Growth models repeatedly show retention is the most sensitive lever.
- **[[source-sarah-tavel|Tavel]]**: Hierarchy of Engagement — core action → accruing benefits → self-perpetuating.
- **[[source-jackson-shuttleworth|Shuttleworth]]**: 600 experiments on retention (streaks) is Duolingo's highest-leverage surface.
- **[[source-deb-liu|Liu]]**: "Growth as cherry on top" — growth wraps a working core product.
- **[[source-casey-winters_|Winters]]**: Consumer subscription needs >60-70% annual retention; most don't hit it.

**Shared insight**: Retention compounds into virality, content, payback. Most teams under-index on it.

## 5. Onboarding is the highest-leverage single surface

- **[[source-adam-fishman|Fishman]]**: "Onboarding is the only part of your product 100% of users touch."
- **[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford]]**: Guided wizard + segmentation → 20% activation lift at Airtable.
- **[[source-merci-grace|Grace]]**: Day-zero value + designed-from-day-one onboarding, not bolt-on carousels.
- **[[source-gia-laudi|Laudi]]**: JTBD-driven value journey, proactive + reactive onboarding.

**Shared insight**: Onboarding should filter poor-fit users (lower conversion ≠ worse outcome) and proactively nudge activation moments.

## 6. Word of mouth is downstream of 10x product quality, not referral hacks

- **[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]**: Invite rates double per NPS bucket; 10x cheaper is the virality threshold.
- **[[source-oji-udezue|Udezue]]**: "Virality = customer-augmented marketing"; synthetic virality fails if product sucks.
- **[[source-karri-saarinen|Saarinen]]**: Linear grew through word of mouth via craft, not referral mechanics.
- **[[source-ayo-omojola|Omojola]]**: Cash App's "instant" was the shareable cut-through differentiator.

**Shared insight**: Build something users would embarrassingly brag about. Referral mechanics polish existing word of mouth; they don't create it.

## 7. Process — and experimentation — cuts both ways

- **[[source-jackson-shuttleworth|Shuttleworth]]**: 600 experiments with a "keeper of sanctity" — high velocity + governance.
- **[[source-karri-saarinen|Saarinen]]**: Zero A/B tests; customer research + judgment.
- **[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]**: "You can't split-test your way to love."
- **[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford]]**: Experiment only for risk mitigation.
- **[[source-ramesh-johari|Johari]]**: Running everything creates risk-aversion by construction.
- **[[source-elena-verna|Verna]]**: "If you can't reach sample size in a month, do pre/post."

**Real disagreement**: Is experimentation the mechanism of learning or a substitute for conviction? All agree it's been abused.

## 8. Data-led growth: instrument first, then layer

- **[[source-hila-qu|Qu]]**: "PLG is fundamentally DLG." Data dictionary before tool selection.
- **[[source-naomi-gleit|Gleit]]**: Jan 2009, FB growth team stopped all roadmap work to instrument data end-to-end.
- **[[source-crystal-w|Widjaja]]**: Most analytics fails at instrumentation — events without rich properties produce observations not insights.
- **[[source-bangaly-kaba|Kaba]]**: "Understand work" (20-40% of team bandwidth) replaces "identify → justify → execute."
- **[[source-timothy-davis|Davis]]**: Signal vs. noise is the primary skill; MMM/incrementality resurgent.

**Shared insight**: Growth without instrumentation is guesswork. Instrumentation without judgment is paralysis.

## 9. Non-obvious growth channels: cap table, community, partnerships

- **[[source-sri-batchu|Batchu]]**: Cap-table-as-growth — seed investors who become customers.
- **[[source-meltem-kuran|Meltem Kuran]]**: Reddit/Quora human answering (15% of funnel in early Deel).
- **[[source-ben-williams|Williams]]**: Community-led growth (in-person developer conferences at Snyk).
- **[[source-meltem-kuran|Meltem Kuran]]**: Partnerships where audience asks the partner (VCs as payroll distributors).
- **[[source-adam-grenier|Grenier]]**: Emerging-channel evaluation framework — three ingredients.

**Shared insight**: The "Big 4" paid channels are table-stakes; advantage comes from non-obvious channels that match your specific audience's trust patterns.

## 10. Adjacent users explain the long game

- **[[source-bangaly-kaba|Kaba]]**: Your next 100M users aren't like your current ones. Cohort curves dipping isn't regression.
- **[[source-elena-verna|Verna]]**: Overlay new growth models every 18 months — partially driven by adjacent-user adoption.
- **[[source-sarah-tavel|Tavel]]**: Growth stalls when you finish "boiling" your thimble; expand to adjacent thimble.
- **[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford]]**: Segmentation by learning style = adjacent-user thinking applied to onboarding.

**Shared insight**: Growth is an evolutionary sequence of cohorts, not a single static persona. [[adjacent-user-theory|Adjacent user theory]] is the canonical frame.

## Meta-pattern: the four primary levers

Across all 30 B7 sources, growth reduces to four primary levers, each with its own tradition:

| Lever | Canonical voices | Core insight |
|-------|------------------|--------------|
| **Acquisition (loops)** | Verna, Houston, Williams, Levesque | Earned beats rented; loops compound |
| **Activation** | Qu, Isford, Gleit, Batchu, Grace | Derive a magic number; filter poor-fit |
| **Retention** | Shuttleworth, Tavel, Hockenmaier, Winters | Compounds harder than acquisition |
| **Monetization** | Timen, Campbell, Ramanujam | Value-metric pricing; benchmarks per model |

Diagnosing *which lever is currently binding* is the most valuable skill.
