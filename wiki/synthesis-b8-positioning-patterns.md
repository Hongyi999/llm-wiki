# Synthesis: B8 Positioning Patterns

Across 19 B8 transcripts on positioning, narrative, and messaging, **eight convergent patterns** emerge. The disagreements are covered in [[comparison-b8-positioning-approaches]].

## 1. Positioning is a stack, not a slogan

- **[[source-andrew-wilkinson|Wilkinson]]**: Market selection precedes positioning.
- **[[source-barbra-gago|Gago]]**: Category precedes positioning.
- **[[source-geoffrey-moore|Moore]]**: Lifecycle stage dictates which positioning playbook.
- **[[source-arielle-jackson|Jackson]]**: Purpose precedes positioning precedes personality.
- **[[source-matthew-dicks|Dicks]]** / [[source-nancy-duarte|Duarte]]: Craft delivers whatever narrative you've built.

**Shared insight**: Each altitude demands its own framework. Most failures are altitude mismatches.

## 2. "Fundamentally different" trumps "incrementally better"

- **[[source-jag-duggal|Duggal]]**: Only fundamentally different experiences create emotional pull driving WOM.
- **[[source-seth-godin|Godin]]**: Purple Cow — worth-making-a-remark-about.
- **[[source-oji-udezue|Udezue]]** (B7): Zone of Benefit = 3x better minimum for behavior change.
- **[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]** (B7): 10x product is the virality threshold.
- **[[source-ayo-omojola|Omojola]]** (B7): Cash App won via "instant" — fundamentally different on one dimension.

**Shared insight**: 10-20% better is invisible. The market only notices (and talks about) discontinuities.

## 3. Narrow audience, wide empathy

- **[[source-seth-godin|Godin]]**: Smallest viable audience.
- **[[source-geoffrey-moore|Moore]]**: Beachhead = 30-50% of small coherent segment.
- **[[source-arielle-jackson|Jackson]]**: Named model persona.
- **[[source-andrew-wilkinson|Wilkinson]]**: Fish where the fish are.
- **[[source-lulu-cheng-meservey|Meservey]]**: Concentric circles of influence.

**Shared insight**: Specificity wins. Broad-targeting is a failure to commit.

## 4. Brand = promise, not logo

- **[[source-seth-godin|Godin]]**: "Hyatt has a logo. Nike has a brand."
- **[[source-arielle-jackson|Jackson]]**: Your brand is who people think you are.
- **[[source-barbra-gago|Gago]]**: Brand values made visible (Miro letters = values).
- **[[source-kevin-aluwi|Aluwi]]** (Gojek): Physical branding (jackets/helmets) as survival mechanism.
- **[[source-jag-duggal|Duggal]]**: Culture that makes "love us fanatically" the #1 value = brand operationalized.

**Shared insight**: Brand is a set of promises consistently kept. Logos are downstream.

## 5. Story requires one memorable thing

- **[[source-matthew-dicks|Dicks]]**: Every story is one five-second moment.
- **[[source-tristan-de-montebello|Tristan]]**: Bow and Arrow — one sentence you want remembered.
- **[[source-nancy-duarte|Duarte]]**: Star moment.
- **[[source-seth-godin|Godin]]**: Know in advance what you want them to say.

**Shared insight**: If the audience can't repeat one sentence, the story failed.

## 6. Internal narrative = external narrative

- **[[source-ami-vora|Vora]]**: Metaphors align cross-functional teams (face-to-face, Dolores Park).
- **[[source-bill-carr|Carr]]**: Amazon's leadership principles wired into mechanisms.
- **[[source-donna-lichaw|Lichaw]]**: Leader's self-story is upstream of team story.
- **[[source-jag-duggal|Duggal]]**: Nubank values memorized and used in decisions.
- **[[source-barbra-gago|Gago]]**: Rebrand-as-product-development mobilizes whole company.

**Shared insight**: You can't tell an external story your team doesn't believe. Brand is downstream of culture.

## 7. Delivery craft matters as much as content

- **[[source-matt-abrahams|Abrahams]]**: Dare to be dull; reframe anxiety as excitement.
- **[[source-tristan-de-montebello|Tristan]]**: Accordion Method; stay-in-character.
- **[[source-wes-kao|Kao]]**: Sales then logistics; MOO; signposting.
- **[[source-matthew-dicks|Dicks]]**: Dinner test for public speaking.
- **[[source-nancy-duarte|Duarte]]**: Can they see what you're saying?

**Shared insight**: A great story badly told still loses. Treat delivery like a trained skill.

## 8. Go direct; earn your distribution

- **[[source-lulu-cheng-meservey|Meservey]]**: Founders speak as themselves; pick one channel.
- **[[source-jason-feifer|Feifer]]**: Press is optional; understand publication mission.
- **[[source-seth-godin|Godin]]**: Pick your distribution as a strategic choice.
- **[[source-andrew-wilkinson|Wilkinson]]**: Underdogs build their own Rolodex; hire finished talent via reputation.

**Shared insight**: Press releases to cold journalists rarely work. Founder-led distribution compounds.

## Meta-pattern: positioning is upstream of most problems most operators blame on other things

Growth teams can't fix broken positioning. Feature shipping can't fix broken narrative. Hiring can't fix broken internal story. Most "scaling crises" are positioning crises one altitude up.
