# Linear

Project management / issue tracker. Founded by [[source-karri-saarinen|Karri Saarinen]] (designer), Jori Lallo, and Tuomas Artman. Profitable two years; net-negative lifetime burn (more cash in the bank than ever raised). Canonical case study of craft-first, no-experimentation, design-led product building.

## Operating philosophy

- **No A/B tests and no per-feature metric goals** — "Magic and science" balance. Talk to customers obsessively; use judgment. "Sometimes people use data a lot because they're afraid of making the wrong choice."
- **One PM at 50 people** — Engineers and designers own projects end-to-end. Project lead is an assignment, not a title.
- **Opinionated software** — Cycles (vs. sprints), triage, built-in defaults. "Design something for *someone*," not everyone.
- **Main quest vs. side quests** — Ignore SOC 2 until asked; skip podcasts until timing is right. YC framing: talk to customers, build product, exercise.
- **Craft + speed via early-in-production shipping** — Design rough → ship behind flag → test with 1-10 opt-in customers → polish before general release.

## Hiring & beta

- **Paid work trial** — Every hire does a multi-day paid contractor stint on real problems in the real codebase.
- **Cohort-based private beta** — 4K waitlist from Twitter launch. Invited ~10 users/week in cohorts so feedback wasn't redundant.
- **Pricing slider** — Literal slider ($1-$20/seat, user chooses) to test willingness to pay before launching paid tier.

## The Linear Method

Company's published operating doc. Combines:
- Cycles of one week
- Triage workflow
- Opinionated defaults
- Keyboard-first UX
- Small team size

## PMF as spectrum

Not binary. Linear targeted **startup PMF** for years 1-2, then **enterprise PMF** for years 3-4. Follow the pull (crypto then, AI companies now).
