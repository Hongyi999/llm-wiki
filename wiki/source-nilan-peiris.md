# Source: How to drive word of mouth | Nilan Peiris (CPO of Wise)

**Guest**: Nilan Peiris (CPO of Wise)
**Published**: 2023-09-24
**Duration**: ~1h 16m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZifSLGOrrw

## Executive summary

Peiris explains how Wise systematically engineered 70% word-of-mouth acquisition (on ~16M customers, +1M/quarter) by obsessively optimizing three "pillars" — price, speed, ease of use — to be 8-10x better than alternatives. Key insight: invite rates roughly double at each NPS bucket (6, 7-8, 9-10), so raising NPS compounds virality. The operational formula: get the customer to feel "an experience they didn't know was previously possible."

## Key ideas

1. **NPS is not vanity — invites double per bucket** — Overlaying referral data on NPS responses showed invite rates roughly 2x'ing from 6 to 7-8, again to 9-10. Reframes NPS as a growth leverage metric. Wise's NPS sits at 70 (iPhone-tier; banks average -30).
2. **The three pillars: price, speed, ease of use** — Derived from NPS verbatim comments read aloud company-wide. Becomes the entire product strategy.
3. **You can't split test your way to love** — Wise deliberately avoids A/B-everything. Build conviction from customer research, then commit to big price cuts (from 0.5% to 0.35%) and big speed improvements (instant transfer) without requiring incremental ROI proof.
4. **8-10x better is the virality threshold, not 10-20%** — "If you entered with a product priced at 5.9% and alternative was 6, customers would use us but they wouldn't talk about us. We only got advocacy when we were 8 to 10 times cheaper."
5. **Work backwards from the theoretical ideal** — Don't iterate forward from today's product. Ask "what's the theoretical minimum cost?" Then fight for years to reach it (e.g., Bank of England account; lobbying Singapore regulators for world's first eKYC license).
6. **Mission authenticity as word-of-mouth multiplier** — A rebrand email with no CTA produced more signups than any campaign. Customers will lobby governments for you if the mission rings true.
7. **Global product + weak local ownership** — Single global tech stack, but regional teams can push code directly, avoiding "HQ vs international" friction.

## B7 angle

Canonical word-of-mouth growth — direct extension of [[source-sean-ellis-growth-hacker|Sean Ellis's]] "make something people love" and [[source-rahul-vohra-superhuman|Vohra's]] "very disappointed" PMF. NPS-to-invite-rate curve is the most tactical operationalization of word-of-mouth in the corpus. Disagrees sharply with [[source-casey-winters|Casey Winters's]] experimentation-heavy playbook ("you can't split-test your way to love"). Strong counterpoint to [[source-meltem-kuran|Meltem Kuran]] at Deel (paid+SEO heavy) — two legitimate paths to fast SaaS scale.
