# Mochary Method

A scripted playbook for CEO and executive leadership developed by [[source-matt-mochary|Matt Mochary]] and his team. Where [[source-ben-horowitz|Horowitz]] writes war stories and [[source-jerry-colonna|Colonna]] excavates psychology, the Mochary Method is pure *script*: numbered, reusable steps for the hardest parts of running a company. Adopted by CEOs of Coinbase, OpenAI, Reddit, Rippling, Brex, Front, Notion, Clipboard Health, Scale AI, Attentive Mobile.

## Core moves

### 1. Fear-gives-bad-advice

> "When someone is in fear, they're gripped. They can't see reality."

**Protocol**: Have a trusted outsider name it ("I perceive you to be in fear"). Do the opposite of what fear counsels. "I've made this bet hundreds of times and so far I've never lost."

### 2. Firing as agency, not abandonment

Separate **decision** (always in service of the customer) from **implementation**. Become the person's agent "like Michael Ovitz" — actively place them in their next job. Test: "Everyone I have let go is still my friend."

### 3. The difficult-conversation script

1. **Warn** — "This is going to be a difficult conversation. Take a few seconds to prepare."
2. **Deliver** the news directly.
3. **Invite emotion** — "What are you feeling?" Reflect it back.

Surprise triggers the amygdala; pre-warning reduces trauma dramatically.

### 4. Humane mass layoffs

1. **Dollar target**, not headcount target.
2. **Let each manager choose** whom to let go.
3. **All 1:1s in a single morning** — "If they hear it from their manager in a 1:1, it'll be okay. If they hear it in an email or group chat, it's terrible."
4. **Same-day all-hands**.
5. **Follow-up 1:1** with every stay-teamer so they can voice sadness/anger/fear.
6. **Cut deep so you only cut once** — Multiple rounds create company-wide PTSD.

Within 60 days, companies perform better in absolute terms.

### 5. Top goal / accountability partner

Every leader has one top goal. Weekly accountability partner (another CEO) to review progress. Drawn from Greg McKeown's *Essentialism*.

### 6. Energy audit / four zones

Map all activities into:
- **Genius** (unique talent, energizing) — maximize.
- **Excellence** (very good, draining) — delegate.
- **Competence** (okay, neutral) — delegate.
- **Incompetence** (bad at it, draining) — stop.

### 7. Anger is a cover for pain

Under anger is almost always hurt, fear, or sadness. Naming the underlying feeling defuses the anger.

### 8. Independent C-corp for innovation

Spin new products into a separate C corp with founder-mentality leads (often recruited from YC-alumni-whose-startups-failed pool). Report directly to CEO. Don't touch core codebase's review process.
