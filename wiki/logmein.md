# LogMeIn

Remote access software. Case study from [[sean-ellis|Sean Ellis]] in **activation** as the hidden bottleneck between PMF and growth.

## The activation unlock

- The Sean Ellis survey was healthy — "very disappointed" score was strong.
- But only ~**5% of signups** ever became active users.
- After fixing onboarding friction (so more signups actually reached the product's value moment), activation jumped to ~**50%**.
- Growth numbers followed the activation curve, not the top-of-funnel.

The lesson: **satisfaction PMF** without **activation** is wasted demand. Survey-based PMF signal only converts to business outcomes if new users can reach the value moment.
