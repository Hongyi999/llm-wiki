# Source: Leveraging growth advisors, mastering SEO, and honing your craft | Luc Levesque

**Guest**: Luc Levesque (CGO at Shopify; ex-Meta growth for Messenger/Instagram/WhatsApp; ex-Tripadvisor; advisor to Pinterest, Twitter, Canva, Thumbtack, Patreon)
**Published**: 2023-06-15
**Duration**: ~1h 20m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xf_ZXIGCQb0

## Executive summary

Playbook for using growth advisors, deep take on SEO as a growth channel (including how ChatGPT/Bard will disrupt it), and a theory of hiring built around "signs of excellence." Cross-cutting theme: impact is the only word that matters, and the right growth advisor at the right moment can change a company's trajectory in a single sentence.

## Key ideas

1. **10X growth advisors are real but rare** — "The right person at the right time can literally say a sentence that changes the trajectory of your company." One landing-page recommendation in a first advisor meeting generated a 3-week lift measured in hundreds of percent for a now-public company.
2. **Structure advisor deals for alignment** — Equity only (aligns exit incentives); vest early rather than late (incentivizes fast knowledge transfer); three-month cliff; long tail (10+ years). Goal: advisor should make themselves unnecessary.
3. **SEO is exponential, not linear** — Single keywords can build entire companies (TravelPod/Tripadvisor: "Travel Blog"). Two archetypes: (a) few-page product sites needing editorial content, (b) UGC/marketplace sites with compounding page generation (LinkedIn's "viral loop feeding an SEO loop").
4. **ChatGPT/Bard will disrupt informational-keyword SEO within 12-24 months** — Google's AI answer box at top-of-SERP will kill click-through on informational queries; transactional/navigational queries more protected. Shift thinking from "optimize for platform" to "teach the AI why you're the best."
5. **Hire on signs of excellence + treat hiring as craft** — Best predictor of future performance is past performance. Look for 3+ concrete signs of excellence. Strongest single signal: a former boss leaves the company, then comes back specifically to poach the candidate. Involve spouses/families in senior recruiting.
