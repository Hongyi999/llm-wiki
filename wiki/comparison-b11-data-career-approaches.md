# Comparison: B11 — Data, metrics, career & personal growth approaches

17 unique voices spanning experimentation, data orgs, OKRs, career strategy, personal growth, and community-led growth. B11 is the most eclectic batch — the "miscellaneous drawer" that connects the analytical and the personal.

## School 1: Experimentation & data rigor

| Voice | Core thesis | Key mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| [[source-ronny-kohavi\|Kohavi]] | Most ideas fail (66-92%); test everything | OEC, Twyman's Law, SRM, P-value corrections |
| [[source-laura-schaffer\|Schaffer]] | 80% of hypotheses are wrong; lower confidence thresholds | Good friction vs. bad friction; painted-door validation |
| [[source-jess-lachs\|Lachs]] | Analytics = business impact, not service | Centralized org with embedded pods; proxy metrics; fail-state metrics |
| [[source-jeff-weinstein\|Weinstein]] | Metrics from the customer's perspective | "Users having a bad day"; zero-ticket metric; Study Groups |

**Convergence**: All four reject vanity metrics. Metrics must be causally linked to user outcomes and drive action, not just report results.

## Key disagreements across B11

1. **Statistical rigor**: Kohavi (95% significance; replicate before shipping) vs. Schaffer (lower confidence thresholds; qualitative corroboration is sufficient for SaaS)
2. **OKR applicability**: Wodtke (vitamin for healthy companies; pilot with best team) vs. Rathi (OKRs fail in three-sided marketplaces; holistic narratives work better)
3. **Power vs. vulnerability**: Pfeffer (appear powerful; use power; success excuses everything) vs. Robin (appropriate vulnerability makes leaders stronger; inquiry > advice)
4. **Career optimization**: Pfeffer (network relentlessly; build a brand; political skill is learnable) vs. Weaver (internal scorecard; external achievement doesn't resolve inadequacy) vs. Chen Rekhi (values alignment over resume optimization)
5. **Metrics philosophy**: Lachs (proxy metrics for long-term outcomes; common currency) vs. Weinstein (customer-perspective metrics; zero-ticket goal) vs. Kohavi (OEC as composite causally predictive of LTV)
