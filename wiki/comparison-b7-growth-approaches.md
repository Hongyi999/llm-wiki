# Comparison: B7 Growth Approaches

The B7 batch surfaces at least **eight distinct schools of growth thought**. Each is internally coherent, advocated by credible operators at credible companies. Most disagree sharply on which levers matter most.

## Eight schools

| School | Canonical voice | Core move |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Earned-channels / anti-pattern** | [[source-elena-verna]] | Own virality/UGC/sharing; rotate models every 18 months; don't rebrand. |
| **Word-of-mouth engineering** | [[source-nilan-peiris]] ([[wise]]) | 10x product + NPS = invite-rate doubling; you can't split-test love. |
| **Paid + SEO education-led** | [[source-meltem-kuran]] (Deel) | Build the skeleton before makeup; Reddit/Quora human answers; long-tail paid. |
| **Craft + opinionated software** | [[source-karri-saarinen]] ([[linear]]) | No A/B tests; one PM at 50; pricing slider; paid work trials. |
| **Performance marketing ops** | [[source-timothy-davis]] ([[shopify]]) | Signs of life tests; lookalike laddering; ops cadence matrix. |
| **Data-led (DLG) PLG** | [[source-hila-qu]] / [[source-ben-williams]] | Data dictionary first; audit funnel; PLG → PLS evolution. |
| **Hierarchy of Engagement** | [[source-sarah-tavel]] (Pinterest/Benchmark) | Core action → accruing benefits → self-perpetuating loops. |
| **Pre-PMF traction first** | [[source-gustaf-alstromer]] (YC) | Can't growth-team your way to PMF; cold outreach 10% rule. |

Plus the customer-first voice:
- **Customer-led growth** — [[source-gia-laudi]]. Kill pirate metrics; map JTBD-driven value journey.

## Six disagreements sharpened

### 1. Can you split-test your way to growth?

- **[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]**: "You can't split-test your way to love." Build conviction from research, commit to big changes.
- **[[source-karri-saarinen|Saarinen]] (Linear)**: No A/B tests at all.
- **[[source-ramesh-johari|Johari]]**: Experiment culture of "run everything" creates risk-aversion by construction.
- **[[source-timothy-davis|Davis]]**: Incrementality testing is the actually-causal frontier (MMM revival).
- **[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford]]**: Experiment only for risk mitigation.
- **[[source-jackson-shuttleworth|Shuttleworth]] (Duolingo)**: 600 experiments on one feature, 50% win rate.

**Real disagreement**: Whether experimentation is (a) the mechanism of learning, (b) a substitute for conviction, or (c) both done poorly.

### 2. Paid or organic-first?

- **[[source-meltem-kuran|Meltem Kuran]]**: Cheap channels first; skeleton-before-makeup.
- **[[source-timothy-davis|Davis]]**: "Paid is for everyone," starting with paid search.
- **[[source-elena-verna|Verna]]**: Own earned channels; rented channels make Google/Meta rich.
- **[[source-nilan-peiris|Peiris]]**: 70% word of mouth; paid is negligible.
- **[[source-yuriy-timen|Timen]]**: Three archetypes — paid works when LTV is strong and free-to-paid >5%.

**Real disagreement**: Which side of the 2×2 (earned vs. paid × strong LTV vs. weak) you're on.

### 3. Activation: high or low rate?

- **[[source-lauryn-isford|Isford]]**: Lower rates are better (5-15%) — higher retention correlation.
- **[[source-hila-qu|Hila Qu]]**: Derive via correlation on high-value actions; don't pick arbitrarily.
- **[[source-sri-batchu|Batchu]] / [[source-naomi-gleit|Gleit]]**: Rally-alignment is the real value; precision matters less.

**Convergence**: All agree activation metric must be carefully chosen, correlated with retention. Disagreement on whether to maximize rate or selectivity.

### 4. Can growth teams exist before PMF?

- **[[source-gustaf-alstromer|Alströmer]]**: No. Growth-team thinking is premature optimization; do unscalable things.
- **[[source-elena-verna|Verna]]**: Hire head of growth too early = destined to fail.
- **[[source-deb-liu|Liu]]**: Zero-to-one inside big companies needs the shadows, not growth infrastructure.
- **[[source-brian-balfour|Balfour]]** / [[source-casey-winters|Winters]] (canonical): Growth-loop thinking can start earlier.

**Shared insight**: Growth teams can't manufacture PMF. Disagreement on what "growth" work looks like pre-PMF — is it product-led (Alströmer/Verna) or structural (Balfour/Winters)?

### 5. Marketplaces: supply or demand first?

- **[[source-camille-hearst|Hearst]]**: Always supply first. Can't optimize demand if cars aren't there.
- **[[source-dan-hockenmaier|Hockenmaier]]**: Demand aggregation is "the only thing that matters"; founders over-rotate on supply.
- **[[source-sarah-tavel|Tavel]]**: Find your thimble (geography × category), boil the white-hot center until suppliers self-recruit.
- **[[source-ramesh-johari|Johari]]**: "A marketplace business never starts as a marketplace business" — focus on the one side you're winning.

**Real disagreement**: Whether supply is the product (Hearst) or the enabler of demand aggregation (Hockenmaier). All converge on "don't chase both equally."

### 6. Is there a canonical activation metric?

- **[[source-sean-ellis-growth-hacker|Sean Ellis]]**: 40% "very disappointed."
- **[[source-hila-qu|Qu]]** (GitLab): 2 users × 2 features × 14 days.
- **[[source-merci-grace|Grace]]** (Slack): 3 humans × 50 messages.
- **[[source-sri-batchu|Batchu]]** (Ramp): 4 events × 30 days.
- **[[source-naomi-gleit|Gleit]]** (Facebook): 7 friends × 10 days.
- **[[source-sarah-tavel|Tavel]]**: The concept generalizes — pick the highest-correlated action via regression.

**Convergence**: All agree *an* activation metric matters. Disagreement on whether it should be derived (Qu/Tavel) or picked by conviction + research (Gleit/Ellis).

## Synthesis

B7 is the largest thematic batch (30 deep-ingested sources) and the most heterogeneous. The eight schools above correspond to different **stages**, **business models**, and **channel DNA**. No single growth playbook is universal; the operative question is: "which school fits my stage + model + channel?"
