# Source: Meta's head of product on working with Mark Zuckerberg, early growth tactics, and more | Naomi Gleit

**Guest**: Naomi Gleit (employee #29 at Facebook, now Meta's head of product; longest-tenured exec besides Zuck)
**Published**: 2024-10-27
**Duration**: ~1h 38m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sTYuKgzZoL8

## Executive summary

19 years of institutional memory: how the legendary Facebook growth team originated the "7 friends in 10 days" activation metric, why they bet on product-and-data-driven growth when the industry expected marketing to drive user growth, and her personal operating system for complex cross-functional projects (canonical docs, extreme clarity, three-option traffic-light decision frameworks, PM-as-conductor).

## Key ideas

1. **The 7-friends-in-10-days myth, clarified** — Yes it was real (also 10-in-14 — same retention curve point). But the bigger value was having "extreme clarity around the goal" so everyone optimized the same thing. The rally-alignment did most of the work.
2. **Growth accounting + data-driven product-driven growth** — Danny Ferrante's framework: net growth = new - stale + resurrected. Discovery that churn and resurrection lines dwarfed new-user is what shifted the team from acquisition to retention focus. In Jan 2009, the growth team stopped all roadmap work to instrument data end-to-end.
3. **Macro and micro barriers** — Macro: college-only → high-school → work networks → any email → feature-phone → Internet.org. Micro: 20% of signups weren't confirming email → allow notifications to unconfirmed accounts, count notification clicks as confirmation.
4. **User-generated translation over professional translation** — Rather than hiring translators, Facebook built an in-line translation product; today supports 100+ languages. Template for product-and-engineering-replacing-a-business-function.
5. **PM as conductor + extreme clarity toolkit** — PMs don't play instruments; they ensure tempo and harmony. Running tactics: canonical doc (one source of truth), single-threaded owners, canonical nomenclature, numbered lists, real-time-edited visuals in meetings, pre-reads 24 hrs ahead, three-option traffic-light decision tables. "80% of project problems are people/process, not strategy/execution."
