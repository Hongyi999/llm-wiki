# How to be the best coach to product people | Petra Wille (Strong Product People)

**Guest**: Petra Wille
**Published**: 2022-11-27
**Duration**: 1:12:17
**YouTube**: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4n3ybRqU5mU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4n3ybRqU5mU)

## Executive summary

Petra Wille, independent product leadership coach and author of *Strong Product People*, provides the most structured framework in B4 for PM leaders who want to become better coaches. Her five ingredients for effective coaching, the PMwheel competency assessment (eight skill buckets), and her push for storytelling as a career-critical skill turn "be a good manager" into a concrete practice. She also argues that curiosity and empathy should be hired for, not coached — you can teach frameworks but you can't teach wonder.

## Key ideas

1. **Five ingredients of PM coaching**: (1) Define what "good" looks like in your context (the compass), (2) Assess where each PM currently is, (3) Share a vision for their growth, (4) Create a development plan (owned by the PM), (5) Follow up consistently. Start with the development plan if you need speed.
2. **PMwheel framework**: Eight buckets — understanding problems, finding solutions, planning, getting it done, listening & learning, teamwork, personal growth, agile foundations. Each bucket carries 15+ framing questions.
3. **Invest in storytelling**: Great stories require two weeks, not one afternoon. Use natural language, remove jargon, prepare in three lengths (75-second elevator, 6-minute briefing, 18-minute TED) and three formats (spoken, written, visual).
4. **Hire for curiosity and empathy**: These personality traits are extremely hard to coach. Screen for them in hiring because you can teach frameworks but you cannot teach wonder.
5. **Community of Practice as a development lever**: Internal CoPs are cheaper and often more effective than external training. They increase retention and reduce coaching burden on managers.
