# Amazon

Amazon is the retail/cloud/media giant whose internal product operating system — Working Backwards, the PR/FAQ, the six-pager, Leadership Principles, the Weekly Business Review — has become one of the most influential organizational templates in tech. More B4 alumni passed through Amazon than any other company, and most carry the operating muscle with them.

## Why Amazon matters to B4

Amazon is the canonical "writing culture" company and the source of multiple frameworks that have diffused across product management:

- **Working Backwards / PR-FAQ** — The press-release-first process for new product definition. Canonical articulation in [[source-ian-mcallister]] (as mechanism, not religion) and [[source-bill-carr]].
- **Six-pager memo** — Narrative documents read silently at the start of meetings; replaces PowerPoint for high-stakes thinking. Referenced in [[source-jason-shah]] and [[source-ian-mcallister]].
- **Weekly Business Review (WBR)** — Cascading metrics ritual with built-in accountability. Explained in [[source-ian-mcallister]].
- **Bezos's three investment tests** — (1) Big idea? (2) Should we do it? (3) Legitimate plan to succeed? (See [[source-ian-mcallister]].)
- **14 Leadership Principles** — Earn Trust, Think Big, Customer Obsession, Bias for Action, etc. Multiple B4 guests cite them as their internal operating code.

## Cultural signatures

- **Writing is thinking**: Documents force clarity that slides hide. The act of writing a six-pager is the thinking.
- **Day 1 mentality**: Bezos's term for the posture of a startup, maintained as a hedge against Day-2 complacency.
- **Customer obsession over competitor fixation**: Work backwards from customer problems, not from competitor features.
- **Two-pizza teams**: Autonomous teams small enough to be fed by two pizzas.
- **Mechanisms over good intentions**: Culture is what your processes produce; design mechanisms, not posters.

## Tensions and critiques

- Amazon's writing culture is labor-intensive and doesn't transfer cleanly to orgs that lack the discipline or leadership backing.
- B4 guests like Ian McAllister stress that the PR/FAQ is a *mechanism* to enforce problem-first thinking — teams that cargo-cult the artifact without the intent get the ceremony and none of the clarity.
- [[source-adriel-frederick]] argues that applying mature-Amazon governance (OKRs, six-pagers) to 0→1 work inside any company kills incubation.
