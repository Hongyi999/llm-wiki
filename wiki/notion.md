# Notion

All-in-one workspace (docs, notes, databases, wikis). Founded by [[source-ivan-zhao|Ivan Zhao]] and Simon Last. Canonical case study of a horizontal consumer-to-business (B2C2B) product with craft-as-religion culture.

## The pivot story

- **Three-to-four "lost years"** with multiple codebase rewrites.
- **Near-death COVID database-scaling crisis** in 2020.
- Final unlock: the "sugar-coated broccoli" framing — hide the Lego-for-software vision inside a form factor (productivity doc) people already want.

## Operating principles

- **Talent density / small-bus metaphor** — Smaller bus turns corners faster. Profitable for years, first salesperson at $10M ARR, first PM at 50 people. Tracks revenue-per-employee, not headcount.
- **Craft as operating value** — Conference rooms named after timeless tools (iPhone, Toshiba rice cooker, Lamy 2000).
- **"We run our company on Notion"** — Dogfooding is absolute.

## Strategic positioning

- **One of only three 1B-user use cases** — docs/notes (alongside calendar and email; both on Notion roadmap).
- **Lego-brick vs. Lego-box framework** — Composable building blocks are beloved by power users but enterprise buyers want "solutions" (pre-assembled). A year-plus recovery from over-solutionizing sprint features that broke composability.
- **B2C2B flywheel** — Consumer adoption → template library → team adoption → enterprise bundle.
