# Reforge

**Reforge** is a growth-and-product education platform founded by [[source-brian-balfour|Brian Balfour]]. It offers cohort-based courses taught by senior operators from companies like HubSpot, Pinterest, Airbnb, Slack, and others. Reforge has been a central node in the modern growth-PM community and a frequent recurring reference across B4.

## Why Reforge matters to B4

- **Frameworks factory**: Many of the canonical growth-PM frameworks (growth loops, four-fits, onboarding funnels, compounding engines) are codified and taught through Reforge.
- **Alumni network**: Guest-lecturers and course graduates seed many of the senior growth and PM roles in tech.
- **Cross-company synthesis**: Reforge's program design pulls together patterns across companies, which is the same pattern-extraction work B4 does at episode scale.

## Concepts closely associated with Reforge

- **Growth loops** over growth funnels.
- **Four fits**: product-market, product-channel, channel-model, model-market.
- **Retention as the foundation** of sustainable growth.
- **Quantitative PM** — metrics literacy as a PM competency, not a growth-PM specialty.
