# Uber

Ride-hailing and delivery platform referenced across four B3 sources as a case study in scaling, leadership, strategy, and product operations. JM Nickels (early PM), Ebi Atawodi (global payments PM), and Brian Tolkin (employee #100) provide firsthand perspectives. Hamilton Helmer uses Uber to illustrate the critical distinction between network effects and network economies.

## B3 appearances

- [[source-brian-tolkin|Brian Tolkin]] — Global ride-hailing company where Brian started in ops, moved to product, launched UberPool, and created the product operations function.
- [[source-ebi-atawodi|Ebi Atawodi]] — Ride-hailing company where Atawodi served as Head of Product for Wallet, Checkout, Pay, and Financial Products.
- [[source-hamilton-helmer|Hamilton Helmer]] — Ride-hailing company cited as having network effects but not network economies, winning through geographically specific scale economies and attrition.
- [[source-john-mark-nickels|John Mark Nickels]] — Global ride-hailing platform where JM built UberPool and led marketplace pricing/matching systems.
