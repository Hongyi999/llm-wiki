# Source: Why ChatGPT will be the next big growth channel (and how to capitalize on it)

## Executive summary

Brian Balfour, founder of Reforge and former VP Growth at HubSpot, maps the four-step platform cycle that defines winner-take-most distribution shifts. He argues new distribution platforms emerge every 15-20 years (Facebook, iOS, Google, LinkedIn), follow a predictable cycle (Step 0: competition → Step 1: moat identification → Step 2: platform opening → Step 3: platform closing/monetization), and ChatGPT appears to be entering Step 2 imminently. The moat: context and memory, where ChatGPT leads (memory feature, data connectors, retention curves). Startups that move early win 10-100x; late adopters face a prisoner's dilemma—everyone eventually plays, but timing matters enormously.

## Key ideas

1. Four-step platform cycle: Step 0 (competitive market, no clear winner), Step 1 (moat identified, ecosystem needed to gather moat faster), Step 2 (platform opens, third-party apps get distribution), Step 3 (platform closes, monopoly locks in, developers starve). Cycles compress—Facebook took 5 years; cycles now 3-5 years.
2. ChatGPT moat is context + memory: unlike commodity LLMs (Claude, Gemini), ChatGPT's defensibility is not base model intelligence but accumulated personal context and memory. More you use it, better outputs. Mirrors successful platforms (Google had search data moat, Facebook had friend graph).
3. Retention signals win: ChatGPT shows rare 'smile curve' retention (dips, then rises back) + higher retention curves than all competitors, improving over time. History shows retention winners, not distribution leaders, win platform wars.
4. Prisoner's dilemma for startups: you cannot opt out. If you don't distribute on the new platform, competitors will, customer expectations shift, you lose. Better to move early and cheap than late and desperate.
5. Cycles getting shorter and margin window thinner: escape velocity windows shrinking (5 years→3 years). Startups must identify and commit to one new platform per cycle; incumbents place multiple bets but move slower by nature.

## Concepts & frameworks

- **Platform Cycle Theory** — Four-step cycle: competition→moat identification→platform opening→closing. Startups win by entering Step 2 early; timing and understanding cycle stage is more important than guessing the exact winner.
- **Context and Memory Moat** — In multi-LLM world, base model intelligence converges; moat shifts to proprietary user context and memory. ChatGPT's memory features and data connectors are competitive defensibility, not the model itself.
- **Smile Curve Retention** — Rare retention pattern where cohort usage dips early, then rises back and compounds. Signals strong product-market fit and learning curve; ChatGPT and Slack both exhibit this pattern.
- **Prisoner's Dilemma in Distribution** — All companies must eventually adopt new platform or lose competitively; cannot opt out. Better to move early and cheap than late and expensive; creates urgency and first-mover advantage.
- [[product-velocity-ai-era|Cycle Compression]] — New platform windows are shrinking (5 years→3 years). Changes to product, code, and business accelerate; founding teams must commit faster; incumbent moat defense time shrinks dramatically.
