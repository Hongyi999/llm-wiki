## Notes on methodology

Classification is regex-based over `title + description` with priority order:

1. **ai-products** wins first (AI is the most distinctive axis in 2024–2025 content)
2. **pricing-monetization** (tiny but highly specific)
3. **sales-gtm** (enterprise / founder-led sales)
4. **data-metrics** (A/B testing, experimentation, analytics)
5. **growth** (acquisition / retention / activation loops)
6. **positioning-narrative** (messaging / brand / storytelling)
7. **product-discovery** (PM craft / strategy / discovery)
8. **design-craft** (design / taste / UX-UI)
9. **hiring-management** (people / leadership / culture)
10. **founder-0to1** (founder stories / early-stage)
11. **career-ic** (career / productivity / self)
12. **misc** (fallback)

First-match wins, so an episode about "AI hiring" lands in ai-products, not hiring-management. Within a batch, sources are sorted by publish date (newest first) so synthesis pages can open with the most recent take and work backward.
