# Managing nerves, anxiety, and burnout | Jonny Miller (Nervous System Mastery)

**Guest**: Jonny Miller
**Published**: 2024-01-28
**Duration**: 1:14:15
**YouTube**: [Lenny's Podcast — Jonny Miller](https://www.lennyspodcast.com/)

## Executive summary

Jonny Miller — founder of Nervous System Mastery (a course for founders and tech leaders), former Techstars 2012 founder who pivoted into somatic coaching after personal tragedy — argues "state over story": change the body first and the mind follows, because four times more neural traffic flows body-to-brain than the reverse. He provides a concrete toolkit (4-4-8 breath, humming, espresso breath, NSDR) and a diagnostic lens (APE: Awareness, Posture, Emotion) leaders can use to regulate nerves before meetings, build interoceptive awareness, and catch early burnout signals. Emotional debt — unresolved stress stored in the body — compounds like technical debt until it forces a crash.

## Key ideas

1. **State over story (bottom-up regulation)**: Reframes, pep talks, and mindfulness are top-down and slow; breath directly changes blood chemistry via the insular cortex and parasympathetic nervous system, which then reshapes thoughts. Exhale twice as long as inhale to downshift; inhale-emphasis to activate.
2. **A leadership toolkit of "if this, then breathe"**: 4-4-8 box breathing and humming before high-stakes meetings, physiological sighs for five-second resets, espresso breath (bellows) for energy, and NSDR / yoga nidra for daily restoration — all selected by context.
3. **APE as an in-meeting check-in**: Awareness (narrow vs. expanded), Posture, Emotion (including somatic sensations). Leaders can scan APE before Zooms, during tough conversations, or whenever they notice racing thoughts.
4. **Feather, brick, dump truck**: Burnout announces itself in subtle signals — morning fatigue, small reactivity, disrupted sleep — long before the health crisis. Interoception (the "sixth sense" of tracking internal state) lets leaders intervene at the feather stage.
5. **Emotional debt compounds like technical debt**: Needing wine/CBD to downshift, disproportionate reactivity, emotional flatness, being stuck in the head are signs you're accumulating allostatic load. Talk therapy alone often doesn't pay it down; somatic practices (breathwork, body-based modalities) do.
