# Sierra AI

Enterprise customer-service AI agents company co-founded by [[source-bret-taylor|Bret Taylor]] (former Salesforce co-CEO, [[openai|OpenAI]] board chair) and Clay Bavor (former Google VP). In B1 Sierra is the canonical case study for **[[outcomes-based-pricing]]** — charging per resolved customer ticket rather than per seat.

## B1 appearances

- [[source-bret-taylor]] — Bret lays out Sierra's thesis: **"agents as the new app."** Every SaaS category will be reinvented around agents that take action on the customer's behalf, and the pricing model must follow — per-outcome rather than per-seat. He gives concrete numbers on customer-service call deflection rates and how enterprise buyers think about [[outcomes-based-pricing|labor-budget vs software-budget]].
- [[source-madhavan-ramanujam]] — Madhavan (pricing expert, author of *Monetizing Innovation*) uses Sierra as the archetype for his [[outcomes-based-pricing|outcome-pricing 2x2 framework]] when advising AI startups on how to move from seats to outcomes.

## Key claims about Sierra in B1

- **Outcomes-based pricing aligns incentives.** Charging per resolved ticket means Sierra only wins when the customer wins — unlike per-seat SaaS where the vendor wins whether or not the seat produces value.
- **Agents replace apps, not features.** Bret argues the right framing is not "AI feature bolted onto CRM" but "agent as the new primary interface for an entire job function."
- **Labor budget vs software budget.** Outcomes-based pricing lets agents compete for the larger labor budget rather than the smaller software budget — unlocking 10x revenue per customer.
- **Single issue voter bias** — Bret warns against evaluating AI products on a single dimension (accuracy, speed, cost); real customer decisions are multi-variate.
