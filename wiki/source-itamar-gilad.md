# Source: Becoming evidence-guided | Itamar Gilad (Gmail, YouTube, Microsoft)

**Guest**: Itamar Gilad (PM at Google on Gmail, YouTube; now coach and author of *Evidence-Guided*)
**Published**: 2023-09-21
**Duration**: ~1h 12m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aJWSn-tz3jQ

## Executive summary

Gilad contrasts two Google projects — the failed Google+ integration (opinion-based, plan-and-execute) and the successful Gmail tabbed inbox (evidence-guided) — to argue the best product orgs balance human judgment with evidence. He walks through his GIST framework (Goals, Ideas, Steps, Tasks) plus supporting tools: metrics trees, the confidence meter, ICE scoring, and the GIST board.

## Key ideas

1. **Opinion-based vs. evidence-guided** — Google+ wasted ~1,000 people for years because leadership bet on conviction. The tabbed inbox succeeded because nobody believed in it at first, so the team was forced to validate with fake-door tests, Wizard-of-Oz prototypes, dogfooders, and ML experiments.
2. **Metrics trees + two top metrics** — North Star measures value *delivered* to users (messages sent, nights booked); top KPI measures value *captured* (revenue). Mapping the full tree below both forces org alignment and team topology.
3. **Confidence meter** — A 0-10 scale where self-conviction = 0.01, pitch deck = 0.1, peer review slightly more, competitor copying almost nothing. Real confidence only comes from fake doors, prototypes, betas, and A/B tests. Decouples confidence from impact in ICE.
4. **ICE properly used** — Impact × Confidence × Ease. Common failure: people assigning themselves 8/10 confidence based on gut. The confidence meter is the corrective.
5. **Steps layer (fake-it → test → experiment → release)** — "You can learn at a much lower cost than you think." Start cheap (assumption mapping, fake doors, Wizard of Oz, concierge) and escalate. Replaces the all-or-nothing MVP mindset.

## B6 angle (hiring, management, leadership Part 2)

Gilad's contribution is a specific anti-HIPPO operating system: he gives mid-level PMs a way to disagree with founders and execs *without politics*, by arming them with cheap evidence. Unlike [[source-ben-horowitz|Horowitz's]] "disagree and commit" or [[source-molly-graham|Graham's]] founder-led vision, Gilad tilts toward a democratized, skeptic-friendly culture — closest in spirit to [[source-dharmesh-shah|Shah]] or [[source-alisa-cohn|Cohn]] but with sharper operational frameworks (confidence meter, GIST board).
