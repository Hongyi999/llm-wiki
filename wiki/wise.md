# Wise

Cross-border money transfer fintech (formerly TransferWise). Founded by Kristo Käärmann and Taavet Hinrikus. ~16M customers, +1M/quarter, **70% word-of-mouth acquisition**. Canonical case study of mission-led + product-led growth.

## The three pillars

All derived from NPS verbatim comments read aloud company-wide:

1. **Price** — 8-10x cheaper than banks. Pushed from 0.5% → 0.35% over years.
2. **Speed** — Instant transfers; lobbied governments for regulatory primitives (eKYC license in Singapore).
3. **Ease of use** — Obsessive UX work.

## NPS as growth leverage

Invite rates roughly double per NPS bucket (6, 7-8, 9-10). Wise's NPS sits at 70 — iPhone-tier; banks average -30. This reframes NPS as a growth leverage metric, not a happiness metric.

## Canonical principles

- **You can't split-test your way to love** — Build conviction from customer research; commit to big changes without incremental ROI proof.
- **10x better is the virality threshold** — "If you entered with 5.9% pricing vs 6%, customers would use us but not talk about us."
- **Work backwards from the theoretical ideal** — Not forward from today's product.
- **Mission authenticity as multiplier** — A rebrand email with no CTA produced more signups than any campaign.

## Org

- Single global tech stack + weak local ownership — regional teams push code directly; avoids "HQ vs international" friction.
- Spotify-style squad/tribe model.
