# Google

Parent company of Search, Cloud, Gemini, and other businesses. In B1 Google appears in four sources, most prominently as the subject of Robby Stein's episode on how Search is evolving under generative AI.

## B1 appearances

- [[source-robby-stein]] — **Robby Stein is VP of Search Product at Google.** The episode is the most detailed public account of how [[answer-engine-optimization|AI Overviews and AI Mode]] were built, the "[[answer-engine-optimization|query fan-out]]" architecture, and why Google sees AI search as **expansionary** (increasing total queries) rather than cannibalizing. Prior to Google, Robby led Instagram Stories.
- [[source-aishwarya-naresh-reganti-kiriti-badam]] — Aishwarya worked on enterprise AI at **Google** (alongside OpenAI, Amazon, Databricks); the 50+ deployments draw heavily on Google customer experience.
- [[source-edwin-chen]] — Edwin references **Google's** DeepMind lineage as an example of how different research cultures produce distinct model values; the Google/Anthropic/OpenAI divergence on post-training is one of his core themes.
- [[source-bret-taylor]] — Bret was **CTO of Facebook, then CEO of Twitter, but his career began at Google** (co-creator of Google Maps). He references Google throughout as the playbook for building consumer products at scale.

## Key claims about Google in B1

- **AI search is expansionary.** Robby's data: AI Mode users increase their total search volume, they don't replace web search with chatbot search.
- **Post-training values.** Google's model training choices encode Google-specific values that are different from OpenAI's and Anthropic's (Edwin Chen).
- **Query fan-out architecture.** AI Overviews internally decompose one user query into dozens of parallel sub-queries against the index.
