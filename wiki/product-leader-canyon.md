# Product Leader Canyon

The **Product Leader Canyon** — articulated by [[source-fareed-mosavat|Fareed Mosavat]] — is the career gap between senior-PM excellence and VP/CPO altitude where many high performers stall. The canyon exists because the *skills* that got you to senior PM aren't the *job* at the next level.

## The shape of the canyon

```
 Senior PM          ── CANYON ──          VP / CPO
 (IC excellence)                       (Leverage through systems)
 • Shipping                              • Hiring & developing leaders
 • Analysis                              • Org & operating design
 • Judgement on features                 • Narrative & strategy
 • Fast-loop execution                   • Long-loop investment
```

At senior PM, success is measured in shipped features, crisp specs, and user-visible wins. At VP/CPO, success is measured in the quality of the teams you build, the clarity of the operating system, and the narrative that moves the company — few of which show up on a weekly ship log.

## Why people stall in the canyon

Most canyon-stuck PMs respond to ambiguity by doing the old job louder. They ship more. They analyze more. They meet more. But the *new* job is leverage through:

1. **Systems** — Rituals, operating rhythms, metrics, artifacts.
2. **People** — Hiring, developing, sponsoring next-generation leaders.
3. **Narrative** — Strategy, storytelling, stakeholder alignment.

Senior-PM muscle doesn't solve any of these. The canyon is a *skill reset*, not a skill extension.

## The learning-loop stretch

Fareed's companion concept: the **PM Learning Loop** lengthens dramatically as you climb:

- **IC PM**: ship → measure → learn. Days to weeks.
- **Director**: hire → develop → org outcome. Quarters.
- **VP**: strategy → investment → market outcome. Years.

PMs who can't tolerate the longer feedback loop regress to IC work and plateau. The canyon is partly a *patience* test.

## Crossing the canyon

- **Shift from feature impact to system impact**: Trade one more shipped feature for one more hire, one better ritual, one sharper narrative.
- **Find sponsors, not just mentors**: A sponsor spends political capital to place you in rooms you aren't in. A mentor gives advice in coffee chats. Careers are made by sponsors.
- **Write more, build less**: At scale, writing is how you multiply. At senior-PM altitude, you ship code; at VP altitude, you ship documents that shape thousands of decisions.
- **Hire up-level before you are up-level**: Hire someone who could replace you in a year, then move up to what only you can do.
