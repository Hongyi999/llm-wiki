# Keeper Test

The **keeper test** is [[netflix|Netflix]]'s primary performance-management mental model, popularized by Reed Hastings's culture memo and articulated in detail by CTO Elizabeth Stone.

## The question

> "If this person told me they were leaving tomorrow, would I fight to keep them?"

Managers ask this continuously — not at annual review time. Reports are encouraged to ask it back. If the answer is no, the conversation is immediate and honest: coach, re-scope, or generously part ways.

## Why it replaces performance reviews

In a high-talent-density org, continuous candor makes annual rating cycles mostly theater. Feedback has been flowing all year; the keeper test is the check that concretizes it. Exit conversations stop being surprises because the information has been shared along the way.

## Preconditions

The keeper test only works stacked on top of two other layers:

1. **High talent density** — additive hiring, personal top-of-market comp.
2. **Candor** — specific, private, continuous feedback.

Without these, keeper-test thinking turns into arbitrary firing.
