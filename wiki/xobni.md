# Xobni

Email contact management startup (acquired by Yahoo in 2013). Historically significant as the company where [[sean-ellis|Sean Ellis]] first formulated the [[sean-ellis-test|Sean Ellis Test]] — out of frustration with ambiguous debates about whether the product had reached [[product-market-fit|PMF]].
