**Duration**: 1:10:30

## Executive summary

Garrett Lord, CEO of Handshake, uncovers the economics of human-in-the-loop model training at frontier labs. By leveraging Handshake's 18M student network (500K PhDs, 3M master's students), he launched a new business pivoting from entry-level job matching to expert data labeling, hitting $50M revenue in 4 months by serving all seven frontier labs. The thesis: expertise-based human data is now the moat in AI, not low-cost labor. His company pays PhDs $100-200/hour to break models and produce research-grade training data—something low-cost generalist platforms cannot scale.

## Key ideas

1. All-seven-frontier-labs supply: Handshake now powers data creation for every major LLM lab (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, etc.), reaching $50M ARR in 4 months because they own the audience and trust.
2. From generalists to experts: the market inverted from 'draw bounding boxes in Philippines' to PhD-level reasoning—labs need domain experts (physicists, mathematicians, software engineers) to identify novel failure modes and advance research.
3. Audience moat beats recruiting at scale: competitors with 200 LinkedIn recruiters and $10M+ monthly ad spend cannot out-compete Handshake's 18M trusted profiles, 10-year brand, and university partnerships (92% of top 500 schools).
4. Human-in-the-loop for a decade: Kevin Weil (OpenAI CPO) confirmed 'the model of today is the worst you'll ever use'—implies continuous improvement requiring human experts for as long as ASI remains out of reach.
5. LTV flywheel via cohorts and quality: Handshake trains fellows in cohorts, runs post-training validation on generated data internally, sells data packages to multiple labs, retains fellows at 80%+ repeat engagement.

## Concepts & frameworks

- [[post-training-data-moat|Expertise Moat in Data]] — The only real moat in human training data is audience access and trust; frontier labs need PhDs ($100-200/hr) not clicks, making brand and university relationships the defensibility.
- **Audience as Unfair Advantage** — 18M active students + 10 years trust + university partnerships = zero CAC, 80%+ retention, 80%+ conversion—competitors with recruiters and ads cannot scale as fast or cheaply.
- **Cohort-Based Onboarding** — New fellows train together, learn via trial-and-error with instructional design, then graduate to production projects—reduces churn and ensures quality vs. dropping people into projects cold.
- **Young AI Native Advantage** — Interns and students raised on AI tools ship code on day one; one intern's first PR was afternoon he started; competitive edge is knowing how to leverage AI in research and work.
