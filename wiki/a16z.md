# Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

**Andreessen Horowitz** (a16z) is a venture capital firm with $46B+ committed capital co-founded by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz in 2009. Its design is explicit: not primarily a capital provider, but a **confidence-restoration machine** for founders.

## Operating thesis (per [[source-ben-horowitz]])

Most founder failures trace to a confidence collapse after early mistakes — not competence. Founders make expensive early mistakes, lose confidence, start hesitating, then senior hires fill the vacuum, politics erupt, and the company becomes ungovernable. The fix isn't more competence — it's rebuilding belief.

A16z's network (CEO barbecues, peer access, direct truth-telling, executive matching, operator network) is structurally oriented around compounding founder confidence so they can keep making the hard calls that most people won't like.

## Leadership principles (Ben Horowitz)

- **Run toward fear/pain** — Hesitation is the worst sin; every consequential CEO decision is between two horrible outcomes.
- **Invest in strength, not lack of weakness** — Judge people on world-class strengths; coach flaws.
- **Irrational desire** — The only valid founding motivation; money-motivated founders quit.
- **Managerial leverage** — Great execs tell *you* what to do; if you're pushing them, replace them.
- **Good PM / Bad PM** — PM is the purest training ground for CEO because it's leadership without authority.
