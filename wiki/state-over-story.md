# State Over Story (Bottom-Up Regulation)

**State over story** is Jonny Miller's shorthand for bottom-up nervous-system regulation: change the *body* first (breath, posture, movement) and the mind follows — not the other way around.

## The physiology

- **Afferent > efferent**: Roughly **4× more neural traffic flows body → brain than brain → body**. The brain continuously reads the body's state and builds the story from it.
- **Insular cortex** — Integrates interoceptive signal; affected directly by breath.
- **Parasympathetic activation** — Long exhales, humming, and vocal cord vibration shift the nervous system toward rest-and-digest.

## The toolkit

| Technique | Use case |
|-----------|----------|
| **4-4-8 box breathing** | Pre-meeting downshift |
| **Humming** | Vagus nerve stimulation, calming |
| **Physiological sigh** (double inhale + long exhale) | 5-second reset |
| **Bellows breath / breath of fire** | Energy activation |
| **NSDR / yoga nidra** | Daily restoration, sleep debt |

**Rule of thumb**: Exhale longer than inhale to downshift; inhale-emphasis to activate.

## APE as an in-meeting check

Three-axis self-check any time:

- **A**wareness — Narrow (tunnel vision) or expanded?
- **P**osture — Collapsed or open?
- **E**motion — What sensation is present? Where in the body?

## Why it matters for leaders

- **Feedback quality** — Leaders who can downshift before hard conversations give better feedback, not lectures.
- **Burnout interception** — Interoception lets you notice the "feather" signals (fatigue, reactivity, sleep) long before the "dump truck" (health crisis).
- **Emotional debt compounds** — Like technical debt. Needing wine/CBD to downshift, disproportionate reactivity, emotional flatness. Talk therapy alone often doesn't pay it down — somatic practices do.
