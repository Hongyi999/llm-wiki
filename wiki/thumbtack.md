# Thumbtack

US home-services marketplace. Case study in [[marketplace-liquidity|marketplace liquidity]] management from [[benjamin-lauzier|Benjamin Lauzier]].

## Marketplace lessons from Thumbtack

- **Hard side = quality pros** (plumbers, electricians, specialty contractors).
- **Fill rate** as the north-star operational metric — % of requests that yield a match.
- **Craigslist jumpstart**: seeded early supply from Craigslist, same playbook as Airbnb.
- **Smoke-machine cautionary tale**: a pro quoting on a smoke-machine rental had to answer 15+ qualifying questions before seeing the lead. Cognitive load killed supply response rate. The fix was to cut questions to the minimum, trading matching precision for liquidity.
