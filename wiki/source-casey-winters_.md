# Source: How to sell your ideas and rise within your company | Casey Winters, Eventbrite (Episode 2)

**Guest**: [[source-casey-winters|Casey Winters]] (ex-Eventbrite CPO; back for a second appearance as the first-ever repeat guest)
**Published**: 2022-07-21 (note: content references GPT-4, Notion AI — likely actual recording early 2023, YAML date may be legacy)
**Duration**: ~55m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6XMUDEYf2OE

## Executive summary

Casey returns after stepping back from the Eventbrite CPO seat. He coins the "zero interest rate phenomenon PM" — a generation of PMs trained by abundance to follow frameworks and lean on research/design/analyst support rather than ship, decide, and use their brains. He also delivers a masterclass on marketplace network effects, the Grubhub-vs-DoorDash disruption story, and why consumer subscription is structurally harder than investors admit.

## Key ideas

1. **Zero-interest-rate PMs** — PMs trained in FANG-style abundance freeze under uncertainty: "I can't even begin to come up with solutions until I see all the data and talk to customers." Real job is making decisions under uncertainty and shipping to learn. Frameworks are "tools in a toolkit, not a coloring book."
2. **Ship to learn, reserve research for high-leverage uncertainty** — Research is a scarce resource; burn it only on big, ill-defined problems. Redesigning a login page? Copy FANG. Casey's Eventbrite blog "On Best Practices and Breakfast Rituals" made the point internally.
3. **Founder intuition vs. team expertise, over time** — Founders with PMF have accumulated subconscious intuition; they wrongly delegate too fast to senior hires. Both sides should proactively signal: "I need direction" / "I've got it, trust me."
4. **Grubhub's disruption as cautionary tale** — Grubhub was asset-light, high-margin. DoorDash/Postmates/Uber Eats raised negative-margin rounds to build delivery networks, dramatically expanded selection, locked national chains. "During existential threats, the only rational reaction is overreaction" (Taleb). Grubhub's only play would've been acquiring DoorDash early.
5. **Three network-effect types + why consumer subscription is brutal** — Direct (WhatsApp), cross-side (Grubhub), data (Pinterest). All social networks eventually become cross-side or data NE businesses. Consumer subscription needs >60-70% annual retention, which almost no one hits (Netflix US, Prime, Spotify, Duolingo). Default paid-acquisition + freemium is mathematically doomed.

## B6 angle (hiring, management, leadership Part 2)

Casey's second appearance is more management/organizational than his first (more growth mechanics), and the "zero interest rate PM" concept is a fresh contribution to the hiring/leadership canon — a memorable diagnostic for mis-hired PMs distinct from [[source-ben-horowitz|Horowitz's]] "wartime vs. peacetime" or [[source-camille-fournier|Fournier's]] functional competence frames. His CPO-specific wisdom (company exec first, product exec second; visibly caring about sales/marketing/legal) complements B5's CEO-centric leadership material.
