# Source: Behind the product: Duolingo streaks | Jackson Shuttleworth

**Guest**: Jackson Shuttleworth (Group PM, Duolingo retention team)
**Published**: 2024-12-15
**Duration**: ~1h 28m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_CCwoQZH5hI

## Executive summary

Shuttleworth narrates the four-year, 600-experiment journey of the streak feature — the single biggest DAU driver for a $14B company with 9M+ year-plus-streak users. Deep-dive into one feature: how XP-based streaks evolved into lesson-based streaks, why making streak-extension "too easy" backfired, how streak freezes balance flexibility vs. perfection, and how Duolingo's metric-owning teams structure roadmaps around CURR (current user retention rate) as the leverage point on DAUs.

## Key ideas

1. **600 experiments on one feature, ~50% win rate** — A streak experiment every other day. ~50% shut down (above industry's ~20% typical), but the team still learns. Copy tests are especially cheap — changing "Continue" to "Commit to my goal" was a massive CURR win.
2. **Zero-to-seven-day loss aversion is the gold vein** — Retention curves show disproportionate jumps from 1→2→3... up to day 7, then flatten. Most streak experimentation targets that window. Giving new users 2 free streak freezes at start was one of the biggest wins ever.
3. **Unit-of-use must match app reality** — Moving from XP-goal to "one lesson/day" was a major DAU win; further loosening to "one exercise/day" moved DAUs zero because it captured disengaged users and destroyed the streak's meaning.
4. **Flexibility vs. perfection as competing mechanics** — Streak freezes = bend-not-break; Perfect Streak (gold, calendar marking, no tangible reward) = celebrates non-use of freezes. A "keeper of streak sanctity" (co-lead Antonia, backed by CEO Luis) protects against cheapening into a one-way-door "extinction event."
5. **Notifications: 23.5-hour + 10pm streak-saver** — Practice reminder fires 23.5 hrs after last practice (revealed > stated preference beat user-selected times). Late-night "streak saver" that would feel spammy for any other app is beloved here. Metric-owning teams + metric-focused goals = key to avoiding local-maxima feature bloat.
