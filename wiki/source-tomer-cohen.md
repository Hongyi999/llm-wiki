# Source: Why AI is disrupting traditional product management

**Guest**: Tomer Cohen

**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R-zCfLQD_84  

## Executive summary

Tomer Cohen, LinkedIn's CPO, reveals how the platform transformed from a destination nobody used to a daily staple through a radical AI-first product development approach. Starting in 2016 (before AI was cool), he reoriented product teams away from control toward enabling AI as 'the ultimate matchmaker,' rebuilt the entire feed experience around understanding what professionals actually care about, and created the 'Full Stack Builder' program to embed AI thinking at every organizational level from strategy through infrastructure. The core insight: let go of what you built and return to the underlying objectives, then solve them with AI-enabled solutions rather than bolting AI onto existing features.

## Key ideas

1. We might be wrong but we are not confused: Making decisions as a team with full clarity and alignment matters more than individual correctness; confusion in execution destroys value more than being wrong in a synchronized direction.
2. AI as the ultimate matchmaker in marketplaces: LinkedIn's biggest wins came from understanding that AI's core strength is connecting value creators with value consumers at scale.
3. Infrastructure as a product lever most PMs ignore: Changing infrastructure often produces bigger product outcome gains than shipping new buttons or features; PMs must think about ML objectives, algorithm architecture, data collection strategy, and fine-tuning.
4. Diverge then converge on AI bets: Let teams explore and experiment wildly with new AI capabilities for weeks to build understanding and excitement, then converge top-down on the highest-potential opportunities.
5. Becoming is better than being: Growth mindset is the competitive edge; continuous evolution of craft is the real north star.

## Concepts & frameworks

- [[ai-native-organization|AI-First Product Development]] — Reframing product work from 'what can we do with this technology' to 'what objectives are we solving, and how can AI solve them better'.
- **The Marketplace Matchmaker Pattern** — In two-sided markets, AI's highest-value role is connecting supply and demand efficiently.
- **Product Leader as Infrastructure Thinker** — Effective PMs must own algorithm objectives, feature sets for the model, data collection and labeling strategy, and fine-tuning plans.

## Memorable quote

> It's almost like being a chef at a restaurant — you're used to deciding every part of the dish. Then this new technology comes in and says, just give me the ingredients, give me the guidelines of how you cook, and I'll take care of it for you. For many folks, this is a very scary feeling. (Tomer Cohen)

## B1 angle

Tomer shows how embedding AI-first thinking into product strategy and organization before it was mainstream built LinkedIn's transformative feed and set the template for how enterprises develop AI products at scale.
