# Source: $1M to $10M: The enterprise sales playbook | Jen Abel

**Guest**: [[source-jen-abel-founder-led-sales|Jen Abel]] (co-founder JJELLYFISH; now GM Enterprise at State Affairs) — second Lenny appearance

## Executive summary

Where her first episode covered zero-to-$1M founder-led sales, this one is a tactical deep-dive on going $1M→$10M ARR in true enterprise sales. Provocative thesis: the mid-market doesn't exist, and founders should chase tier-1 logos (Walmart, NVIDIA, ExxonMobil) early because those incumbents are the real early adopters — they need alpha to defend their #1 position and will turn over rocks for you.

## Key ideas

1. **The mid-market is a dangerous middle** — Every deal is either upper-SMB (marketing-led) or lower-enterprise (sales-led); hybrid playbooks lose. Hire and price to one game.
2. **Tier-1 logos are the early adopters** — They have budget, procurement skill, and a need to stay #1. Lock them in before cheaper alternatives copy you.
3. **Vision-casting, not problem-selling** — Sell the gap/alpha (Mario-on-fire metaphor, "10x engineers only join Cursor shops"), not the pain. Problem-selling "kills the vibe."
4. **Land at $75K-$150K or don't bother** — $10K deals anchor you forever; a 10x jump demands 15x defendable value. Better to sell services wrapped around the product.
5. **Deals are co-authored via text** — Real enterprise closes happen relationship-first through SMS with a champion; every deal looks different ("cosplay the founder"). AI outbound tools all pull from the same databases — go through the back door manually.
