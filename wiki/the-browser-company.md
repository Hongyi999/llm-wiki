# The Browser Company

Makers of Arc, a consumer web browser. Founded by [[source-josh-miller|Josh Miller]] (CEO) and Hursh Agrawal (CTO). Canonical case study of an anti-operator, feelings-first, transparency-maximal approach to product building.

## Operating philosophy

- **Optimize for feelings, not metrics** — Feelings (surprise, joy, airiness, focus, secure) are the creative input; metrics are a fidelity check.
- **D5/D7 as single metric** — How many people use Arc 5+ days a week. Combines retention, engagement, and growth into one uncheatable number. Track cohort-over-cohort.
- **The company is the product** — Hursh and Josh started the company so they could hire whoever they wanted. Hiring announcements are product launches.
- **No PMs** — "PM" is a role on a project, not an org.
- **Assume you don't know** — Everything is a prototype, including values and org structure.

## Non-traditional orgs

- **Membership team** — Owns the full human relationship (support + success + research).
- **Storytelling team** — 3 people (ex-NBC, ex-Snap/Verge, indie filmmaker); owns PR + marketing + investor relations as one function.

## Values (from "Notes on Road Trips" essay)

1. Heartfelt intensity
2. Assume you don't know
3. Start by asking what could be
4. Swarm for the crew
5. Make them feel something

## Build-in-public

- Board meetings on YouTube. Reaction videos to competitor launches. Bet: browsers handle sensitive data; trust in tech is broken; the antidote is letting people feel they know the humans.
