# Source: Pricing your AI product: Lessons from 400+ companies and 50 unicorns | Madhavan Ramanujam

**Guest**: Madhavan Ramanujam
**Published**: 2025-07-27
**Duration**: 1:11:44  
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NR85H55eYkM  

## Executive summary

Madhavan, the world's leading pricing strategist, reveals that AI founders must tackle monetization from day one—not later—because AI products tap into labor budgets (10x larger than software budgets) and risk chronic undermonetization if they train customers early on low prices. He presents a game-changing two-by-two framework where the golden quadrant is outcome-based pricing with great attribution and autonomy; only 5% of companies inhabit this quadrant today, but he predicts 25% will within three years. He provides tactical frameworks for value selling, negotiation tactics like 'good-better-best' pricing, and POC design that co-creates ROI models with customers.

## Key ideas

1. AI founders must decide pricing strategy on day one: unlike SaaS, you cannot grow and figure out monetization later because you're anchoring expectations immediately.
2. Labor budget displacement means outcomes-based pricing is the target: if your AI handles work worth $100K in annual labor costs and you price at $20/month, you've undermonetized and trained customers to expect more for less.
3. The outcome-based pricing golden quadrant requires two things: attribution (can you prove the impact?) and autonomy (does the AI act without human intervention?)—only ~5% of AI companies are truly here today.
4. POCs must co-create ROI models, not just test technical functionality: frame them as 30-day pilots to build business cases together; charge smartly to filter tire-kickers, but clarify the POC price doesn't anchor the real deal.
5. Negotiation tactics matter hugely in B2B: show good-better-best options, use value selling with affirmation loops, taper concessions (don't keep giving more after each ask), and ask for gives in exchange for your concessions (value audits, for example).

## Concepts & frameworks

- [[outcomes-based-pricing|Outcome-Based Pricing Two-by-Two]] — Four quadrants based on attribution (high/low) and autonomy (high/low); golden quadrant is high attribution + high autonomy, enabling pricing for work delivered.
- **POC-as-Business-Case** — Reframe POCs from technical feasibility tests to 30-day partnerships co-creating ROI models and value attribution with customers.
- **Gives and Gets in Negotiation** — Every concession you give (discount, feature) must be met with a get (value audit, commitment); maintains reciprocity and prevents being beaten up.

The definitive playbook for AI product monetization: move from underpricing out of caution to confident outcome-based pricing that captures the true labor-displacement value you're delivering.
