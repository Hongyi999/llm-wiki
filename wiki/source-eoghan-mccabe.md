## Executive summary

Eoghan McCabe, CEO of Intercom, navigated a burnout exit and came back to rebuild the company for the AI era. Rather than incremental product updates, he placed a $100M bet on Fin, an agentic AI customer service agent achieving 59% autonomous resolution rates (vs. <30% competitors). He displaced ~40% of employees, rewrote culture around 'founder mode' decisiveness and AI-native work, and achieved 98-99% leadership approval post-rebuild. The broader thesis: agents will automate repetitive CX/sales/operations work, but humans' craft, beauty, and connection remain premium. Organizations flatten dramatically as humans shift from 'IC doing tickets' to 'humans overseeing agent fleet.'

## Key ideas

1. Founder mode decisive bets over committees: when returning as CEO, Eoghan cut costs, selected service as the strategic focus (abandoning $80M ARR of other products), allocated $100M to Fin, and accepted 40% workforce turnover to reshape culture.
2. Value is beauty and craft, not efficiency: as AI commoditizes operations, humans win on storytelling, imperfection, and connection; Lenny the podcaster survives while generic AI summaries approach zero value.
3. Culture as sharp knife: designed values (resilience, high standards, hard work, shareholder value) to explicitly filter departing employees; automated quarterly performance scoring (goals + values) to enable non-subjective separations.
4. Agents everywhere with human oversight: future org = humans managing agent fleets, checking edge cases, contextualizing, overseeing—not replacing humans but changing their IC roles from 'reply to ticket' to 'configure and monitor agent.'
5. AI is mandatory, not optional: 'You don't have a choice.' Disruption will be violent. Young AI-native companies work 12hrs/day leveraging AI in every task. Previous-generation companies that don't radically rebuild will lose all categories.

## Concepts & frameworks

- **Founder Mode Decisiveness** — CEO moves unilaterally on strategy, accepts organizational risk, holds moral authority to reshape company even when unpopular; contrasts with 'professional CEO' committee-based approach.
- **Human Premium in AI Age** — As AI automates operations, humans command premium for craft, beauty, story, connection, imperfection—Lenny's unique voice worth more than generic summaries; abundance of AI content destroys generalized value.
- **Culture as Retention Filter** — Explicit values + automated scoring (not subjective manager discretion) allows rapid, non-emotional employee selection; reshapes org in image of new values faster than gradual hiring.
- [[ai-agents|Agents and Human Oversight]] — Agents handle repetitive, mechanical, demeaning work; humans monitor, override, contextualize, and configure—flattens orgs, raises human job quality by removing tedium.
- [[ai-native-organization|AI Bet or Death]] — AI disruption will be violent and comprehensive. Previous-gen companies that don't radically redesign (culture, speed, tech stack) will lose all categories to young, AI-native competitors.
