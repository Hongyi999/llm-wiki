# How to build your product strategy stack | Ravi Mehta

**Guest**: Ravi Mehta
**Published**: 2023-01-19
**Duration**: 1:21:25

## Executive summary

Ravi Mehta (former CPO at Tinder, PM leader at Facebook, VP Product at Tripadvisor; now CEO of Outpace) introduces the Product Strategy Stack — a five-layer framework (mission, company strategy, product strategy, roadmap, goals) that separates commonly-conflated concepts and enables both top-down design and bottom-up debugging. His contrarian claim: goals should come *after* the roadmap, not before. He also offers the frontier-of-understanding concept (four risks determining what kind of goal to set), distinguishes velocity from latency (startups' real edge), and provides a 12-competency PM skills framework.

## Key ideas

1. **Product Strategy Stack**: Five layers — mission, company strategy, product strategy, roadmap, goals — working as a system. Work top-down to design; work bottom-up to debug why prioritization is failing.
2. **Goals after roadmap (contrarian)**: Without strategic context, teams optimize metrics that undermine the destination. Decide where you're driving before committing to 250 miles.
3. **Frontier of understanding**: Four types of risk — understanding, dependency, execution, strategic — determine what kind of goal to set. If you don't know what moves retention, goal yourself on *learning*, not retention targets.
4. **Velocity vs. latency**: Startups win on latency (idea → validated test), not velocity (sheer output). Big companies are faster cars; startups have a tighter turning radius.
5. **12 PM competencies**: Product execution, customer insight, product strategy, leadership — each subdivided into three sub-skills. A concrete career development tool.
