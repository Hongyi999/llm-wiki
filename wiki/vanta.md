# Vanta

SOC 2 compliance automation startup founded by Christina Cacioppo. Referenced by both [[todd-jackson|Todd Jackson]] and [[jen-abel|Jen Abel]] as an example of a company that reached [[product-market-fit|PMF]] unusually fast by **starting from the market, not the technology**.

## PMF story

- Christina was obsessed with talking to founders about a painful problem: passing SOC 2 audits.
- She built the first version in **spreadsheets**, manually shepherding customers through the process before writing production software.
- Because the market was identified before the product, the path to PMF was compressed — no searching for who the customer was.
- Jen Abel's framing: market-first companies reach PMF faster but may be **upside-capped** relative to technology-first bets (like Airtable or Figma) that start with an insight and search for the market.
