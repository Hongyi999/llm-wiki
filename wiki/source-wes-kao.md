# Source: Persuasive communication and managing up | Wes Kao

**Guest**: Wes Kao (co-founder altMBA with Seth Godin; co-founder Maven; creator of popular executive-communication course)
**Published**: 2022-08-28
**Duration**: ~53m 8s
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4jtGsyz4jLs

## Executive summary

Dense tactical masterclass on written and verbal communication. Communication is the highest-leverage skill for operators. Named frameworks — Sales Then Logistics, MOO (Most Obvious Objection), signposting, strategy-not-self-expression, CEDAF delegation — plus a mental model that "thinking and communicating are more intertwined than we think."

## Key ideas

1. **Sales, then logistics** — Most operators over-explain "how" before securing buy-in on "why." 30 seconds of framing unlocks engagement; switching the order kills response rates.
2. **MOO — Most Obvious Objection** — Before sending/presenting, anticipate the most obvious objection. Forces empathy with the audience and reveals gaps in your argument.
3. **Concise ≠ brief; concise = economy of words** — Bottleneck to concision is usually not knowing what your core point is. Preparation, not trimming, is the fix. "Blast radius" of sloppy message in a 15-person Slack channel is bigger than it feels.
4. **Signposting** — "For example," "because," "as a next step," "first/second/third" give readers structural cues without bolding/bullets. Overuse of formatting hides lazy thinking.
5. **Speak accurately about conviction** — Over-confident operators state hypotheses as fact; under-confident ones diminish strong recommendations. Calibrate language to actual evidence.
6. **Manage up by sharing a point of view** — Instead of "what should we do?", come with "here's what I think — where are the gaps?" Senior people manage up more, not less.
7. **CEDAF delegation framework** — Comprehension, Excitement, De-risk, Align, Feedback loop. Also a playbook for delegating to AI agents.
8. **Feedback = strategy, not self-expression** — Goal is behavior change, not being heard. Bring only the 10% that motivates change.
9. **Swipe file** — Collect smart things; capture alone trains your alertness.
