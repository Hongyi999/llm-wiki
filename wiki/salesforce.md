# Salesforce

Enterprise CRM and the canonical per-seat SaaS company. In B1 Salesforce is referenced as the incumbent whose pricing and GTM playbook the [[ai-agents|AI agents]] wave is explicitly trying to disrupt.

## B1 appearances

- [[source-bret-taylor]] — **Bret Taylor was co-CEO of Salesforce** (2021–2022) before founding [[sierra-ai|Sierra]]. He uses Salesforce repeatedly as the reference point for "the old enterprise playbook": per-seat pricing, long enterprise sales cycles, products sold by feature lists. Sierra is his deliberate counter-bet on [[outcomes-based-pricing|outcomes-based pricing]].
- [[source-jason-droege]] — Jason references Salesforce as an example of how enterprise AI adoption takes 6–12 months because legacy systems like Salesforce are where the training data and workflows live.

## B1 framing

Salesforce plays the "foil" role in B1 — every AI-agents guest defines their thesis against it. The core critique: per-seat software can't capture the value of AI that completes whole jobs, because the software is competing for the wrong budget ([[outcomes-based-pricing|software budget]]) instead of the labor budget.
