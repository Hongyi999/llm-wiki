# Jen Abel

Co-founder of [[jjellyfish|JJELLYFISH]]. Embeds alongside founders to teach enterprise sales and drive the first $1M of ARR. Jen's central claim: **founder-led sales is research, not revenue**, and is inseparable from finding [[product-market-fit|product-market fit]] in B2B.

## Key contributions

- **[[founder-led-sales|Founder-led sales]]** as a PMF-discovery discipline, not a revenue motion.
- **"The founder is the product"** in zero-to-one sales.
- **Manual 30 exercise**: before any sales tooling, find 30 prospects by hand and write each a 15–20 minute note.
- Cold outreach principle: **relevancy > personalization**, lead with a **counterintuitive insight**, focus on the problem not the solution, fit on one mobile screen.
- **Services-first sales** for markets that don't yet know how to buy the technology — 40–50% of early B2B SaaS deals need it.
- **Qualification is the whole game**: bottom-of-funnel problems are always top-of-funnel problems in disguise.

## Conviction lines

- "Everyone says they have a bottom-of-funnel problem. It's never a bottom-of-funnel problem."
- "Sales is supposed to feel fun for the buyer."
- Don't hire any salespeople until Series A — seed is experimentation.
