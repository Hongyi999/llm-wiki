# Lookout

Mobile security product. Case study from [[sean-ellis|Sean Ellis]] in how **repositioning** can move the [[sean-ellis-test|Sean Ellis Test]] number.

## PMF turnaround

- Initial survey: **7% "very disappointed"** — nowhere near the 40% threshold.
- Original positioning was antivirus (crowded, commoditized).
- Sean's drill-down question on "main benefit" revealed that users actually valued **lost-phone recovery** more than the security features.
- Re-positioning around find-my-phone moved the number above 40% and unlocked sustainable growth.

The lesson: PMF is often about **which benefit you lead with**, not which features you ship.
