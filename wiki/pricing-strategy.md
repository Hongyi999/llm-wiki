Landscape page for pricing and monetization frameworks across the wiki. Pricing is a product decision, not a finance decision — it shapes adoption, competitive positioning, and long-term growth trajectories.

## Key pricing lessons from B9

1. **Pricing protects growth engines** — Figma's pivot from 3-collaborator to 3-file limit preserved viral spread. Pricing that limits sharing kills bottom-up adoption.
2. **Day-one monetization for AI** — AI startups that discount early permanently anchor low. Establish willingness-to-pay from the start.
3. **Viewers free, editors paid** — Figma's model: only charge the people who create, never the people who consume. Maximizes adoption surface.
4. **Simple enough for customers to explain** — If prospects can't articulate your pricing, it's too complex.
