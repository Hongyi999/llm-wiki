# Opendoor

iBuying real estate platform. Brian Tolkin (employee #100, VP Product) describes Opendoor's 'twin turbine' model — a product-operations company where the product builds technology and operations handle the physical world — and how Jobs-to-be-Done research revealed that sellers hired Opendoor for certainty, not speed.
