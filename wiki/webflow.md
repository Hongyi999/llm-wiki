# Webflow

No-code website and visual development platform. In B1 Webflow is referenced by two growth-focused guests as a model case study for [[product-velocity-ai-era|product-led growth]] in the AI era.

## B1 appearances

- [[source-julian-shapiro]] — Julian uses Webflow as a [[product-velocity-ai-era|product-led acquisition]] case study: users build sites publicly, those sites become acquisition channels for new users. This is his "building state" / "settlement debt" pattern for embedding growth into the product itself.
- [[source-ethan-smith]] — Ethan (AEO growth expert) references Webflow in the context of [[answer-engine-optimization|content strategy for LLMs]] — how the websites built on Webflow are now competing for visibility inside AI answers, not just Google's blue links.
