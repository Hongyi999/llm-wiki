# LinkedIn's product evolution and the art of building complex systems | Hari Srinivasan

**Guest**: Hari Srinivasan
**Published**: 2023-07-16

## Executive summary

Hari Srinivasan, VP of Product for LinkedIn Talent Solutions, describes how [[linkedin|LinkedIn]] builds and maintains its interconnected ecosystem of hiring, learning, feed, and premium marketplaces. His core operational insight is that complex, multi-sided systems demand explicit decision frameworks (RAPID, the 5-day escalation rule) and a visible North Star that can actually resolve product-review debates in real time. He also describes the post-COVID shift to skills-first hiring — a structural reframe that is still rippling through the labor market — and shares his PM skills triangle as a development model for product careers.

## Key ideas

1. **Skills-first hiring**: COVID exposed that title-based matching prevented marketplace rebalancing. LinkedIn now has 47% of recruiters explicitly searching by skills. The structural shift is slow but real.
2. **North Star as operational mechanism**: "Connecting people to economic opportunity" is not aspirational wall art — it's the tiebreaker used in every product review. Hari's career-defining moment was a failing review that pivoted once he reframed everything through this lens.
3. **RAPID + 5-day escalation**: In interconnected systems, decision paralysis kills more than bad decisions. RAPID assigns a single decision-maker; the 5-day rule forces unresolved debates to escalate automatically.
4. **PM Skills Triangle**: Creator/storyteller, data scientist, and general manager. The best PMs live on the edges playing to their strength, not at the center trying to be balanced.
5. **Systems-thinking as hiring signal**: Hari's favorite interview prompt is "what's the most complex thing you ever built?" He looks for candidates who both gravitate toward hard problems *and* simplify them for others.
