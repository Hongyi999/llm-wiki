# Source: How we restructured Airtable's entire org for AI | Howie Liu (co-founder and CEO)

**Guest**: Howie Liu
**Published**: 2025-08-31
**Duration**: 1:40:42  
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GT0jtVjRy2E  

## Executive summary

Howie Liu argues that every software company must be 'refounded' in the AI era because AI evolves so rapidly that new model releases demand continuous reinvention of product UX and business model. To stay ahead, he's shifted into what he calls the 'IC CEO' mode—getting hands dirty coding, testing Claude and ChatGPT hourly, spending hundreds of dollars on AI inference to extract insights from sales transcripts. He restructured Airtable into two groups (fast thinking for AI exploration, slow thinking for stability), eliminated standing one-on-ones in favor of timely topic-driven meetings, and emphasized that to be the 'chief taste maker,' you must 'taste the soup' yourself—playing with raw models and capabilities, not just reviewing screenshots.

## Key ideas

1. Every software product in the AI era must be refounded; AI paradigm shifts as fast as individual model releases, requiring continuous reinvention of UX and business model, not one-time adaptation like mobile-to-cloud.
2. IC CEOs (CEOs as individual contributors) coding and using AI tools hourly are the signal of AI success; you can't optimize product and taste without being in the details—playing with models directly, not delegating to reports.
3. Airtable restructured into 'fast thinking' (AI exploration, rapid iteration) and 'slow thinking' (stability, performance) groups, allowing half the company to move at startup speed on AI while protecting core product.
4. Spending hundreds of dollars on AI inference for high-value tasks (e.g., mapping sales call insights) is trivial versus the strategic value; more leaders should aggressively throw compute at problems that would cost consultants millions.
5. Cut standing one-on-ones and replace with timely, insight-driven meetings; constrain calendar to force focus on high-impact opportunities discovered real-time, not ritual check-ins.

## Concepts & frameworks

- [[ai-native-organization|Product Refounding in AI Era]] — Unlike mobile or cloud shifts, AI paradigm shifts with each model release; every software company must continuously reinvent UX and business model, not achieve one-time fit.
- **IC CEO (Individual Contributor CEO)** — CEOs getting back into code, daily AI tool usage (ChatGPT, Claude), and hands-on product work to understand possibilities and set taste; delegation precludes deep understanding of what's possible.
- **Fast Thinking / Slow Thinking Split** — Organizational structure with one group exploring AI at startup speed (fast) and another protecting stability/performance (slow); decouples innovation velocity from product stability.
- **Timely Over Ritual Meetings** — Replace standing one-on-ones with topic-driven meetings triggered by real discoveries or insights; constraint forces focus on high-impact conversations, not habit-based synchronization.

## Memorable quote

> To really understand the solution space of what's possible, you have to be in the details. You can't just look at screenshots or a pre-recorded video of a new product feature. AI is something you have to play with.

## B1 angle

Liu's IC CEO model and fast/slow org split are a blueprint for mature products pivoting to AI; shows that sustained competitiveness requires continuous product refounding, not one-time retooling.
