# Rahul Vohra

Founder and CEO of [[superhuman|Superhuman]]. Previously founded Rapportive (sold to LinkedIn). Best known for operationalizing [[product-market-fit|product-market fit]] into a repeatable engine.

## Key contributions

- **[[superhuman-pmf-engine|Superhuman PMF Engine]]** — a four-step process that turned the [[sean-ellis-test|Sean Ellis Test]] into a roadmap-driving loop. Took Superhuman from 22% → 58% "very disappointed."
- **Solution deepening vs. market widening** framing — see [[solution-deepening-vs-market-widening]].
- **Positioning-first pricing**: applied Van Westendorp to land Superhuman at $30/month.
- **Manual concierge onboarding**: every new user got a 1:1 session from a team of ~20 onboarders.
- **Game design over gamification**: mechanics borrowed from real games, not Duolingo-style badges.

## Operating philosophy

- **Single Decisive Reason** (from Reid Hoffman) for hard decisions.
- **Switch Log**: personal context-switch journal.
- **TM meditation** twice daily.
- Hired a president to collapse 8 direct reports into 2.
