# Sean Ellis

Growth marketer and author. Coined the term **"growth hacker"** and created the [[sean-ellis-test|Sean Ellis Test]] for measuring [[product-market-fit|product-market fit]].

## Key contributions

- **[[sean-ellis-test|Sean Ellis Test]]**: the "how disappointed would you be if you could no longer use this product?" survey. The **40%+ very disappointed** threshold became the de facto industry benchmark for PMF.
- **PMFsurvey.com**: free tool for founders to run the test.
- **Growth hacking** as a discipline: reframing marketing as engineering-style experimentation.
- **ICE prioritization** (Impact, Confidence, Ease) for ranking growth experiments.
- Author of *Hacking Growth*.

## Operating history

- First growth lead at [[dropbox|Dropbox]] — helped design the double-sided referral program.
- Early growth work at [[logmein|LogMeIn]] (unlocked activation from ~5% to ~50%), [[xobni|Xobni]] (where the test was born), and [[lookout|Lookout]] (rode the test from 7% to above the threshold).
