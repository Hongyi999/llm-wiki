# Benjamin Lauzier

Marketplace PM and educator. Ex-[[lyft|Lyft]], ex-[[thumbtack|Thumbtack]]. Currently building Nurra Health (nurra.me) for chronic care navigation. Teaches the Reforge marketplace course.

## Key contributions

- **Two PMFs per marketplace**: supply-side PMF and demand-side PMF must be reached separately. See [[marketplace-liquidity]].
- **Pick the hard side** (usually supply) and build **single-player mode** value for it before expecting the other side to show up.
- **Market Health Metric** at Lyft: ETA threshold as the magical-feeling signal.
- **Fill rate** as the operational proxy for liquidity at [[thumbtack|Thumbtack]].

## Case notes

- Lyft rental-car partnership with GM → 4th-largest rental fleet in the US, supply unlock.
- Lyft **mentor/ambassador** program: $35/session + $20/recruit, peer-to-peer driver onboarding.
- [[thumbtack|Thumbtack]] smoke-machine example: 15+ qualifying questions killed pro response rate — cognitive load as liquidity killer.
- Sidecar failed by over-controlling pricing and matching, suffocating supply agency.
