# Source: The AI-native startup: 5 products, 7-figure revenue, 100% AI-written code. | Dan Shipper (Every)

## Executive summary

Dan Shipper runs Every, a 15-person company that has shipped 5 products, publishes a daily newsletter, and operates a 7-figure consulting arm—all with zero hand-written code. He describes a radical AI-first operating model: a dedicated 'Head of AI Operations' builds prompts and automations for the entire team; engineers use Claude Code and agents instead of writing code; even editorial uses AI to maintain quality faster. Dan's big insight: the one company predictor for AI success is a founder who continuously experiments and doesn't fear looking stupid in front of the team. He believes AI-first organizations will feel like the future not because the tech is magical, but because every person has agency multiplied by 100x.

## Key ideas

1. Hire a Head of AI Operations on day one: This person sits weekly with the CEO, identifies repetitive work, and builds prompts/automations so teams adopt efficiency without fighting fires.
2. Engineers don't hand-write code anymore at Every: They use Claude Code and agents to define requirements and ship. The limiting factor is no longer prototype speed—it's building increasingly robust, maintainable complex systems.
3. Claude Code (and similar CLI tools) are wildly underrated for non-coders: You can feed it War and Peace, your meeting notes, or a folder of files, and it reads autonomously, creates to-do lists, and processes everything without stuffing it all in context.
4. AI may be the biggest reshoring force, not a job killer: Cheap intelligence lets small US companies afford services (legal, customer support) previously only available to large enterprises, and AI-leveraged workers are more valuable onshore than offshore.
5. The one predictor of AI success is founder comfort with experimentation and incompetence: Teams where the founder models trying things, failing, and learning normalize risk-taking and beat teams where the founder needs to look smart.

## Concepts & frameworks

- [[ai-native-organization|Head of AI Operations Role]] — Dedicated person (not scattered across managers) who builds and maintains prompts, workflows, and automations so teams can adopt AI frictionlessly without context-switching.
- [[product-velocity-ai-era|Vibe Checks]] — Qualitative testing by actual users doing real work, capturing subjective feel and usability beyond benchmarks; the best writing on AI tools comes from people building with them daily.
- **Claude Code for Non-Coders** — CLI tool that processes large document sets autonomously without stuffing context; can read files, create internal notebooks, and complete multi-turn analysis.
- **AI as Reshoring Leverage** — Cheap intelligence multiplies the leverage of high-wage workers, making onshore labor more economical than offshore for customer-facing roles and enabling small companies to compete with big ones.
