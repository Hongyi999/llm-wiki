## Executive summary

Robby Stein, VP of Product at Google Search, explains Google's AI resurgence through three products: AI Overviews (quick answers), Google Lens (visual search growing 70% YoY), and AI Mode (conversational frontier experience). He reveals that AI is expansionary rather than cannibalistic—people ask more questions now, not fewer. Success comes from embodying relentless improvement: being dissatisfied with status quo and making measurable two-move increments that compound into tipping points.

## Key ideas

1. AI search is expansionary: people ask more questions across visual, complex reasoning, and advice domains because AI unlocks new use cases (not replacement)
2. Google Lens is 70% year-over-year growth with billions of searches; multimodal AI (camera + text) represents the next frontier beyond keyword search
3. AI Mode connects all of Google's data (50B products, 250M places, finance, the entire web) into one conversational interface powered by state-of-the-art models
4. Embodying relentless improvement means being the harshest critic of your own work and always asking 'why,' then making two-move improvements that compound
5. Successful AI product adoption requires making interfaces natural and human-like; people prefer asking natural language questions over prompt engineering or fine-tuning

## Concepts & frameworks

- **Expansionary AI** — AI unlocks new question categories and use cases rather than replacing existing search; growth comes from new curiosity being fulfilled
- [[product-velocity-ai-era|Relentless improvement]] — Being the physical manifestation of relentlessness + making-things-better; never content, always the harshest critic of own work
- **Query fan-out** — AI model uses Google search as a tool, expanding a single query into dozens of sub-queries to gather context before answering
- **Natural language search** — Returning from keyword optimization to conversational questions; Ask Jeeves' vision now viable with better AI models

## Memorable quote

> You have to be the physical manifestation of two pieces of things. One is just relentlessness, just complete effort that is always exerted in a direction of positive productivity. And then the second is make things better. You have to always make things better, you're never content. — Robby Stein
