# Behind the scenes of Calendly's rapid growth | Annie Pearl (CPO)

**Guest**: Annie Pearl
**Published**: 2023-02-26
**Duration**: 1:01:49
**YouTube**: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-tUIGpgmsZw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-tUIGpgmsZw)

## Executive summary

Annie Pearl, CPO at Calendly, describes how a tool for solo users evolved into a team/enterprise platform while 70% of signups still arrive via the viral loop of receiving a Calendly link. She anchors Calendly's prioritization in [[strategy-choice-cascade|Playing to Win]] (winning aspiration, where to play, how to win), runs horizon-based resource allocation, and details the explicit PLG → SLG transition that added 20% of ARR via a sales motion carefully matched to the inbound persona. The episode is dense with team rituals (OPA, competitive war gaming) and practical career advice on breaking into PM.

## Key ideas

1. **Product strategy as integrated choices ([[strategy-choice-cascade|Playing to Win]])**: What's your winning aspiration? Where will you play (markets, segments, personas)? How will you win? This clarity is the foundation of all prioritization.
2. **Horizon-based resource allocation**: Year 1: 70% Horizon 1 / 30% H2 / 0% H3. Year 2: 50/50. Year 3: 30/60/10. Deliberately shift investment as strategy matures.
3. **PLG → Sales-led transition**: Calendly went from 99% PLG ARR to 20% sales-sourced. Early sales hires must match the inbound motion and the real buyer persona (department heads, not CIOs), not heavy-outbound hunters.
4. **Viral loop as growth engine**: 70% of Calendly signups come from receiving a scheduling link. First 10 users came from CSMs at a shared dev firm.
5. **Focus Wisely**: Saying no is hard. Calendly narrowed target personas to sales, recruiting, and customer success teams — enabling teams to build amazing products for specific users rather than average products for everyone.
