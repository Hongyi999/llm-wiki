# Mercor

AI data-labeling startup founded by [[source-brendan-foody|Brendan Foody]] that supplies PhD-level and expert-judgment labelers to [[openai|OpenAI]], [[anthropic|Anthropic]], [[meta|Meta]], and other frontier labs. In B1 Mercor is the canonical "post-[[scale-ai|Scale AI]]" startup — built explicitly for the [[post-training-data-moat|post-training data market]].

## B1 role

[[source-brendan-foody]] is the definitive episode. Brendan describes Mercor's growth from **$1M → $500M ARR in 17 months**, driven by a recognition that:

- Frontier labs' demand shifted from commodity labeling (image bounding boxes) to expert-level judgment tasks (grading PhD-level answers, writing test questions, doing complex evals).
- [[scale-ai|Scale AI]] was built for the previous era; the new work requires matching labelers with PhD backgrounds to lab demand.
- **Evals as PRD and marketing** — Brendan argues the eval set is both the product spec and the go-to-market pitch in frontier AI.
- **Ridiculous goals as culture** — public impossible-sounding targets as a recruiting and focus mechanism.

## Key themes

- **[[post-training-data-moat|Post-training is the moat]]** and expert human data is the raw material.
- **Evals drive everything** — see [[ai-evals]].
- **Speed of matching** is the defensible skill: connect frontier demand with expert supply faster than anyone else.
