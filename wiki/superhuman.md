# Superhuman

Premium email client founded by [[rahul-vohra|Rahul Vohra]]. Famous for its $30/month price, its speed-first positioning, and for operationalizing [[sean-ellis-test|Sean Ellis's PMF survey]] into a roadmap-driving engine.

## PMF journey

- Started at **22% "very disappointed"** — below Sean Ellis's 40% threshold.
- Built the [[superhuman-pmf-engine|Superhuman PMF Engine]] — a four-step survey/segment/analyze/deepen loop.
- Reached **58% "very disappointed"** in about a year.

## Defining choices

- **Positioning**: "the fastest email experience ever made." Applied April Dunford's *Obviously Awesome*.
- **Pricing**: $30/month, landed via Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter. Positioning before pricing.
- **Onboarding**: 1:1 concierge sessions by a ~20-person team. Expensive, un-scalable, intentional.
- **Design**: custom Adelle Sans typeface; game design over gamification.
- **Growth**: word of mouth, not engineered virality. (Elliot Shmukler: virality ≤ word of mouth.)
- **Expansion**: from Gmail-only to Outlook to unlock enterprise; AI features layered on the speed promise.
