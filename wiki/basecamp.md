# Basecamp

Project management tool built by 37signals. Ryan Singer, Head of Strategy at 37signals, created the Shape Up methodology at Basecamp — a radical alternative to Scrum that uses fixed-time appetites, collaborative shaping, and circuit breakers instead of sprints, estimates, and backlogs.

- [[source-ryan-singer|Ryan Singer]] — Software company (makers of Basecamp and HEY) where Shape Up was developed over nearly two decades.
