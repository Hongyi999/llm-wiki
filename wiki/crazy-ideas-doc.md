# Crazy Ideas Doc

The **Crazy Ideas doc** is an annual innovation ritual at Retool, described by [[source-eeke-de-milliano|Eeke de Milliano]]. A concrete, copyable artifact for keeping a scaling organization willing to think past the current roadmap.

## How it works

1. **Once a year (January)**, David Hsu (Retool CEO) sends out a blank doc titled "Crazy Ideas."
2. **Everyone contributes** — however crazy, however out of scope.
3. **Implicit contract**: "90% chance these shouldn't happen; 10% chance they're 10-100x for the business."
4. **The team ships 3-8 items per year** from the doc.

## Why it works

- **Permission to think** — Most teams aren't innovative because (a) fear of failure, (b) urgent work crowds out important, (c) thinking big is hard. The Crazy Ideas doc addresses all three by explicitly pre-approving bad ideas.
- **No cost to contribute** — A line in a doc doesn't commit anyone.
- **CEO-owned ritual** — Signal that big thinking comes from the top and is an annual calendar event, not a permission asymmetry.
- **Concrete pipeline to shipping** — 3-8/year emerged means 2/3 of 2022's three product launches (Workflows, Mobile, Database) originated there.

## Companion artifacts at Retool

- **"Think Bigger" section in every team charter** — "With 20% more time, what would you do that isn't on this list?" Forces planning past current headcount.
- **Internal-startup model** — 1 engineer + 1 PM/designer for first 6 months; treat new products as startups inside the company. Retool Mobile ended up with a different target customer than core web builder — only realized *because* team was isolated.
