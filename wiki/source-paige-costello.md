# Source: How to ask the right questions, project confidence, and win over skeptics | Paige Costello (Asana)

**Guest**: Paige Costello (Head of Core Product at Asana; ex-Intercom, ex-Intuit)
**Published**: 2023-07-09
**Duration**: ~1h 3m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N7S6nSmOsUI

## Executive summary

Costello is known for being "the youngest person in the room" while leading more experienced people. She shares how she wins trust (bring the insight, not the authority), how she coaches PMs by modeling rather than instructing, how Asana's Double Diamond planning and review cadence works, and the Conscious Leadership mental models (above/below the line, "how might the opposite be true") that shape her leadership.

## Key ideas

1. **Bring the insight, not the authority** — "Know thy customer. Know thy market. Know thy competitors. Know thy numbers. Know thy product." Anne Raimondi's trust equation: (credibility + reliability + authenticity) / perception of self-interest. Youngest-in-the-room wins by being curious, vulnerable, data-grounded.
2. **Double Diamond + 6-month rolling 12-month planning** — Asana plans every 6 months for a rolling 12 months. Reviews mapped to inflection points on the Double Diamond: kickoff, customer/direction selection, design concept, spec, design crit, launch.
3. **Three Es of career growth** — Experience, Exposure, Education. Most people over-index on education and under-index on exposure.
4. **"How might the opposite be true?" + above/below the line** — From Conscious Leadership. Challenges false trade-offs and scarcity mindset. Above the line = curious, learning, open; below the line = committed to winning, being right, black-and-white.
5. **"Always answer the question they should have asked"** — Advice from an Intuit BU leader: achievers answer the literal question; leaders answer the bigger-picture question the asker didn't know to ask.
6. **Feedback as Situation-Behavior-Impact + no more than 3 reviews / 10 people per meeting** — Intuit's SBI formula plus Asana's operational hack: one blocking approver, three reviews max, 10 people max per meeting.

Paige adds a distinctly *coaching-first*, gentle-authority voice.
