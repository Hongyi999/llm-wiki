# Source: An inside look at Figma's unique GTM motion | Claire Butler

**Guest**: Claire Butler (Figma's 10th employee, first GTM hire)
**Published**: 2023-09-07
**Duration**: ~1h 31m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UmirRfy-gzA

## Executive summary

Bottom-up motion Figma built from stealth through IPO-scale: **Step 1 — get ICs to love you; Step 2 — enable them to spread it inside their orgs**. Figma went ~3 years with no sales team, then hired their first sales rep on the same day as their first designer advocate (DA) — creating the "Tom Factor" of technical-practitioner-driven sales. Pricing pivoted from 3-collaborator-limit to 3-file-limit once they realized the first model was strangling their growth engine.

## Key ideas

1. **Two-step motion** — (1) Get ICs to love you via credibility + co-building + authenticity. (2) Help them spread it via frictionless free tier + designer advocates in sales + exploit operational blocker (design systems).
2. **Designers have a high BS meter** — No marketer-speak, no "collaboration/efficiency" buzzwords. Deeply technical content (grid systems posts, Vector Networks engineering deep-dives on Hacker News).
3. **Designer Advocate (DA) function is the magic dust** — First DA hired before any other marketing hire; first sales rep hired same day as next DA. DAs are ex-practitioners, never carry quota, live outside sales team. "Tom Factor" drove massively higher close rates on calls Tom joined.
4. **Pricing as growth-engine protection** — Pivoted starter tier from 3-collaborator-max-with-unlimited-files → unlimited-collaborators-with-3-file-max because first version cut off viral spread. **Viewers always free; only editors paid.**
5. **Do things that don't scale aggressively** — Drove to Palo Alto to fix a designer's MacBook for first-ever customer Coda. Dylan DMed designers in shared Lyfts.
6. **Twitter as designer watering hole** — Dylan built a custom social-graph scraper to cluster design influencers and super-spreaders.
7. **Design systems = operational wedge** that converts Pro → Org/Enterprise. Biggest early blocker became biggest upsell driver.
8. **Signal over metrics at small N** — "You can't optimize your way to product market fit."
9. **Transparency at scale** — Public postmortems; during Adobe acquisition announcement, open Twitter Space with Dylan + CTO to let users ask anything.

## B9 angle

Highest-signal GTM episode in B9. Unique contributions: (1) Microsoft came to Figma asking to pay properly because the node graph had spread out of their control — shows zero-sales-team-for-3-years can produce enterprise scale. (2) Pricing lever explicit: files-limit-instead-of-collaborator-limit. (3) **Practitioner-advocate role (no quota, not on sales team)** is underdocumented in canonical playbooks. Strong complement to [[source-april-dunford|Dunford's]] "arm the champion" — Figma's version amplifies the champion externally.
