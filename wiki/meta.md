# Meta

Parent company of Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp; maker of Llama open-weight models. In B1 Meta appears as both an [[ai-native-organization|AI-era product company]] (Julie Zhuo's history) and as one of the frontier labs buying [[post-training-data-moat|expert data]] (Jason Droege on Scale/Handshake).

## B1 appearances

- [[source-julie-zhuo]] — **Julie Zhuo led Facebook Design** for over a decade. In this episode she reflects on how management must change in the AI era: the role is less about coordinating humans and more about [[ai-native-organization|orchestrating AI as teammates]]. Meta is her reference point for how big product orgs used to operate.
- [[source-jason-droege]] — Jason lists Meta alongside [[openai|OpenAI]] and [[anthropic|Anthropic]] as the frontier labs buying expert-labeler hours from Handshake and Scale; Meta's Llama post-training pipeline is a major customer of the expert-labeling market.
