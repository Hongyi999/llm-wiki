# Source: Behind the product: Replit | Amjad Masad (co-founder and CEO)

## Executive summary

Amjad Masad, co-founder and CEO of Replit, demonstrates how AI-powered coding tools are fundamentally changing who can build software and how. Through a live demo, he shows a complete full-stack application being built from a natural language prompt in under 10 minutes at roughly 15 cents of compute cost — work that would take a skilled engineer days. Masad argues that Replit's end-to-end platform (covering IDE, runtime, deployment, and AI agent) is democratizing software creation for non-technical users including PMs, founders, and operations teams. He introduces "Amjad's Law" — the ROI of learning to code is doubling every six months — and predicts that within five years, someone could run a billion-dollar company with zero employees, with AI handling development, support, and maintenance.

## Key ideas

1. **AI Computer Interfaces (ACI)**: Replit has built a computer specifically designed for AI agents to use, distinct from human interfaces. LLMs receive text-based representations of the shell, editor feedback on errors, and tool access rather than processing visual interfaces — creating a new discipline analogous to HCI but optimized for how AI "thinks."
2. **Amjad's Law — ROI of coding doubles every 6 months**: The return on investment for learning basic coding skills is compounding rapidly. You don't need deep CS knowledge — understanding app structure, prompting, and debugging lets you build increasingly complete products as AI capabilities improve on a roughly six-month cadence.
3. **Jevons' Paradox applied to software**: As the cost of creating software plummets, total software production will explode rather than contract. Non-technical people are already building tools they previously would have hired developers for, and the lower activation energy means ideas that would never have been worth pursuing now get built.
4. **Idea generation as the new bottleneck**: With AI removing the production bottleneck, the limiting factor shifts from engineering capacity to the speed at which you can generate, test, and iterate on ideas. PMs and founders should focus on being more generative and creative rather than on traditional technical execution skills.
5. **Kill your roadmap — embrace AI-driven agility**: In an era where foundational model capabilities drop unpredictably, rigid roadmaps are liabilities. Replit slaughtered its roadmap when Anthropic released computer use capabilities, immediately building around it. Companies must be fluid, break down silos between design/PM/engineering, and react to new AI capabilities within days.

## Concepts & frameworks

- **AI Computer Interfaces (ACI)** — A new discipline of designing tool interfaces optimized for LLM agents rather than humans, using text representations instead of visual UIs.
- [[jevons-paradox-software|Amjad's Law]] — The principle that the ROI of learning to code doubles every six months as AI coding tools improve.
- [[jevons-paradox-software|Jevons' Paradox]] — When the cost of something decreases, total consumption increases — applied here to software creation as AI makes building cheaper.
- **Society of Models** — The architectural pattern where products are built from many specialized AI models (coding, critique, embeddings) rather than a single monolithic model.
- [[ai-coding-tools|AI-native coding]] — A fundamentally different approach to programming that skips traditional CS foundations and focuses on prompting, app structure, and debugging.
