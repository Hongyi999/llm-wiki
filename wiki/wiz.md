# Wiz

Cloud security startup founded in 2020 by former Microsoft Azure security team. Reached $100M ARR in ~18 months — the fastest in SaaS history at that point. Acquired by Google for $32B (2024), the largest acquisition in Google's history.

## Wiki appearances

- [[source-raaz-herzberg]] — CMO and VP Product Raaz Herzberg on building the fastest-growing startup in history: PLG + sales-led hybrid, community-led growth, and "design for wow" product principles.

## Key themes

- **Hypergrowth GTM**: Combined PLG (free tier for individual security practitioners) with aggressive enterprise sales. Both motions ran simultaneously from early on.
- **Community-led growth**: Built security community and practitioner credibility before pushing product.
- **Product-led with sales assist**: Free individual scans → team adoption → enterprise contract pipeline.
