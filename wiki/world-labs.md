# World Labs

AI research and product company founded by [[source-dr-fei-fei-li|Dr. Fei-Fei Li]] to build **spatial intelligence** and **world models** — generative 3D environments that can be navigated, manipulated, and reasoned about, as opposed to video that can only be watched. In B1 World Labs is the canonical representative of the "next-frontier-beyond-language-models" thesis.

## B1 role

[[source-dr-fei-fei-li]] is the definitive episode. Fei-Fei (ImageNet creator, Stanford HAI co-founder) argues language models have captured much of what text data can teach, but the next leap requires **3D/4D spatial understanding** that videos and LLMs fundamentally cannot provide. World Labs' first consumer product is:

- **Marble** — prompt-to-world generation for VFX, game design, robotics training, even therapy applications. marble.worldlabs.ai

## Key themes

- **[[spatial-intelligence|World models ≠ video]]** — videos predict pixels; world models enable reasoning, interaction, and navigation.
- **The [[post-training-data-moat|data-architecture-compute trio]]** that powered ImageNet → LLMs also applies to spatial AI, but requires different data (synthetic, teleop) and architecture.
- **Bitter-lesson limits.** Unlike language, robotics can't be solved by scaling data alone — physical embodiment introduces irreducible complexity.
- **Human-centered AI.** Ordinary people (artists, farmers, nurses) must retain agency as AI scales.
