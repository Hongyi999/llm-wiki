# LinkedIn

Professional networking platform owned by Microsoft. In B1 LinkedIn appears as a case study for [[ai-native-organization|organizational transformation to an AI-first product company]], led by CPO [[source-tomer-cohen|Tomer Cohen]].

## B1 role

[[source-tomer-cohen]] is the definitive episode. Starting in 2016 (pre-ChatGPT), Tomer reoriented LinkedIn's product teams away from feature ownership toward AI as "**the ultimate matchmaker**" — reframing every product decision as "what objective is this solving, and how can AI solve it better?" The feed rebuild became the canonical example of [[ai-native-organization|AI-first product development]]: instead of shipping new UI, the biggest wins came from changing the underlying ML objectives.

He also created the **Full Stack Builder** program — an internal curriculum that trains PMs to own algorithm objectives, data collection strategy, and fine-tuning plans, not just UI specs.

## Key themes

- **AI as matchmaker in marketplaces** — LinkedIn's core economic transaction is connecting value creators with value consumers; AI's best leverage is on the match itself.
- **Infrastructure as product lever** — changing infrastructure often produces bigger outcome gains than new features.
- **Clarity over correctness culture** — "we might be wrong, but we are not confused."
- **Becoming is better than being** — continuous evolution of craft over mastery claims.
