# GROW Model

**GROW** is a coaching conversation framework popularized by Sir John Whitmore and used by executive coach Rachel Lockett as the default structure for manager one-on-ones.

## The four stages

1. **Goal** — What does the person want? ("What does success look like in six months?")
2. **Reality** — Where are they now? ("What have you tried? What's working?")
3. **Options** — What paths are available? ("What could you do? What else?")
4. **Way forward** — What will they actually commit to? ("What's the next step? By when?")

## Why managers should coach, not advise

Rachel's rule (see [[source-rachel-lockett]]): advise only when you know the right answer, it's urgent, or the person lacks the skill. Otherwise, coach through GROW.

**The trap of always advising**: "When you try to advise and have the answer all the time, you're not actually equipping your team to go solve the hard problems. You're training your team to come to you with all of the hard problems."

- **[[source-rachel-lockett|Three levels of listening]]** (internal / focused / global) — GROW works better when the coach is at level 3.
