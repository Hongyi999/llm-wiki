# Build better products with continuous product discovery | Teresa Torres

**Guest**: Teresa Torres
**Published**: 2022-10-06
**Duration**: 48:18
**YouTube**: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RFaz9ZBXpk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9RFaz9ZBXpk)

## Executive summary

Teresa Torres, author of *Continuous Discovery Habits*, is the most influential voice in modern product discovery. She argues that discovery is not a phase but a weekly habit — one that lives inside the Product Trio (PM, designer, engineer) and is structured by an Opportunity Solution Tree. Her core insight is that most "customer research" fails because PMs ask about solutions instead of behaviors. Story-based interviewing — asking for concrete past stories, not opinions — unlocks the truth that surveys and opinion interviews miss.

## Key ideas

1. **Continuous discovery is a weekly cadence, not a phase**: Interview at least one customer every week. The cadence matters more than the depth — consistency builds intuition that bursts of research can't.
2. **Opportunity Solution Tree**: A single tree maps desired outcome → opportunities (customer needs/pain points) → solutions → assumption tests. It forces PMs to consider multiple opportunities and multiple solutions per opportunity, not the first one that comes to mind.
3. **Product Trio discovery**: PM, designer, engineer discover together. The three perspectives (viability, usability, feasibility) catch different assumption risks. Solo PM discovery is an org anti-pattern.
4. **Story-based interviewing**: Don't ask "would you use this?" or "what do you think?" — both are fiction. Ask "tell me about the last time you..." — concrete past stories are the only reliable signal of real behavior.
5. **Test assumptions, not ideas**: Every solution embeds assumptions (desirability, viability, usability, feasibility, ethics). Discover the riskiest assumption first, then design the cheapest test for it.
