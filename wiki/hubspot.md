# HubSpot

**HubSpot** (~$30B market cap, 7,000+ employees) is the inbound marketing and CRM platform co-founded by Brian Halligan and Dharmesh Shah in 2006. It is unusually culture-forward for a public company of its scale: its operating identity is built around the **Culture Code** (a living 128-page document) and radical transparency defaults.

## Signature patterns

- **[[culture-code|Culture Code]]** — Versioned 128-page document treating culture as a product spec; authored by Dharmesh (who called himself the worst possible person for the job).
- **Radical transparency** — Default is "all information equally shared" except illegal-to-share material or other people's salaries. The N+1 insiders anecdote is cultural legend.
- **Zig vs. zag** — HubSpot's early multi-product bet zigged when conventional wisdom (focus on one thing) said zag.
- **Lean into strengths** — Dharmesh rejects management, has had zero direct reports in 18 years, refuses to become "passively okay" at a skill he doesn't enjoy.
- **No meetings before 11 AM** — Night-owl friendly by design.
- **LPM (laughs per minute)** — Public speaking decomposed into measurable subskill; custom SoloWare tracks it across Dharmesh's talks.
- **SoloWare** — Tools for exactly one user (yourself) to compound personal leverage.
