# An inside look at how CNN builds product | Upasna Gautam

## Executive summary

Upasna Gautam, a product manager at CNN Digital, reveals how product management works inside one of the world's largest news organizations. She explains how her team rebuilds CNN's content management system while onboarding journalists, navigating breaking news chaos, and maintaining four structured touchpoints with editorial staff: demo days, working sessions, breaking news dress rehearsals, and office hours. The episode uniquely bridges mindfulness practice with PM craft, arguing that equanimity — the ability to pause before reacting — is the most important skill for thriving in high-pressure product environments. Gautam also discusses the News Product Alliance, a growing movement to bring product thinking to newsrooms worldwide.

## Key ideas

1. **Equanimity as a PM superpower**: The ability to remain mentally calm and pause before reacting is the most important skill for product managers, especially in chaotic environments. Reactivity is the root cause of many workplace frustrations, and breaking that pattern through mindfulness gives an outsized advantage in stakeholder management, team morale, and translating user feedback.

2. **Four touchpoints for editorial collaboration**: CNN's product team maintains structured engagement with journalists through weekly demo days, collaborative working sessions (3-6 per onboarding cycle), breaking news dress rehearsals that stress-test the platform, and open office hours. This system builds trust and embeds users deeply into the product discovery process.

3. **Engineers as partners, not resources**: Bringing the tech lead and engineers into the product discovery process — including user testing sessions, design jams, and editorial conversations — dramatically improves technical feasibility decisions and team alignment. When engineers understand the 'why' at the same depth as PM and design, the entire process becomes more efficient.

4. **Building buffers for chaos**: In a breaking news environment, product teams must build systematic buffers into every plan — sometimes days, sometimes weeks. Quick decision-making and the ability to objectively assess situations in real time are essential when breaking news regularly disrupts research sessions and onboarding schedules.

5. **Product management in news is still in its infancy**: The News Product Alliance is working to bring product thinking to newsrooms globally, where journalists often take on PM responsibilities without training. The organization's mentorship network matched 150 mentees with 50 mentors, helping create product practices from scratch in resource-strapped newsrooms.

## Concepts covered

- **Equanimity** — Mental calmness and composure under stress; the ability to pause before reacting, cultivated through mindfulness and meditation practice.
- **Breaking News Dress Rehearsals** — Scripted simulations of breaking news scenarios used to stress-test CNN's content management platform at the speed of real events.
- **Mindful Communication** — Being deeply present in conversations, listening before responding, and conversing in the language of the listener rather than using technical jargon.
- **High Agency Under High Pressure** — The combination of skills enabling rapid, intuitive decision-making and pivoting in chaotic, time-sensitive product environments.
- **Product Management in News** — The emerging discipline of applying product thinking in newsrooms, which face unique constraints like breaking news interruptions and journalist-as-user dynamics.
