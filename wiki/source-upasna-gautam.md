# An inside look at how CNN builds product | Upasna Gautam

## Executive summary

Upasna Gautam, a PM at CNN Digital, describes what it takes to build internal product for a newsroom where a breaking story can instantly override any plan. Her core PM skill is equanimity — the ability to pause before reacting — which she treats as a learnable discipline rather than a personality trait. She also details CNN's four-touchpoint editorial engagement system (demo days, working sessions, breaking-news dress rehearsals, office hours) and embeds engineers as discovery partners so they become experts on the "why," not just the "how."

## Key ideas

1. **Equanimity as a PM superpower**: The ability to pause before reacting is the root skill for stakeholder management, morale, and translating user feedback. Reactivity is the root cause of most workplace woes.
2. **Four-touchpoint editorial engagement**: Weekly demo days, working sessions (collaborative user testing), breaking-news dress rehearsals, and office hours provide a structured yet flexible cadence with internal customers.
3. **Breaking-news dress rehearsals**: Scripted simulations of breaking-news scenarios stress-test the CMS at real-world speed — a unique form of QA and discovery.
4. **Engineers as embedded discovery partners**: Bringing tech leads into user research and editorial conversations makes them experts on the "why," not just the "how."
5. **Mindful communication**: Listening before speaking, conversing in the listener's language, and pausing before reacting matter more than articulate speech.

- **Breaking-news dress rehearsals** — Scripted stress-testing for event-driven systems.
- **Internal product management** — Building for employees rather than customers.
- **Mindful communication** — Listening before speaking; matching the listener's language.
- **Engineers as discovery partners** — Embedding technical staff in user research.
- **Buffers for chaos** — Structural slack that lets teams absorb unexpected demands.

Reframes PM craft for environments where customers' priorities override yours by the hour.
