# Source: Bending the universe in your favor | Claire Vo (LaunchDarkly, Color, Optimizely, ChatPRD)

**Guest**: Claire Vo

## Executive summary

Claire Vo, CPO at LaunchDarkly and creator of ChatPRD, offers a masterclass in career agency, organizational pace-setting, and the practical impact of AI on product management. Her core thesis is that the universe is bendable to your will — she demonstrates this through stories of proposing her own promotions, selling her startup to Optimizely while 34 weeks pregnant, and building ChatPRD on nights and weekends into a six-figure side product. On AI's impact on PMs, she draws a critical distinction between lowercase-c communication (information trading that AI will replace) and uppercase-C Communication (influence, boldness, charisma that remains human). She advocates for the CPTO role combining product and engineering under one leader, and shares a practical "one click faster" framework for accelerating organizational pace.

## Key ideas

1. **"One click faster" pace-setting framework**: If you think something needs to be done this year, it needs to be done this half. This half becomes this quarter, this quarter becomes this month, and so on. Combined with breaking the habit of snapping to meeting cadences for decisions, this creates compounding speed improvements across an organization.
2. **The CPTO role — combining product and engineering**: Running product, engineering, and design under one leader eliminates functional optimization debates in favor of organizational optimization. But it requires genuine technical depth — Claire reviews PRDs alongside GitHub, goes on pager duty, and handles engineering-specific talent challenges that pure product leaders never face.
3. **ChatPRD and the future of PM skills**: Built from a prompt Claire crafted to IC a technical PRD in a single meeting, ChatPRD now has thousands of users. It demonstrates that AI excels at scaffolding solved problems (data export specs) but cannot replace the bold, creative, future-seeing work of great PMs. The PM identity will shift from "my value comes from my ideas" to "am I building the right stuff quickly and delivering?"
4. **Lowercase-c vs uppercase-C Communication**: AI will replace the functional trading of information across teams (status updates, coordination, synthesis). But influential persuasion, organizational alignment, charisma, and boldness — the work of getting a system of humans to follow you — remains firmly human and becomes even more valuable.
5. **Career agency through organizational problem-solving**: Propose promotions by solving company problems, not requesting career growth. Claire got her first director role by drawing an org chart with herself at the top after the marketing lead left, and expanded into engineering at Color by identifying a scaling problem she knew she could fix. Frame every career move as "here's how this role is good for the company."

## Concepts & frameworks

- **Zone of genius** — The intersection of what you're uniquely good at and what gives you the most energy — Claire advocates ruthlessly focusing here and delegating everything else.
- **One click faster** — A pace-setting framework where you compress every timeline by one iteration unit: year to half, half to quarter, quarter to month, etc.
- **CPTO role** — The combined Chief Product and Technology Officer role that unifies product, engineering, and design under one leader for organizational optimization.
- **Lowercase-c vs uppercase-C Communication** — The distinction between routine information coordination (AI-replaceable) and influential persuasion and leadership alignment (uniquely human).
- **Personal SLA** — A leader's commitment to fast response times so they never become a bottleneck for their organization's decision-making velocity.
