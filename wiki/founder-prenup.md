# Founder Prenup

The **founder prenup** is Alisa Cohn's structured alignment artifact co-founders create at the start of a company — before disagreements emerge — to pre-negotiate the handful of questions that most frequently dissolve partnerships.

## Why it matters

Per Noam Wasserstein's research (cited repeatedly in the batch), **65% of startups fail due to co-founder conflict**. The conflict isn't usually about strategy — it's about values mismatch, unequal commitment, decision-making when you disagree, or unspoken expectations that only surface under stress.

## The five questions

Per [[source-alisa-cohn|Alisa Cohn]]'s version:

1. **Values** — What do we care about most? When values conflict, which wins?
2. **Vision of success** — What does a great outcome look like for each of us? (Lifestyle business? IPO? Acquihire?)
3. **Conflict style** — How does each of us handle conflict? Where do our styles clash?
4. **Disagreement resolution** — When we disagree and must decide, how do we decide? Who has the final call on what domain?
5. **Culture** — What kind of company are we trying to build? What behaviors are celebrated, tolerated, exiled?

## Paired practice: Co-founder vows

Rachel Lockett (see [[source-rachel-lockett]]) extends the founder prenup into ongoing practice: **co-founder vows** — quarterly structured recommitments, like marriages need date nights. A one-page plan serves as the alignment artifact.

## Paired practice: Personal Operating Manual

For non-founder team relationships, Cohn recommends a **Personal Operating Manual** covering:

- Communication style (sync vs. async, detail level, response time)
- Delegation preferences (what to flag up vs. decide alone)
- Gold-star behaviors (what earns your respect)
- Pet peeves (what erodes trust)
