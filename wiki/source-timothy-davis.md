# Source: The ultimate guide to paid growth | Timothy Davis (Shopify)

**Guest**: Timothy Davis ([[shopify]] head of performance marketing; ex-IBM/SoftLayer; consultant to Pinterest/LinkedIn/Redfin/Eventbrite)
**Published**: 2024-07-28
**Duration**: ~1h 42m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNJyb3R_Pnc

## Executive summary

"Paid is for everyone" — paid search especially. Tactical playbook: start with "signs of life" tests on platforms where users already show up, hire data-first people, obsessively operate weekly/bi-weekly/monthly op cadences, focus on signal not noise. Operator's manual for setting up and scaling performance marketing.

## Key ideas

1. **"Paid is for everyone," but start with paid search** — Paid search is user-driven (you only show when someone types a relevant keyword). Should be first channel even for organic-driven companies. Everything else (Meta, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn) is "disruptive media" needing creative fit.
2. **Signs of life tests + lookalike laddering** — Use CRM list to build 1%, 2-4%, 5-7%, 8%+ lookalikes on Meta. The 1% almost always works; higher tiers riskier but occasionally huge. "We're either winning or we're learning."
3. **Signal vs. noise is the real skill** — Hire criterion isn't Google Ads expertise (teachable) but data-thinking: "Who cares about impressions if the goal is conversions?" Hire data-first, then creative, then dedicated data scientist.
4. **Ops cadence spreadsheet** — Every account has a matrix of rocks × pebbles × frequency (weekly/biweekly/monthly). This is why agencies get "crushed in a month" — they just don't do the reps.
5. **Pre-PMF paid is a trap** — IBM burned money across Africa before realizing the problem was currency/operations fit, not interest. "All you're going to do is really annoy the users" who then hold one bad experience against you forever.
6. **Click share > impression share** — Impression share at top is "ego marketing"; click share = you're talking to the right users.
