# Source: How to hit revenue targets in a recession | Sahil Mansuri (Bravado)

**Guest**: Sahil Mansuri (CEO of Bravado, 300K+ member sales community; ex-Meltwater, ex-Glassdoor, sold to Fortune 500 including Facebook, Google, Amazon)
**Published**: 2022-12-04
**Duration**: ~1h 27m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pYZ0S7a72po

## Executive summary

Tactical recession playbook grounded in real-time data: 76% of SaaS companies missed Q3 2022 targets; 85%+ missed October. This signals a structural shift, not company-specific failure. Core prescription: stop chasing new logos (cold email response rates at historic lows, enterprise sales cycles extended from 62 to 115 days) and redirect your best salespeople to retention and expansion. Restructure comp plans to reward retention and multi-year contracts, not just new logo volume. Forecast with monthly milestones, not annual plans.

## Key ideas

1. **Quota miss rate is systemic** — 76% of companies missed Q3; 85%+ missed October. Plan conservatively with predetermined milestones rather than assuming recovery.
2. **Sales comp plans are broken** — Traditional 50/50 base-to-commission rewards new logo volume, ignoring churn. Rep A closes 15 deals (churns 10) gets celebrated; Rep B closes 12 (retains all, gets upsells) gets ignored. This is backwards in downturns.
3. **Redirect to retention and expansion** — New logo pipeline is drying up. Your best lever: keep existing customers happy, expand their spend, leverage them for warm introductions.
4. **Make best salespeople into CSMs** — In downturns, deploy top performers to ensure existing customers never leave. Sounds radical but makes economic sense when acquisition costs spike.
5. **Conservative planning with milestone-based unlocks** — Don't forecast a full year. Set conservative Q1 targets; hitting them unlocks additional budget and optimism. Missing signals need to adjust down immediately.
6. **Compensation redesign** — Reward retention kickback bonuses, multi-year contracts, and customer expansion deals. HubSpot and monday.com have moved to this model.
