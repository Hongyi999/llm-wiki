# Source: The hierarchy of engagement | Sarah Tavel (Benchmark, Greylock, Pinterest)

**Guest**: Sarah Tavel (Benchmark GP; ex-first-PM at Pinterest; ex-Greylock)
**Published**: 2023-12-27
**Duration**: ~1h 50m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H9g4pzcz6Tk

## Executive summary

Tavel — explicitly featured despite Lenny's "no VCs" rule because she thinks like a product person — walks through two frameworks developed while at Pinterest/Greylock to cut through vanity metrics. The Hierarchy of Engagement (core action → retained users → self-perpetuating product) is a consumer-social growth ladder; the Hierarchy of Marketplaces (focus → tip → dominate) is a marketplace-specific ladder.

## Key ideas

1. **Pick a core action, not MAUs** — Every consumer product has one action that proves the user "got it." Pinterest ran a regression over like/follow/click/pin/time-on-site and landed on "weekly active pinners/repinners." YouTube shifted to *subscribing* (Shishir Mehrotra told Sarah) because it benefits both creators and viewers.
2. **Level 2: accruing benefits + mounting loss** — Product should get better the more you use it, and you should have more to lose by leaving. Pinterest's "picked for you" algorithmic feed (possibly first algo home feed in social) is the archetype.
3. **Level 3: self-perpetuating via network effects + loops** — "Kinetic energy" of each user action should feed back. Clubhouse/Houseparty hit ceiling because only re-engagement loop was push notifications.
4. **TikTok spent >$1B on paid before becoming self-sustaining** — TikTok only invested massively in paid after solving levels 1 and 2 organically.
5. **Happy GMV + minimum viable happiness** — GMV is marketplace equivalent of MAUs. What matters is retention per transaction. Find a "thimble" (geography × category constraint, e.g., DoorDash → suburbs) and boil it.
6. **Tipping loops: growth loops + happiness loops** — Growth loops recruit both sides; happiness loops (search ranking, reputation) act like kidneys, churning out bad suppliers. Both run in parallel.
7. **Markets are currents, not bodies of water** — Don't chase TAM; chase momentum. Underestimated small markets (Airbnb, Etsy, Hipcamp) ideal because competition is sparse and market expands from within.
8. **Six marketplace tippability conditions** — Concentration on supply side is first killer. Need fragmented, hungry suppliers who can become "the new incumbents" (Bill Gurley).

## Concepts that deserve their own wiki page

- **Hierarchy of Engagement** — Core action → retention → self-perpetuating.
- **Hierarchy of Marketplaces** — Focus → tip → dominate.
- **Happy GMV & minimum viable happiness** — Marketplace retention framing.
- **Tipping loops** — Growth loops + happiness loops; distinct from Balfour's growth loops.
- **White-hot center / thimble focus** — Marketplace geographic concentration.
- **Accruing benefits + mounting loss** — The structural retention mechanism.
