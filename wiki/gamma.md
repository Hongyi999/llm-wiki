# Gamma

AI-powered presentation, document, and website design tool founded in 2020. In B1 Gamma is the case study for **product-led viral growth in the AI era** — going from an investor calling it "the dumbest idea I've ever heard" to **$100M ARR in just over two years** without ads.

## B1 role

[[source-grant-lee]] is the definitive episode. Grant (co-founder/CEO) walks through Gamma's inflection points:

- A middling Product Hunt launch that was mistaken for PMF.
- Four months of full-team focus on rebuilding the first-time user experience so AI-generated slides look stunning in 30 seconds.
- Organic signups jumped from hundreds to 20,000+/day **without advertising**.
- A micro-influencer engine (thousands of small creators, not celebrities) to amplify word-of-mouth.
- Heavy brand investment **before** performance marketing.

## Key themes

- **Word-of-mouth is the baseline**, ads are amplifiers.
- **Viral onboarding as product strategy** — when the first 30 seconds are magical, the growth problem inverts.
- **Micro-influencer + niche echo chambers** beat mega-influencers.
- **Founder-led marketing** compounds over 3–6 months.
