# Nubank

Latin American digital banking platform. Founded by David Velez. Canonical case study of extreme customer-fanaticism culture and fundamentally-different-not-incrementally-better positioning.

## Operating principles

- **"We want our customers to love us fanatically"** is the #1 value.
- **80-90% word-of-mouth acquisition** driven by emotional pull of a fundamentally different experience.
- **50% [[source-sean-ellis|Sean Ellis]] PMF threshold** as scaling gate (higher than the canonical 40%).
- **Bullseye cohort analysis** — Hunt for the segment where PMF is strongest and build toward them.
- **Amazon-style mock press release** for new products.
- **"Owners, not renters"** — PMs push back when something isn't ready.

## Canonical frames

- **Fundamentally different > incrementally better** — Only a fundamentally different experience creates emotional pull that drives word-of-mouth.
- **Strategy as concentrated, coherent bets** — [[source-richard-rumelt|Rumelt]]-derived. Concentration builds wealth; diversification preserves it.
- **Hypothesis-led, anecdote-trusted discovery** — "Pick up the phone and call 10 customers" beats formal research.
