# Source: Unorthodox frameworks for growing your product, career, and impact | Bangaly Kaba

**Guest**: Bangaly Kaba (ex-Facebook growth PM, Head of Growth at Instagram 440M → 1B users, VP Product at Instacart, now Director of PM at YouTube)
**Published**: 2024-05-26
**Duration**: ~1h 42m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ub9UkWByFIQ

## Executive summary

Two high-leverage frameworks: (1) a career-impact equation (impact = environment × skills, broken into six environment variables) and (2) a product-growth methodology built around "understand work" and the "adjacent user." Slowing down to really understand users and context produces dramatically higher velocity and win-rates later (Instagram was shipping 60-70% positive experiments per quarter).

## Key ideas

1. **Impact = Environment × Skills** — Score six environment variables (manager, resources, scope, team, compensation, culture) each 0-2 every year. Manager is highest-leverage. If score is poor, fix it or leave. "Compensation is an output, impact is the input."
2. **Understand work** — A Facebook-era framework (understand → identify → execute) reframed as a dedicated, scheduled line item on roadmaps (~20-40% of team bandwidth). Anti-pattern: "identify → justify → execute." When Kaba joined YouTube Shopping Live, first sprints were pure funnel instrumentation before any shipping.
3. **Adjacent user theory** — Your next 100M users aren't like your current ones. Cohort curves dipping over time isn't regression — it's adjacent users adopting and needing different things. Literally create a new account and use the product as them. Instacart found 90% of orders were repeats but reordering took 7-8 clicks.
4. **Managing complex change framework** — Vision + Skills + Incentives + Resources + Action Plan. Missing any produces a specific failure mode (confusion, anxiety, resistance, frustration, false starts).
5. **Instagram's growth was invites + celebrity partnerships + SEO, not just virality** — Celebrity partnerships seeded SEO via news media, amplified by Instagram embeds. The famous 2017 "connections pivot" deprioritized celebrity recommendations for new users in favor of friend-friend connections because new users were posting into echo chambers and churning.
6. **PM as team sport, not CEO of the product** — Coaching-tree metaphor: your legacy is who you grew. Applied Bloom's Taxonomy to diagnose where PMs are stuck.

## B7 angle

One of the most important growth episodes in the wiki. "Adjacent user" lens directly sharpens [[source-elena-verna|Verna's]] PLG/AI-growth framing by adding a persona-evolution dimension ignored in most retention work. Kaba's account-access-churn story (Instagram losing 10-12M users/year and fixing with a login omnibox) is a perfect counterpoint to [[source-rahul-vohra-superhuman|Vohra's]] PMF engine — same spirit (listen to churning users), different mechanism. Understand work is a counterweight to experimentation-maximalism.
