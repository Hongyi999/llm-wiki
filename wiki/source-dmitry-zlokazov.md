# Source: How Revolut trains world-class PMs — the Local CEO model ft. Dmitry Zlokazov

**Guest**: Dmitry Zlokazov (Global Head of Product, Revolut)
**Published**: 2025-05-15
**Duration**: ~1h 10m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-kPpd21bFrE

## Executive summary

Revolut's product org runs on "Local CEO" ownership: 150+ product owners manage cross-functional pods with hiring/firing power, genuine P&L accountability, and end-to-end responsibility. The company hires for raw intellect and hunger over experience, with many top performers being internal transfers (ex-engineers, ops managers) or former startup tech co-founders. Founders review 100% of shipped screens weekly. The "go deep on 7–10 out of 100 projects" approach creates a discipline signaling effect — teams self-organize to high standards knowing scrutiny rotates. Products must be "wow" before they ship; if something is 99% done, it's closer to 0% than 100%.

## Key ideas

1. **Local CEO model** — Product owners are not scrum-style POs but genuine local CEOs: cross-functional teams (eng, design, data, ops) report to them as line managers. Three specializations: UX POs (taste, consumer-facing), Technical POs (ex-engineers, deep systems), Data Science POs. 85–90% of skills are shared across all three types.
2. **Raw intellect + hunger > experience** — Experienced hires take longer to ramp, may rest on laurels, and have inflated expectations. Best profile: tech co-founder from a startup who has built things end-to-end. Source candidates by identifying apps/products the team loves, then targeting those companies.
3. **Go deep on 7–10 out of 100** — Rather than spreading thin across all projects, leadership goes code-reading deep on the most impactful 7–10. Signal effect: teams know scrutiny rotates, so all 100 teams self-organize to high standards. Contrasts with Chesky's approach of cutting scope; Revolut refuses to give up growth opportunities.
4. **Founder screen review at scale** — Founders review 100% of shipped screens weekly. Every product owner presents directly to CEO/CTO — no management layer filtering. Creates quality floor across 50+ countries and 150+ pods.
5. **99% done ≈ 0% done** — Cut features to the critical minimum, but never compromise on quality/UX/aesthetics. When something is 99% complete, the remaining 1% (edge cases, polish, details) represents the difference between a product people tolerate and one people love.
6. **Algorithmize after going deep** — Launch in one country, reverse-engineer the ideal process, formalize into an algorithmic process with quality gates, then scale to 50 countries. Zoom deep → zoom out → systematize.

## B10 angle

Revolut represents the founder-detail-obsession pattern at extreme scale (150+ pods, 50+ countries). The "99% ≈ 0%" philosophy echoes [[source-stewart-butterfield|Butterfield's]] divine discontent and S-curve thresholds. Raw-intellect hiring philosophy is the talent-side analog of [[source-jessica-livingston|Livingston's]] social radar for founder potential. Contrasts with Chesky's scope-cutting approach — Revolut keeps 100 things running but applies selective deep scrutiny.
