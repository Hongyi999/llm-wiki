# Source: The new AI growth playbook for 2026 | How Lovable hit $200M ARR in one year

## Executive summary

Elena Verna, head of growth at Lovable (which hit $200M ARR in under a year with 100 people), reveals why traditional growth playbooks are now 95% obsolete for AI companies. Core problem: product-market fit is no longer a destination—it's a treadmill you must recapture every 3 months as new model capabilities emerge. From November 2024 launch to $200M ARR in 8-10 months while maintaining 8M+ total users. Her secrets: building in public with founder/employee socials, giving product away aggressively, removing entry barriers, and constantly building new growth loops instead of optimizing old ones. She spends 95% of time on growth innovation vs 5% on optimization—the inverse of traditional SaaS.

## Key ideas

1. Product-market fit is no longer a one-time achievement; it's a recurring 3-month cycle. Every new model release (GPT-5, Claude 4, etc) creates a capability step-change that re-shifts what the market expects, forcing you to continuously reinvent your product and go-to-market.
2. Building in public + founder/employee socials + aggressive free-tier giveaways create exponential word-of-mouth. Lovable removes barriers: hackathons request free credits? 'Take it, how much do you need?' This seeded adoption at scale.
3. Only 30-40% of traditional growth tactics transfer to AI companies; 60-70% is invention. Lovable invests 95% in growth innovation and only 5% in optimization—you must constantly create new loops instead of optimizing funnels.
4. Consumer expectations for AI products change monthly, not yearly. What ChatGPT should do 8 months ago vs now is 'night and day.' Lovable must recapture adjacency constantly to avoid becoming a tool 'just for startups' or 'just for techies.'
5. Even a $200M ARR company at 10% month-over-month growth with 100 people is at risk of losing product-market fit in 3 months—OpenAI itself lost 6% market share in a week when Gemini 3 launched.

## Concepts & frameworks

- **Product-Market Fit Treadmill** — In AI, PMF must be recaptured every 3 months due to rapid model capability changes and shifting consumer expectations.
- **Building in Public** — Founder/employee socials + transparent shipping create word-of-mouth at scale without paid acquisition.
- **Aggressive Giveaway Strategy** — Remove entry barriers entirely: give unlimited free credits to hackathons, communities, and experiments to seed organic adoption.
- **Growth Innovation vs Optimization** — Spend 95% inventing new growth loops, 5% optimizing funnels—opposite of traditional SaaS where you optimize conversion
- **Pioneer-to-Majority Gap Risk** — AI companies risk alienating latent majority by hyper-focusing on pioneering use cases and bleeding-edge features; adjacent users may never catch up.
