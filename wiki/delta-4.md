# Delta 4 Framework

**Delta 4** is Kunal Shah's framework for predicting which products become durable, zero-CAC viral businesses. Published from his work founding Freecharge and CRED.

## The rule

Score the user's experience with the **incumbent solution** (1–10) and the user's experience with **your product** (1–10). If the delta is **≥4**, three things are true:

1. **Irreversibility** — Users won't go back. The status quo feels broken once they've tasted the delta.
2. **High tolerance for failure** — They'll forgive bugs, outages, awkward onboarding — because the gap is still so large.
3. **UBP — Unique Brag-worthy Proposition** — They tell others unprompted. Zero-CAC word-of-mouth engine.

## Canonical examples

| Before | After | Delta |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Hailing a cab | Uber | ≥4 |
| AltaVista | Google | ≥4 |
| Stack Overflow / googling | ChatGPT | ≥4 |
| Cable TV | Netflix (early) | ≥4 |

## Why the 4-point threshold

Below 4, users may prefer the new product but won't promote it or forgive failures. The incumbent's switching cost (habits, accounts, sunk learning) dominates. At 4+, the delta overwhelms switching cost and the product becomes *the story* — which is the only sustainable CAC engine in consumer software.

## Operator implication

If you're building and the honest delta is 2, you haven't found the product yet — you've found a feature. Increase the delta or kill it.
