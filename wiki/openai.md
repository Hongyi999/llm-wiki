# OpenAI

AI research lab and the most-referenced company across Lenny's Podcast AI episodes — cited in **10 of 29 B1 transcripts** and **11 of 32 B2 transcripts** (21 total). OpenAI sits at the intersection of foundation-model supply, consumer product distribution ([[chatgpt|ChatGPT]]), and the frontier-data market that pays experts to generate training signal.

## B1 appearances

- [[source-nick-turley]] — **Nick Turley** leads [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] product; tells the inside story of ChatGPT's growth from hackathon demo to 700M users.
- [[source-aishwarya-naresh-reganti-kiriti-badam]] — Aishwarya worked on **OpenAI enterprise deployments**, draws lessons from 50+ AI product launches.
- [[source-bret-taylor]] — Bret is **OpenAI's board chair** while running [[sierra-ai|Sierra]].
- [[source-brendan-foody]] — [[mercor|Mercor]] sells expert labelers to **OpenAI and peers** as the main customers.
- [[source-edwin-chen]] — Edwin's Surge AI supplies **OpenAI** with human preference data; discusses OpenAI's objective-function choices.
- [[source-hamel-husain-shreya-shankar]] — Hamel and Shreya benchmark evals against **OpenAI models** and their API behavior.
- [[source-garrett-lord]] — [[scale-ai|Scale AI]] and Handshake compete for the **OpenAI data contract**.
- [[source-asha-sharma]] — Microsoft AI's relationship with **OpenAI** as the model supplier.
- [[source-elena-verna-40]] — **OpenAI's** aggressive free-tier giveaways as growth case study.
- [[source-sander-schulhoff-20]] — **OpenAI models** as the test bed for prompt-injection and jailbreak research.

## Roles discussed in B1

- **Model supplier** to nearly every AI product in the batch.
- **Data customer** for expert-labeling startups ([[mercor]], [[scale-ai]], [[source-garrett-lord|Handshake]]).
- **Consumer hub** via [[chatgpt|ChatGPT]] — a distribution surface that creates [[source-brian-balfour|Balfour's]] "context and memory moat."
- **Board chair** position occupied by [[source-bret-taylor|Bret Taylor]].
