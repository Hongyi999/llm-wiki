# OpenAI

AI research lab and the most-referenced company across [[index-by-theme|B1 (AI products 2025+)]] — cited in **10 of 29 B1 transcripts**. OpenAI sits at the intersection of foundation-model supply, consumer product distribution ([[chatgpt|ChatGPT]]), and the frontier-data market that pays experts to generate training signal.
