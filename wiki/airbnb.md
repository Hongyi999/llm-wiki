# Airbnb

Home-sharing marketplace and travel platform founded by Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia, and Nathan Blecharczyk. Referenced across three B4 sources as a case study in founder-mode leadership, design-driven culture, and intergenerational collaboration. Chip Conley (Head of Hospitality) provides the deepest inside account of working alongside Brian Chesky.

## B4 appearances

- [[source-chip-conley|Chip Conley]] — Hired by Brian Chesky as Head of Hospitality at age 52 to work for a 20-something founder. Describes founder-mode management, intergenerational mentoring ("mutual mentorship"), and how Airbnb democratized hospitality through community trust.
- [[source-jason-shah|Jason Shah]] — References Airbnb as an exemplar of humility-driven leadership culture where even senior leaders maintain deep craft engagement.
- [[source-nickey-skarstad|Nickey Skarstad]] — Former Airbnb PM; describes the company's design-centric product culture, quality bar, and how Brian Chesky's vision translated into product decisions.
