# Source: Unpacking Amazon's unique ways of working | Bill Carr (Working Backwards)

**Guest**: Bill Carr (co-author *Working Backwards*; 15-year Amazon veteran)
**Published**: 2023-11-02
**Duration**: ~1h 33m
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9WHQa_AJQo

## Executive summary

Unpacks the operating practices Amazon developed in a narrow 2003-2007 window of process innovation. Bezos treated org design scientifically; customer-obsession was an article of faith; concrete mechanisms ([[working-backwards|PR/FAQ]], single-threaded leaders, input/output metrics, disagree-and-commit, Bar Raiser) were engineered to reinforce the leadership principles at scale.

## Key ideas

1. **Working backwards / [[working-backwards|PR/FAQ]]** — New products begin with a mock press release plus FAQ describing customer, problem, and solution before any code or resource is committed; the document iterates in concentric review circles.
2. **Single-threaded leadership** — Shift from project to program orientation: one leader owns a durable domain with dedicated cross-functional resources, trading some functional excellence for speed, ownership, and agility.
3. **Input vs. output metrics (avoid compound metrics)** — Amazon tried a weighted "fitness function" and scrapped it; compound metrics obfuscate which action drove which result.
4. **Have backbone; disagree and commit** — The hardest-to-use principle: obligated to voice dissent until heard and understood, then commit based on understanding the decision-maker's reasoning.
5. **Leaders are right a lot** — Good decisions come from judgment built on experience (often mistakes); leaders who are consistently wrong quickly lose their teams.

## B8 angle
