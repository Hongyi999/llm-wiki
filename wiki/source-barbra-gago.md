# Source: Category creation and brand building | Barbra Gago

**Guest**: Barbra Gago (founder/CEO of Pando; former CMO at Miro; VP Marketing at Greenhouse; head of marketing at Culture Amp)
**Published**: 2022-10-27
**Duration**: ~55m 48s
**YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gCEaUfZUuI0

## Executive summary

Most concrete treatment of category creation available: when to create a category, when to abandon, and how to execute. Contrasts Miro success (inventing "visual collaboration") with Greenhouse failure (trying and abandoning "recruiting optimization platform" because customers and budgets stayed stuck on "ATS"). Covers rebranding mechanics (RealtimeBoard → Miro) and opinionated software.

## Key ideas

1. **Category validation has three inputs** — Budget (is there a line item?), language (how customers describe their pain), competition (a category doesn't exist until multiple companies claim it — competition validates, not threatens).
2. **Dual-track category strategy** — Sell into an existing category where budget lives (ATS, performance management) while building thought leadership for the new category (employee progression, visual collaboration); bottom-up shift, not a sudden switch.
3. **Category creation tactics** — Customer listening for language, PR, analyst relationships (Gartner/Forrester/G2), thought leadership to educate buyers.
4. **Rebrand as product development** — Sprint teams, owners per workstream, keep essence (RealtimeBoard's yellow Post-it became Miro's yellow M), bring customers along. Done in ~5 months start to South by Southwest launch.
5. **Brand = values made visible** — Miro's name letters each embodied a value (agility = wiggly letter); values become the DNA that makes every touchpoint on-brand without training.
6. **Opinionated software** — Enforce best practices in the product (Atlassian/agile, Greenhouse/structured recruiting, Pando enforcing leveling transparency) when industry status quo is broken.
